# ～恐れがある {#恐れがある}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～恐れがある

**Furigana form:** ～おそれがある

**Meaning:** "There is a fear/risk/danger that ~" — used to express concern that a negative or undesirable event may occur, typically in formal announcements, news reports, and weather warnings.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形 (verb dictionary form) + 恐れがある — 例: 崩れる恐れがある (there is a risk of collapsing)
- 名詞 + の + 恐れがある — 例: 津波の恐れがある (there is a risk of a tsunami)
- な形容詞 + な + 恐れがある
- い形容詞 + 恐れがある

**Typical context:** Formal, written — very common in news broadcasts, weather forecasts, official warnings, and written reports. Rarely used in casual daily conversation; sounds stiff if used about trivial personal matters.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **かもしれない** — "might happen," much more neutral and casual; works for both good and bad outcomes.
- **おそれがある vs 危険性がある** — 危険性がある is more generic "there is a danger/risk," often used in technical/safety contexts; 恐れがある leans toward natural disasters and official warnings.
- **ないとも限らない** — "it's not necessarily impossible that," a softer, more indirect way of hedging about a negative possibility.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though 望みがある ("there is hope/promise that ~ good thing will happen") contrasts in sentiment.

**Example sentences:**
1. 今夜、大雨で川が氾濫する恐れがあります。
   (Tonight, there is a risk that the river will flood due to heavy rain.)
2. この薬は副作用が出る恐れがあるので、注意してください。
   (This medicine may cause side effects, so please be careful.)
3. 台風の影響で、明日は交通機関が乱れる恐れがある。
   (Due to the typhoon, there is a risk that transportation will be disrupted tomorrow.)
4. その崖は崩れる恐れがあるため、立ち入り禁止になっている。
   (That cliff is off-limits because there is a risk it could collapse.)
5. このままでは資金が不足する恐れがある。
   (At this rate, there is a risk that funds will run short.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners often use 恐れがある for everyday minor possibilities (e.g., "I might be late"), but this grammar is reserved for serious, often large-scale negative outcomes — 遅れるかもしれない is more natural for small personal risks.
