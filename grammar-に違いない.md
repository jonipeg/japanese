# ～に違いない {#に違いない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に違いない

**Furigana form:** ～にちがいない

**Meaning:** "Must be / surely is" — expresses the speaker's strong, confident conjecture based on available evidence or reasoning, almost certain though not directly confirmed.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に違いない (例: 犯人は彼に違いない)
- な形容詞 (plain) + に違いない (例: 彼は暇に違いない)
- い形容詞 (plain) + に違いない (例: この店は美味しいに違いない)
- 動詞 (plain form, any tense) + に違いない (例: 彼は知っているに違いない / 彼は行ったに違いない)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, used in both writing and speech; common in written deduction/narration (novels, news analysis) as well as confident spoken assertions.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **はずだ** — "should be/is expected to be," based on logical expectation rather than personal conviction; slightly less emotionally assertive.
- [**に相違ない**](grammar-に相違ない.md) — nearly identical meaning, more formal/written, common in legal or official text.
- **かもしれない** — "might be," much weaker certainty, a clear contrast in confidence level.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～かもしれない ("might be," weak conjecture) contrasts in certainty level; no direct grammatical opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. あの慌てぶりを見ると、何かあったに違いない。
   (Judging by how flustered he is, something must have happened.)
2. この字の美しさからして、彼女は書道を習っていたに違いない。
   (Given how beautiful this handwriting is, she must have studied calligraphy.)
3. 彼がこんなに疲れているのだから、仕事が大変だったに違いない。
   (Since he's this tired, work must have been tough.)
4. この味、お母さんの手作りに違いない。
   (This taste — it must be homemade by Mom.)
5. 毎日遅くまで残業しているのだから、彼は責任感が強いに違いない。
   (Since he works overtime late every day, he must have a strong sense of responsibility.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に違いない expresses a subjective but very confident conclusion — stronger than はず and much stronger than かもしれない. It should not be used for simple factual statements the speaker already knows to be true; it is reserved for inference from evidence. Also note it cannot attach directly to polite forms (not ですに違いない).
