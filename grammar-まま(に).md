# ～まま(に) {#まま(に)}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**1. Kanji version:** ～儘に (rare; almost always written in hiragana)

**2. Furigana version:** ～ままに

**3. Meaning:** As it is / passively following along with something (a circumstance, another person's words, one's own feelings) without exercising one's own initiative.

**4. Conjugation (connection form):** 動詞辞書形／た形 + ままに; also 受身形 + るままに (e.g. 言われるままに)

**5. Usage context:** Slightly literary/written register, though the set phrase 言われるままに also appears in spoken Japanese. Neutral politeness level.

**6. Similar grammar structures:**
- つもりで — expresses the subject's own deliberate intention or pretense, the opposite orientation from まま(に)'s passive "going along with." Confirmed as a real student confusion (Q5, student wrongly picked つもり).
- 次第で — "depending on X," a conditional/causal relationship, different from まま(に)'s "following along as-is" sense.
- なすがまま — a related fixed idiom meaning "letting things happen to you," close in spirit to まま(に) but used as a fixed expression rather than a productive grammar pattern.

**7. Opposite grammar structures:** No direct grammatical opposite; conceptually 自分の意志で (of one's own will) contrasts with the passivity of まま(に).

**8. Example sentences:**
1. 思うままに、意見を述べてください。(Please state your opinion freely, just as you feel it.)
2. モデルたちはカメラマンに言われるままに次々とポーズをとった。(The models took poses one after another, just as the photographer told them to.)
3. 彼は親に勧められるままに、医学部に進学した。(He went to medical school, simply following his parents' recommendation.)
4. 昔住んでいた家は、荒れ果てたまま放置されていた。(The house I used to live in had been left abandoned, just as it was.)
5. 子供のころの夢のままに、彼女は今も絵を描き続けている。(Just as in her childhood dream, she still continues to paint even now.)

**9. Common mistakes / key nuances:** Confirmed real mistake: a student confused まま(に) with つもり. The key distinction is agency — まま(に) describes passively following along with an external force, instruction, or feeling without resistance, while つもり expresses the subject's own deliberate intention or plan.
