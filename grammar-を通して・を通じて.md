# ～を通して／を通じて {#を通して-を通じて}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を通して／～を通じて

**Furigana form:** ～をとおして／～をつうじて

**Meaning:** "Through, by means of (over a period or via a medium)" — indicates a channel, medium, or duration through which something happens or is accomplished. を通じて also carries a secondary meaning of "throughout (a period of time)."

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + を通して/を通じて: 友人を通して紹介された (introduced through a friend); 一年を通じて (throughout the year)
- Both forms are largely interchangeable when referring to means/medium; を通じて is preferred for continuous time spans (一年を通じて, 生涯を通じて)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, both spoken and written. Common in describing how information, connections, or experiences are conveyed, and in describing something that holds true across an entire duration.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**によって**](grammar-によって・により.md) — "by means of," more about method/cause/agent, less about ongoing channel or duration.
- [**を経て**](grammar-を経て.md) — "via/after passing through (a stage)," emphasizes sequential stages rather than medium.
- **の間(中)** — simple "during," lacks the "throughout the entire period" emphasis of を通じて.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼とは共通の友人を通して知り合った。
   (I got to know him through a mutual friend.)
2. このニュースはSNSを通じて瞬く間に広まった。
   (This news spread in an instant through social media.)
3. 一年を通じて、この地域は温暖な気候だ。
   (Throughout the year, this region has a mild climate.)
4. 留学を通して、多くのことを学んだ。
   (Through studying abroad, I learned a great many things.)
5. 彼女は長い闘病生活を通じて、強さを身につけた。
   (Through her long battle with illness, she developed inner strength.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** While を通して and を通じて are largely interchangeable for "via a medium/channel," を通じて is strongly preferred when describing a continuous span of time ("throughout"), whereas を通して more often emphasizes a specific means or experience. Also don't confuse either with によって, which marks direct agency/method/cause rather than an ongoing channel.
