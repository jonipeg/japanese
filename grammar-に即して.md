# ～に即して {#に即して}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～に即して

**Furigana form:** ～にそくして

**Meaning:** "In accordance with / based on X" — to handle, judge, or plan something in a way that precisely matches or conforms to a given standard, rule, or actual real-world condition.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に即して／に即した＋名詞 (base verb: 即す)
- 規定に即して — "in accordance with the regulations"

**Typical context:** Formal, written register — common in business, administrative, and legal contexts where plans or decisions must match regulations or real conditions.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**に基づいて**](grammar-に基づいて.md) — "based on," broader and more general; usable with abstract sources (data, theory, law).
- [**に沿って**](grammar-に沿って.md) — "along the lines of," used especially for plans, policies, or guidelines.
- [**を踏まえて**](grammar-を踏まえて.md) — "taking X into account," emphasizes using X as a premise rather than conforming exactly to it.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～に反して ("contrary to X") expresses acting against a standard rather than conforming to it.

**Example sentences:**
1. 規定に即して出張費が出ます。
   (Travel expenses are paid in accordance with the regulations.)
2. 会社の現状に即した経営計画を考える。
   (We devise a management plan that matches the company's actual current situation.)
3. 時代の変化に即して、教育方法も見直す必要がある。
   (Educational methods also need to be reviewed in line with the changing times.)
4. 現実に即して対策を立てるべきだ。
   (We should formulate countermeasures that match reality.)
5. 事実に即して報告書を作成してください。
   (Please prepare the report in accordance with the facts.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に即して stresses precise conformity to a concrete standard or actual situation (規定, 現状, 事実, 時代), unlike に基づいて, which can refer to a more abstract basis or source. It often appears with words denoting rules, conditions, or circumstances, and is rarely used for personal opinions or feelings.
