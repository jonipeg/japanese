# ～に至るまで {#に至るまで}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～に至るまで

**Furigana form:** ～にいたるまで

**Meaning:** "Even down to X" / "all the way to X" — emphasizes that the range or scope of something extends even to an unexpected, extreme, or surprising example, underscoring just how broad that range is.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に至るまで
- 髪型からスカートの丈に至るまで厳しく指導される — "we're strictly instructed on everything from hairstyle down to skirt length"

**Typical context:** Written, somewhat formal register — used in descriptive writing to emphasize the thoroughness, extremity, or surprising breadth of a scope (school rules, trip preparations, hobbies, etc.).

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **～から～まで** — plain "from X to Y" range, without the added nuance of surprise or extremity.
- **～はもちろん～も** — "not only X but even Y," similarly builds toward an additional, extending example; often paired together with に至るまで in the same sentence.
- [**～を問わず**](grammar-を問わず.md) — "regardless of X," expresses non-discrimination across a category rather than extension along a scale or range.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a phrase that narrows scope, like ～に限って ("only in the case of X"), would restrict rather than extend the range.

**Example sentences:**
1. わたしの学校は服装に厳しい。制服の着方はもちろん、ヘアスタイルやスカートの長さに至るまで注意される。
   (My school is strict about appearance. Not just how we wear the uniform, but even our hairstyles and skirt length are checked.)
2. 今度の旅行のスケジュール表は綿密だ。起床時間から飛行機内の食事開始時間に至るまで書いてある。
   (The itinerary for this trip is meticulous — it even lists everything from wake-up times down to when meals start on the plane.)
3. 父の趣味は料理です。食材も自家製でないと気が済まないらしく、みそ、豆腐に至るまで自分で作ります。
   (My father's hobby is cooking. He apparently can't be satisfied unless the ingredients are homemade too — he even makes his own miso and tofu.)
4. 彼のコレクションは古銭から切手に至るまで多岐にわたる。
   (His collection is wide-ranging, extending from old coins all the way to stamps.)
5. そのレストランは接客態度から皿の並べ方に至るまで細かく決められている。
   (At that restaurant, everything down to the staff's manner and even how the dishes are arranged is precisely prescribed.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Use に至るまで for the extreme or unexpected endpoint of a range, not for an ordinary, expected item. It's common to set up the expected example first with ～はもちろん, or a different kind of starting point with ～から, before landing on the surprising example marked by に至るまで.
