# ～をはじめ {#をはじめ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を始め

**Furigana form:** ～をはじめ

**Meaning:** "Starting with X, such as X (and others)" — introduces a representative example of a larger group or category, implying there are other similar members not explicitly listed.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + をはじめ(として/とする): 校長先生をはじめ、多くの先生方が出席した (starting with the principal, many teachers attended)
- Modifying form: 〜をはじめとする + 名詞: 日本をはじめとするアジア諸国 (Asian countries, starting with/such as Japan)

**Typical context:** Formal, written-and-spoken register. Common in speeches, formal introductions, news reports, and descriptions of representative members within a larger group.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**など**](grammar-など・なんて・なんか.md) — simple "etc./and so on," much more casual and lacks the "most representative/prominent example" nuance.
- [**はもちろん**](grammar-はもちろん.md) — "needless to say X, (and also Y)," emphasizes the obviousness of the first item rather than its representativeness.
- **を筆頭に** — nearly synonymous, "topped by X," slightly more formal/literary, often used for rankings or hierarchies.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. この祭りには市長をはじめ、多くの来賓が参加した。
   (Many dignitaries, starting with the mayor, attended this festival.)
2. 日本をはじめとするアジア諸国で、この製品は人気がある。
   (This product is popular in Asian countries, starting with Japan.)
3. 社長をはじめ社員一同、心よりお祝い申し上げます。
   (All of us, starting with the president, offer our heartfelt congratulations.)
4. このチームは田中選手をはじめ、優秀な選手が揃っている。
   (This team is stacked with talented players, starting with Tanaka.)
5. 富士山をはじめとする日本の名所を巡る旅に出た。
   (We went on a trip visiting famous Japanese sites, starting with Mt. Fuji.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** をはじめ signals that the named item is the most prominent/representative example of a group, not necessarily literally "first" in sequence — learners sometimes mistake it for a literal "starting point in time." It's also more formal than など, so substituting など in a ceremonial or formal context can feel too casual.
