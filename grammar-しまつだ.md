# ～しまつだ {#しまつだ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～始末だ

**Furigana form:** ～しまつだ

**Meaning:** "Ended up in the (bad) state of X" — describes the regrettable final outcome of a chain of escalating bad events or behavior, often with exasperation.

**Conjugation:**
- V辞書形 + 始末だ (typically after a list of escalating bad events, often preceded by こんな/あんな)

**Typical context:** Formal-leaning written/spoken register, used when recounting a series of mistakes or problems with a tone of exasperation or regret.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**あげく(に)**](grammar-あげく(に).md) — a near-synonym meaning "as a result of [much V-ing]"; あげく focuses on the drawn-out process leading up to the bad result, while しまつだ focuses on naming the final sorry state itself.
- [**のきらいがある**](grammar-のきらいがある.md) — describes an ongoing character tendency, not a single culminating bad result like しまつだ.
- [**それまでだ**](grammar-それまでだ.md) — expresses finality/futility regardless of effort ("if X happens, it's all over"), different from しまつだ's description of a specific accumulated bad outcome.
- [**といったところだ**](grammar-といったところだ.md) — a neutral, modest approximation of degree, lacking しまつだ's negative, regretful conclusory tone.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. きょうは最悪の日だった。朝は社長に叱られ、午後はコーヒーをこぼしてスーツを汚し、最後は帰りの電車にかばんを忘れる始末だった。
   (Today was the worst day. I got scolded by the president in the morning, spilled coffee and stained my suit in the afternoon, and finally ended up forgetting my bag on the train home.)
2. 彼は約束を何度も破り、とうとう誰からも信用されなくなる始末だ。
   (He broke promises so many times that he ended up being trusted by no one at all.)
3. ダイエットのつもりが、ストレスで余計に食べてしまう始末だ。
   (I meant to diet, but ended up eating even more out of stress.)
4. 何度も注意したのに、また同じミスを繰り返す始末だ。
   (Despite being warned many times, he ended up repeating the same mistake again.)
5. 計画が次々と狂い、最後は旅行自体を中止する始末だった。
   (The plans kept falling apart one after another, and in the end we ended up canceling the trip altogether.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** しまつだ belongs to the N1 extreme-degree/outcome confusable cluster and is often mixed up with あげく(に) because both describe a bad result after a process. The distinguishing point: あげく focuses on and names the preceding process ("after much V-ing, ..."), while しまつだ is purely about stating the regrettable end state itself, often following a list of escalating incidents rather than one single preceding clause.
