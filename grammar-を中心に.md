# ～を中心に {#を中心に}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を中心に

**Furigana form:** ～をちゅうしんに

**Meaning:** "Centering on, focused around" — indicates that something is the central focus, hub, or main point around which other things are organized or revolve.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + を中心に(して/として): 東京を中心に活動している (active centering around Tokyo)
- Often used to describe geographic spread, group membership, or thematic focus: 若者を中心に人気がある (popular mainly/centering among young people)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, both spoken and written. Common in news reports, descriptions of organizations, geographic/demographic spread, and discussions of main themes or key members.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **を中心として** — more formal variant with identical meaning, common in written/academic contexts.
- **が中心となって** — "with X serving as the center," emphasizes active leadership role rather than simple focus.
- **をメインに** — casual loanword equivalent, used in informal speech.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though ～を除いて ("excluding X") contrasts by marking exclusion rather than central focus.

**Example sentences:**
1. この台風は関東地方を中心に大きな被害をもたらした。
   (This typhoon caused major damage, centering on the Kanto region.)
2. 若い世代を中心に、このアプリの人気が広がっている。
   (This app's popularity is spreading, mainly centering among the younger generation.)
3. 彼女を中心に、新しいプロジェクトチームが結成された。
   (A new project team was formed with her at the center.)
4. この店は和食を中心にしたメニューを提供している。
   (This restaurant offers a menu centered on Japanese cuisine.)
5. 会議は来期の予算を中心に進められた。
   (The meeting proceeded with next term's budget as the central topic.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** を中心に describes the primary focus while still allowing for peripheral elements (it doesn't mean "exclusively") — learners sometimes mistakenly treat it as equivalent to だけ/のみ (only). Note the distinction between を中心に (centering on, as an adverbial phrase) and が中心だ (X is the center, as a predicate stating a fact).
