# ～に先立って {#に先立って}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に先立って

**Furigana form:** ～にさきだって

**Meaning:** "Prior to X / in advance of X" — indicates that an action is carried out before a more important or larger event, often as preparation for it.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に先立って — 開会式に先立って (prior to the opening ceremony)
- 動詞辞書形 + に先立って — 出発するに先立って (prior to departing)
- Noun-modifying form: 名詞 + に先立つ + 名詞 — 公開に先立つ試写会 (a preview screening prior to the release)

**Typical context:** Formal, written — news reports, ceremonies, official announcements, academic/business contexts.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **の前に** — plain, general "before X," usable in any register; に先立って is reserved for formal, significant events.
- **に先んじて** — similar meaning "ahead of X," slightly more literary, often implies being first/pioneering.
- [**をひかえて**](grammar-をひかえて.md) — "with X coming up / in anticipation of X," emphasizes the approaching event rather than sequencing an action before it.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～に続いて ("following X") contrasts as "after X" rather than "before X."

**Example sentences:**
1. 試合に先立って、両チームの選手が挨拶を交わした。
   (Prior to the match, players from both teams exchanged greetings.)
2. 新製品の発売に先立って、記者発表会が開かれた。
   (Prior to the new product's release, a press conference was held.)
3. 海外出張に先立ち、必要な書類を準備した。
   (Prior to the overseas business trip, I prepared the necessary documents.)
4. 開会式に先立って、関係者によるテープカットが行われた。
   (Prior to the opening ceremony, a ribbon-cutting by officials was held.)
5. 工事の開始に先立って、住民への説明会が行われた。
   (Prior to the start of construction, an explanatory meeting for residents was held.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に先立って is reserved for formal, often ceremonial or officially significant events — using it for trivial everyday sequencing (e.g., "before I eat breakfast") sounds unnaturally stiff; の前に is correct there instead.
