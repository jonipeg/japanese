# ～を禁じえない {#を禁じえない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を禁じ得ない

**Furigana form:** ～をきんじえない

**Meaning:** "Cannot help but feel X" — a strong emotion (sympathy, anger, tears, surprise) wells up involuntarily and cannot be suppressed, usually in reaction to something moving or tragic.

**Conjugation:**
- N(感情を表す名詞) + を禁じ得ない
- Attaches to emotion nouns: 同情, 涙, 怒り, 驚き, etc.

**Typical context:** Very formal, written, literary register — common in news commentary, essays, and formal reflections on tragic or moving events. Not used in casual speech.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**てやまない**](grammar-てやまない.md) — a sustained, continuous wish/feeling expressed via a verb, in contrast to を禁じえない's sudden, involuntary single-moment emotional reaction expressed via a noun.
- [**のきわみ**](grammar-のきわみ.md) — describes reaching a peak emotional state as a description of the situation, rather than the speaker's inability to suppress a reaction.
- [**のいたり**](grammar-のいたり.md) — a humble, self-referential extreme feeling (often about the speaker's own honor/embarrassment), distinct from を禁じえない's focus on an uncontrollable reaction to external events.
- [**かぎりだ**](grammar-かぎりだ.md) — an adjective-based personal feeling exclamation (うらやましい限りだ), lighter in tone than を禁じえない's formal "cannot suppress" framing.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 大地震で両親を失ってしまった子どもが多い。同情を禁じえない話である。
   (Many children lost their parents in the big earthquake. It's a story that makes you unable to help feeling sympathy.)
2. 彼の身勝手な言動には怒りを禁じえなかった。
   (I couldn't help feeling anger at his selfish words and actions.)
3. その感動的な再会の場面には涙を禁じえなかった。
   (I couldn't hold back tears at that moving reunion scene.)
4. 突然の発表に驚きを禁じえない関係者も多かった。
   (Many people involved couldn't help being shocked by the sudden announcement.)
5. 被災地の惨状を目にし、やりきれない思いを禁じえなかった。
   (Seeing the disaster area's devastation, I couldn't suppress a feeling of helplessness.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** を禁じえない is a core member of the N1 "extreme degree / can't help but" cluster and is most often confused with てやまない on MC tests. The key distinguishing point: を禁じえない describes a sudden, momentary, involuntary reaction to a specific event (always noun + を禁じえない), while てやまない describes a continuous, ongoing wish or feeling (always verb-て + やまない). If the sentence describes a one-time emotional reaction to news/an event, choose を禁じえない; if it describes an enduring hope or sentiment, choose てやまない.
