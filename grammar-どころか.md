# ～どころか {#どころか}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～どころか

**Furigana form:** ～どころか (no special kanji readings needed)

**Meaning:** "Far from X," "let alone X" — strongly denies a modest premise and asserts a much more extreme (often opposite) reality, emphasizing the gap between expectation and truth.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞／普通形 + どころか + (contrasting/extreme statement)
- 合格するどころか、試験を受けることさえできなかった。(Far from passing, I couldn't even take the exam.)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, spoken and written; used for emphatic contrast, often conveying surprise, complaint, or correction of an assumption.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**はおろか**](grammar-はおろか.md) — near-synonym, more literary/written, equally emphatic "let alone."
- [**ばかりか**](grammar-ばかりか.md) — "not only X but also," adds an extra fact rather than negating an initial premise.
- **むしろ** — "rather," signals a milder contrast/correction, lacking どころか's emphatic denial.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼はお礼を言うどころか、謝りもしなかった。
   (Far from saying thank you, he didn't even apologize.)
2. この問題は簡単どころか、専門家でも解けないほど難しい。
   (Far from being easy, this problem is so difficult that even experts can't solve it.)
3. ダイエットを始めたが、痩せるどころか太ってしまった。
   (I started a diet, but far from losing weight, I actually gained weight.)
4. 彼女は怒るどころか、笑って許してくれた。
   (Far from getting angry, she laughed and forgave me.)
5. 新人のくせに、仕事を覚えるどころか、やる気さえ見せない。
   (Despite being a newcomer, far from learning the job, he doesn't even show motivation.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** どころか requires a sharp contrast or reversal between the stated premise and reality — it's stronger than simple negation. Learners sometimes use it for mild contrasts where むしろ or かえって would be more natural; reserve どころか for genuinely surprising or extreme gaps.
