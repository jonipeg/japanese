# ～にあたり／あたって {#にあたり・あたって}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に当たり／に当たって

**Furigana form:** ～にあたり／にあたって (当 read あ(たる))

**Meaning:** "At the time of," "on the occasion of," "in undertaking X" — marks a significant, often formal, event or juncture as the moment for the following action (commonly used for beginnings, important tasks, or ceremonies).

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + にあたり／にあたって
- 動詞辞書形 + にあたり／にあたって
- 新年を迎えるにあたり、ご挨拶申し上げます。(On the occasion of welcoming the new year, I'd like to offer my greetings.)

**Typical context:** Formal, often written — speeches, official announcements, ceremonies, business correspondence. Rare in casual conversation.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**に際して**](grammar-際に・に際して.md) — nearly synonymous, equally formal, slightly more common in written announcements.
- [**を機に**](grammar-を機に.md) — "taking X as an opportunity," emphasizes a catalyst for change rather than simply marking a formal moment.
- [**際に**](grammar-際に・に際して.md) — a more general, slightly less formal "on the occasion of/when."

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 新しいプロジェクトを始めるにあたり、皆様のご協力をお願いいたします。
   (As we begin this new project, I ask for everyone's cooperation.)
2. この契約を結ぶにあたって、いくつか確認したいことがあります。
   (In concluding this contract, there are a few things I'd like to confirm.)
3. 入社にあたり、研修を受けていただきます。
   (Upon joining the company, you will undergo training.)
4. 式典の開催にあたって、来賓の方々にご挨拶いたします。
   (On the occasion of holding the ceremony, I will greet our distinguished guests.)
5. 新生活を始めるにあたり、必要なものをリストにまとめた。
   (In starting a new life, I made a list of necessary items.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にあたり／にあたって is reserved for formal, often one-time or significant occasions — using it for trivial everyday actions (like "when I eat breakfast") sounds unnatural; use とき instead for mundane situations. Learners sometimes confuse this with を機に, which specifically frames the event as a catalyst for a resulting change, whereas にあたって simply marks the formal timing.
