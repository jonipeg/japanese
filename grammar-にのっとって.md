# ～にのっとって {#にのっとって}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**1. Kanji version:** ～に則って

**2. Furigana version:** ～にのっとって

**3. Meaning:** In accordance with / based on (rules, precedent, tradition, or an established spirit/code).

**4. Conjugation (connection form):** 名詞 + にのっとって

**5. Usage context:** Formal, written register. Common in ceremonial, legal, traditional, or sports-related contexts (rules of conduct, religious rites, codes of honor).

**6. Similar grammar structures:**
- に基づいて — "based on X," more general/neutral and usable in everyday contexts, lacking にのっとって's formal/ceremonial connotation.
- に沿って — "along the lines of X," used more for plans or guidelines rather than formal codes or traditions.
- に従って — "in accordance with/following X," the broadest synonym; can describe following orders or instructions generally, not just adherence to an established formal code like にのっとって implies.

**7. Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; に反して (contrary to X) is the closest conceptual opposite.

**8. Example sentences:**
1. 我々はスポーツマン精神にのっとって、正々堂々と戦うことを誓います。(We vow to fight fair and square, in accordance with the spirit of sportsmanship.)
2. この儀式は古くからの習わしにのっとって行われている。(This ceremony is carried out in accordance with age-old customs.)
3. 会議は正式な手続きにのっとって進められた。(The meeting proceeded in accordance with formal procedure.)
4. 彼は会社の規定にのっとって、適切に処理を行った。(He handled it properly, in accordance with the company's regulations.)
5. その裁判は法律にのっとって公正に行われた。(The trial was conducted fairly, in accordance with the law.)

**9. Common mistakes / key nuances:** Students often treat にのっとって as fully interchangeable with に基づいて or に従って. The key nuance is that にのっとって specifically implies adherence to an established, often formal or traditional rule, precedent, or spirit (e.g. sportsmanship, ritual, law), rather than just any general basis for an action.
