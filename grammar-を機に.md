# ～を機に {#を機に}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を機に

**Furigana form:** ～をきっかけに (read きに in 機に itself: ～をきに)

**Meaning:** "Taking X as an opportunity/trigger" — marks an event as the turning point or catalyst that prompted a subsequent decision or change, often a significant life event.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (noun) + を機に
- Example from source: 出産を機に (taking the birth [of a child] as an opportunity)

**Typical context:** Formal, written and spoken — common in both formal writing (announcements, biographies) and polite spoken Japanese (e.g., explaining a life decision at work or in an interview). Neutral-to-formal politeness; not rude, often used in slightly ceremonious or reflective contexts (retirements, marriages, births, anniversaries).

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **を契機に** — nearly synonymous "taking X as a trigger"; slightly more formal/written, common in news and official writing, while を機に is usable in both speech and writing.
- **をきっかけに** — the colloquial equivalent, "taking X as a chance/opportunity"; more casual and commonly used in everyday conversation, whereas を機に feels a bit more formal.
- [**に際して**](grammar-際に・に際して.md) — "on the occasion of X," used for formal events (ceremonies, important occasions); lacks the "catalyst for change" nuance that を機に carries.
- **を境に (さかいに)** — "with X as the turning point/boundary," emphasizing a before/after division rather than X being the motivating opportunity for a decision.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though ～にもかかわらず ("despite X") contrasts in spirit by describing an event that failed to trigger a change.

**Example sentences:**
1. 二人目の出産を機に、退職することにしました。
   (Taking the birth of my second child as an opportunity, I decided to retire.)
2. 彼は病気を機に、たばこをやめることにした。
   (Taking his illness as an opportunity, he decided to quit smoking.)
3. 大学卒業を機に、一人暮らしを始めた。
   (Taking my university graduation as an opportunity, I started living alone.)
4. 引っ越しを機に、不要な物を全部処分した。
   (Taking the move as an opportunity, I got rid of everything I didn't need.)
5. 結婚を機に、実家を離れて新しい土地に住むことにした。
   (Taking marriage as an opportunity, we decided to leave our hometown and live somewhere new.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** No confirmed student wrong-answer is recorded for this pattern in the source, but a common learner confusion is mixing up を機に with をきっかけに (more casual) and を契機に (more formal/written) — all three are close synonyms, and in most sentences any of the three would be acceptable, but register and the degree of "life-changing significance" implied can differ subtly. を機に tends to be the balanced, default choice between the two.
