# ～を限りに {#を限りに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～を限りに

**Furigana form:** ～をかぎりに

**Meaning:** "As of X, ... will stop" — a formal declaration that, from the point in time marked by X onward, something that had been continuing will be brought to an end.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + を限りに
- 今日を限りに禁煙する — "as of today, I'm quitting smoking"

**Typical context:** Formal, declarative register, both written and spoken — used in official announcements, resolutions, retirement speeches, and dramatic personal declarations, often with a decisive or emotionally weighty tone.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**～をもって**](grammar-をもって.md) — "with/as of X," a more neutral, business-formal way to announce an ending (common in official notices), without the emotional weight of を限りに.
- **～を最後に** — "making X the last time," a very similar meaning of ending a series or habit, often used for the final instance of a recurring action.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; ～を皮切りに marks the beginning of a series rather than its ending.

**Example sentences:**
1. 本年度を限りにこの講座の受講生募集を行わないことになりました。
   (We have decided that, as of this academic year, we will no longer be recruiting students for this course.)
2. 今日を限りにたばこをやめるぞ！
   (Starting today, I'm quitting smoking for good!)
3. これを限りにお前とは親子の縁を切る。以後親でもなく、子でもない。
   (As of today, I am severing the bond between us as parent and child. From now on, I am no longer your parent, nor you my child.)
4. 今月を限りにこの店は閉店することになった。
   (This shop is closing for good at the end of this month.)
5. 今回の大会を限りに、彼は現役を引退する。
   (This tournament will be the last of his active career — he's retiring after it.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern typically attaches to time-related nouns (今日, 今回, 本年度, etc.) marking a decisive cutoff point. It carries a formal, often resolute or emotionally weighty tone, so it isn't suited to mundane plans — reserve it for firm declarations of ending something significant.
