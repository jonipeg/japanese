# ～て以来 {#て以来}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～て以来

**Furigana form:** ～ていらい

**Meaning:** "Ever since doing X" — marks a point in the past after which a situation has continued up to the present.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞て形 (verb, te-form) + 以来
- (also usable with noun + 以来, e.g., 卒業以来)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, used in both spoken and written Japanese.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **てから** — "since doing X," more neutral simple sequencing, without the strong "ongoing continuation" nuance of て以来.
- **以来 (with noun)** — the noun-based variant, e.g., 卒業以来 ("ever since graduation").

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 日本に来て以来、ずっと一人暮らしをしている。
   (Ever since coming to Japan, I've been living alone the whole time.)
2. 彼女と別れて以来、彼は元気がない。
   (Ever since he broke up with her, he's been down.)
3. 大学を卒業して以来、一度も国に帰っていない。
   (I haven't returned to my home country even once since graduating from university.)
4. 入院して以来、彼は健康に気を遣うようになった。
   (Ever since being hospitalized, he's become careful about his health.)
5. あの事故があって以来、このあたりの交通規則が厳しくなった。
   (Ever since that accident, the traffic rules in this area have become stricter.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** て以来 implies a continuous, ongoing state or situation since the event — it cannot be used for a one-time completed action without a lasting consequence. Learners sometimes confuse it with てから, which is more neutral and doesn't require ongoing continuation.
