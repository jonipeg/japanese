# ～あまり(に) {#あまり(に)}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～余り(に)

**Furigana form:** ～あまり(に)

**Meaning:** "So much ~ that / to the extent that (as a result of excess)" — indicates that an extreme degree of some emotion or state directly causes an (often unusual or extreme) result.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形/た形 + あまり(に) — 例: 驚いたあまり (being so surprised that)
- 名詞の + あまり(に) — 例: 緊張のあまり (out of excessive nervousness)
- な形容詞 + な + あまり(に) — 例: 心配なあまり

**Typical context:** Written and formal spoken — common in narrative writing, news reports, and dramatic storytelling; less common in casual daily small talk.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **あまりの～に** — a related construction, e.g. あまりの暑さに (because of the extreme heat), places あまり before the noun instead of after.
- **すぎて** — "too much, so ~," more casual and broadly applicable, without the formal/dramatic weight of あまり.
- [**ほど**](grammar-ほど・ほどの・ほどだ.md) — "to the extent that," used for comparisons/degree but without あまり's "excess causing a result" structure.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 驚いたあまり、言葉を失ってしまった。
   (I was so surprised that I lost the ability to speak.)
2. 緊張のあまり、手が震えてしまった。
   (Out of sheer nervousness, my hands started shaking.)
3. 心配するあまり、夜も眠れなくなった。
   (I worried so much that I couldn't even sleep at night.)
4. 嬉しさのあまり、思わず涙が出た。
   (I was so overjoyed that tears came out unconsciously.)
5. 悲しみのあまり、何も食べられなくなった。
   (I was so overcome with sadness that I couldn't eat anything.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** あまり connects an extreme emotional/mental state to an unusual or involuntary physical result, unlike すぎて which can attach to almost any excessive action or state in casual speech — using あまり for mundane situations (e.g., 食べ過ぎたあまり, meaning merely "ate too much") sounds overly dramatic and unnatural.
