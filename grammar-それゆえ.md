# それゆえ {#それゆえ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** それ故

**Furigana form:** それゆえ

**Meaning:** "Therefore, hence, for that reason" — a very formal, literary conjunction marking the following clause as a reasoned consequence of the preceding one.

**Conjugation:** Sentence-initial conjunction, no conjugation needed — attaches at the sentence boundary, connecting the previous sentence/clause to the next. It can also appear as それゆえに mid-sentence or at a clause boundary (〜であり、それゆえに〜) in highly formal writing, but even then it functions as a connector between two logically linked statements rather than a conjugating element.

**Typical context:** Very formal, literary/written register — found in philosophy, legal writing, academic essays, classical-leaning prose, and formal speeches. It has an almost archaic, bookish flavor and is essentially never used in everyday conversation; using it in casual speech would sound theatrical or pompous.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**だから**](grammar-だから.md) — casual, spoken; the everyday conversational equivalent.
- [**それで**](grammar-それで.md) — casual-neutral, spoken; narrates a reason/result conversationally.
- [**そのため**](grammar-そのため.md) — neutral-formal, written; common and natural in ordinary explanatory writing, less elevated than それゆえ.
- [**したがって**](grammar-したがって.md) — formal, written/business/academic; the standard formal "therefore," less literary/archaic than それゆえ.
- [**というのは**](grammar-というのは.md) — written/spoken explanatory; explains the reason behind a statement rather than asserting a logical conclusion.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, but in formal/literary writing its contrastive counterpart would be にもかかわらず ("despite this") or それにもかかわらず, which denies the expected logical consequence.

**Example sentences:**
1. 人間は過ちを犯す生き物である。それゆえ、寛容の精神が必要とされる。
   (Human beings are creatures that err. For that reason, a spirit of tolerance is necessary.)
2. 真理は常に変化する。それゆえ、絶対的な答えを求めることは誤りである。
   (Truth is ever-changing. Hence, seeking an absolute answer is a mistake.)
3. 彼の理論は多くの矛盾を含んでいる。それゆえ、学界では広く支持されていない。
   (His theory contains many contradictions. Therefore, it is not widely supported in academic circles.)
4. 権力は腐敗しやすい。それゆえ、監視と抑制の仕組みが不可欠である。
   (Power tends to corrupt. For that reason, mechanisms of oversight and restraint are essential.)
5. 自由には責任が伴う。それゆえに、人は自らの行いに説明を求められる。
   (Freedom comes with responsibility. Hence, people are called to account for their own actions.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** The main pitfall is register mismatch in both directions: using それゆえ in ordinary conversation or casual writing makes the speaker sound stilted, overly formal, or even pretentious, since native speakers reserve it for essays, philosophy, and formal argumentation. Conversely, learners writing academic or legal Japanese sometimes default to the more common したがって or そのため when それゆえ would better match the elevated, literary register of the surrounding text — the choice among だから/それで/そのため/したがって/それゆえ should track smoothly with how formal and written the rest of the passage already is.
