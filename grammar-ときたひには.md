# ～ときたひには {#ときたひには}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ときた日には

**Furigana form:** ～ときたひには

**Meaning:** "Speaking of X (in such an extreme/bad case)" — introduces an extreme or exasperating example as the final straw in a list of complaints, often leading to a dramatic conclusion.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞／普通形 + ときた日には
- Example base: 性格も悪い → 性格も悪いときた日には

**Typical context:** Spoken, casual register; conveys strong exasperation or comic complaint, typically as the capstone of a list of grievances about a person or situation. Colloquial and expressive, not used in formal writing.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**ときたら**](grammar-ときたら.md) — "speaking of X..."; closely related (and arguably the base form of ときた日には), used to introduce a critical or exasperated remark about a topic, though ときたら can stand with milder single complaints while ときた日には emphasizes an extreme culminating case.
- [**となると**](grammar-となると.md) — "when it comes to X"; a more neutral topic-shift marker about changed circumstances, lacking ときた日には's exasperated, complaint-driven tone.
- [**ともなると**](grammar-ともなると・ともなれば.md) — "when it reaches the level of X"; shares some escalation nuance (reaching an extreme degree) but is more neutral/respectful in tone, often about status or scale, not personal complaint.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a plain, approving remark (～は素晴らしい, "X is wonderful") would contrast with ときた日には's exasperated complaint framing.

**Example sentences:**
1. 家事は全然だめ、趣味はパチンコ、その上性格も悪いときた日には、姉さんは当分嫁に行けないね。 (Hopeless at housework, pachinko for a hobby, and on top of that a bad personality — with a guy like that, my sister won't be getting married anytime soon.)
2. 給料は安い、残業は多い、おまけに上司が理不尽ときた日には、辞めたくなるのも当然だ。 (Low pay, lots of overtime, and on top of that an unreasonable boss — of course you'd want to quit.)
3. 遅刻する、忘れ物はする、その上言い訳ばかりときた日には、信用できるわけがない。 (He's late, forgets things, and on top of that just makes excuses — there's no way you can trust him.)
4. 雨は降る、電車は遅れる、おまけに傘まで忘れたときた日には、もう最悪な一日だった。 (It rained, the train was late, and on top of that I even forgot my umbrella — it was just the worst day.)
5. あの店、態度は悪い、値段は高い、その上料理もまずいときた日には、二度と行かないよ。 (That restaurant — rude staff, expensive, and on top of that the food is bad too — I'm never going back.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** ときた日には almost always appears at the end of a cascading list of complaints (X、Y、その上Zときた日には), functioning as the dramatic final blow that justifies an exasperated conclusion — using it in isolation without preceding complaints loses its rhetorical effect. This is a colloquial, somewhat theatrical expression; it should not be used in neutral or formal writing.
