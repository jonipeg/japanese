# ～(は)ですら {#ですら}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～（は）ですら

**Furigana form:** ～（は）ですら

**Meaning:** "Even X" — emphasizes an extreme or unexpected example to highlight how broadly a statement applies, often implying "if even this surprising case is true, then certainly everything else is too."

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞（＋は）＋ ですら
- Example base: 専門家 → 専門家ですら

**Typical context:** Formal, written/spoken register; used for emphatic rhetorical effect, common in essays, formal arguments, and persuasive speech. Slightly more literary than さえ.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**さえ**](grammar-さえ.md) — "even X"; the more common, slightly more neutral emphatic particle with nearly identical meaning and usage, interchangeable with ですら in most contexts, though ですら feels a touch more formal/written.
- [**すら**](grammar-すら.md) — functionally identical to ですら without the copula だ/です attached directly to certain word types; a stylistic variant rather than a separate meaning.
- [**も**](grammar-にかけては・も.md) — plain "also/even" in some contexts (e.g., 子供も知っている); a much weaker, more general emphatic particle lacking さえ／ですら's rhetorical "surprising extreme example" force.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; だけが／のみが ("only X") would contrast with ですら's implication of broad applicability extending even to extreme cases.

**Example sentences:**
1. この問題は専門家ですら解けないそうだ。 (Apparently, even experts can't solve this problem.)
2. 彼の名前は子供ですら知っている。 (Even children know his name.)
3. そんな基本的なことは初心者ですらわかる。 (Even a beginner understands something that basic.)
4. この暗号は最新のコンピューターですら解読できない。 (This code can't be deciphered even by the latest computers.)
5. 彼女の才能には先生ですら驚かされた。 (Even the teacher was astonished by her talent.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** ですら is used to present an extreme case (the most unlikely/surprising example) in order to imply the statement holds even more strongly for ordinary cases — the chosen example should be genuinely surprising or notable (experts, children, the latest technology), not a mundane, unremarkable instance. Learners should note ですら and さえ are nearly interchangeable, but ですら attaches with です and tends to appear in slightly more formal or written contexts, while さえ is more universally flexible across registers.
