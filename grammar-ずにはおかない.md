# ～ずにはおかない {#ずにはおかない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ずにはおかない

**Furigana form:** ～ずにはおかない

**Meaning:** "Is bound to (cause X)" / "won't let X not happen" — describes an inevitable natural consequence, or a strong determination/emotion that guarantees a particular outcome will occur.

**Conjugation:**
- V-ない形(remove ない) + ずにはおかない

**Typical context:** Formal, written/literary register, often describing forces of nature, strong emotions, or firm resolve that inevitably produce an effect.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**ずにはすまない**](grammar-ずにはすまない・ないではすまない.md) — expresses a social/moral obligation making it impossible to avoid doing something oneself, whereas ずにはおかない expresses that an outcome is GUARANTEED to occur, often affecting someone/something else, driven by natural force or strong determination.
- [**を余儀なくされる**](grammar-を余儀なくされる・させる.md) — describes being passively FORCED into an undesired state by external circumstance, in contrast to ずにはおかない's sense of actively guaranteeing an outcome through inevitability or strong will.
- [**んばかり**](grammar-んばかり.md) — a visual "on the verge of" simile, structurally different from ずにはおかない's assertion of a guaranteed eventual outcome.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. あんなことをされたんだから、仕返しをしないではおかないぞ。
   (After what they did to me, I'm bound to get revenge.)
2. この映画は観客を感動させずにはおかない。
   (This movie is bound to move its audience to tears.)
3. 彼の熱意は周りの人を動かさずにはおかなかった。
   (His passion inevitably moved the people around him.)
4. そのニュースは世間を騒がせずにはおかないだろう。
   (That news is bound to stir up public commotion.)
5. 真実はいつか明らかにならずにはおかない。
   (The truth is bound to come to light eventually.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** ずにはおかない is a frequently tested member of the N1 "inevitable outcome" cluster and is routinely confused with ずにはすまない because the two look almost identical. The key test: ずにはすまない is about the SUBJECT'S OWN inescapable obligation (私が謝らないではすまない, "I can't avoid apologizing"), while ずにはおかない is about an outcome or effect on SOMETHING/SOMEONE ELSE being inevitable due to an external force, emotion, or strong will (感動させずにはおかない, "is bound to move [the audience]").
