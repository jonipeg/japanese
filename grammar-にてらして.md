# ～にてらして {#にてらして}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に照らして

**Furigana form:** ～にてらして

**Meaning:** "Compared against/checked against X (a standard)" — evaluates or judges something by measuring it against an established standard, rule, or precedent.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に照らして
- Example base: 常識 → 常識に照らして

**Typical context:** Formal, written/spoken register; common in legal, academic, and official contexts where a judgment is made by reference to a rule, law, or accepted norm.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**に基づいて**](grammar-に基づいて.md) — "based on X"; similar in referencing a standard, but に基づいて emphasizes using X as a foundation for an action, while に照らして specifically emphasizes checking/comparing against X to judge or verify something.
- **にかんがみて** — "in light of X / considering X"; close in formal register and function, used to weigh a situation against a precedent or circumstance, with slightly more emphasis on reflective consideration than direct comparison.
- [**に沿って**](grammar-に沿って.md) — "in accordance with X / along the lines of X"; emphasizes following a guideline/policy, rather than the evaluative "measure against" sense of に照らして.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; 独自の基準で判断する ("judge by one's own independent standard") would contrast with照らして's reliance on an external established standard.

**Example sentences:**
1. 常識に照らして考えれば、いいことかどうかぐらいわかるだろう。 (If you think about it in light of common sense, you should be able to tell whether it's good or not.)
2. この行為は法律に照らして違法と判断された。 (This act was judged illegal when checked against the law.)
3. 過去の事例に照らして、今回の対応を検討すべきだ。 (We should consider this response by comparing it against past cases.)
4. 彼の行動を企業倫理に照らして評価する必要がある。 (We need to evaluate his conduct in light of corporate ethics.)
5. 現実に照らして、この計画は実行可能だろうか。 (Checked against reality, is this plan actually feasible?)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に照らして always requires a stated or clearly implied standard (law, common sense, precedent, ethics) against which something is being checked — using it without a clear reference point sounds incomplete or vague. Learners sometimes confuse it with に基づいて; remember 照らす literally means "to shine light on," so に照らして carries the image of holding something up to a light/standard for inspection, rather than simply building upon a foundation.
