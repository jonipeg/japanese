# ～によって・により {#によって・により}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に依って・により (commonly written in kana: によって・により)

**Furigana form:** ～によって・により

**Meaning:** Multi-purpose formal connector with three main senses depending on context: (1) "by means of X" (method/agent, often passive-voice actor), (2) "due to X / because of X" (cause), (3) "depending on X / according to X" (varying basis).

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + によって／により (例: 台風によって、専門家によって、場合によって)
- Attributive form: による + 名詞 (例: 地震による被害)
- により is a slightly more formal/written variant of によって, often used in written notices and legal text.

**Typical context:** Formal, used in both speech and writing; により is more common in written/official contexts (signs, legal documents), while によって is used more broadly in both speech and writing.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**で**](grammar-で.md) — can express cause in casual speech (台風で休校になった) but is less formal than によって.
- [**に基づいて**](grammar-に基づいて.md) — "based on X," overlaps with the causal/basis sense but implies a more formal/evidentiary grounding.
- **次第で** — "depending on X," overlaps with the "depending on" sense of によって but has a stronger nuance of "the outcome hinges entirely on X."

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. この橋は江戸時代に建てられた職人たちによって作られた。 (agent/method)
   (This bridge was built by craftsmen during the Edo period.)
2. 台風により、多くの地域で停電が発生した。 (cause)
   (Due to the typhoon, power outages occurred in many areas.)
3. 結果は人によって異なる。 (depending on)
   (The results differ depending on the person.)
4. この小説はある実在の事件によって書かれたそうだ。 (method/basis)
   (This novel is said to have been written based on an actual incident.)
5. 地震による被害は甚大だった。 (cause, attributive)
   (The damage caused by the earthquake was immense.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** によって is highly polysemous, and learners must determine the intended sense (agent, cause, or variation) from context — a common mistake is using it interchangeably with で in casual speech, when によって/により sounds notably more formal. により is essentially a stylistic variant of によって favored in written/official registers, not a different grammatical function.
