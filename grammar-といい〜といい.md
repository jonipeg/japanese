# ～といい～といい {#といい〜といい}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**1. Kanji version:** ～と言い～と言い (rarely written with kanji; usually kana)

**2. Furigana version:** ～といい～といい

**3. Meaning:** Both X and Y / whether you look at X or Y — citing two illustrative examples to support an evaluative statement about the whole.

**4. Conjugation (connection form):** 名詞 + といい、名詞 + といい

**5. Usage context:** Written, somewhat formal/literary register, used to back up an opinion or evaluation with supporting examples. Neutral politeness.

**6. Similar grammar structures:**
- なり〜なり — offers a CHOICE between actions, not supporting examples for an evaluation. This is the confirmed recurring real-world confusion: the same student mixed up なり〜なり and といい〜といい across two different tests.
- にも〜にも — "in both X and Y," a broader structure usable for more general dual-applicability statements, without といい's specific "illustrative evidence for an evaluation" nuance.
- とも〜とも — an archaic/literary "both... and..." pattern, more bookish than といい〜といい.
- わ〜わ — a casual, exclamatory pattern listing simultaneous states/reactions ("わ、わ"), much more casual register than といい〜といい.
- といった〜といった — means "such as X and such as Y," an exemplifying list rather than evaluative evidence; different nuance.

**7. Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**8. Example sentences:**
1. あのレストランは味といい、サービスといい、最高級だ。(That restaurant is top-notch, whether you consider the taste or the service.)
2. この着物は柄といい、色といい、とても上品だ。(This kimono is very elegant, both in its pattern and its color.)
3. 彼女は性格といい、能力といい、申し分のない人材だ。(She is a flawless talent, both in personality and ability.)
4. この部屋は広さといい、日当たりといい、申し分ない。(This room is excellent, both in its spaciousness and its sunlight.)
5. 新しい車はデザインといい、燃費といい、満足のいくものだった。(The new car was satisfying, both in its design and its fuel efficiency.)

**9. Common mistakes / key nuances:** Confirmed recurring student error mixing up といい〜といい with なり〜なり. Remember といい〜といい never offers a choice of action — it always cites two aspects/examples to collectively support one evaluative judgment about the subject as a whole.
