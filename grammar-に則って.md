# ～に則って {#に則って}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～に則って

**Furigana form:** ～にのっとって

**Meaning:** To act by following a standard, norm, rule, law, or tradition as the governing basis — "in accordance with / following X."

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に則って／に則った＋名詞 (base verb: 則る)
- 伝統に則って — "in accordance with tradition"

**Typical context:** Formal, often ceremonial written/spoken register — used for laws, regulations, sportsmanship, customs, and traditional events.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **に従って** — "following X," more general and neutral, usable for instructions, orders, or trends as well as rules.
- [**に即して**](grammar-に即して.md) — emphasizes matching actual conditions, whereas に則って emphasizes following an established norm or precedent.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～に反して ("contrary to X") describes acting against the established rule or standard.

**Example sentences:**
1. スポーツマンシップに則って試合をする。
   (Play the match in accordance with sportsmanship.)
2. 古くからの伝統に則った祭りが行われている。
   (A festival conducted in accordance with age-old traditions is held.)
3. 規則に則って手続きを進める。
   (Proceed with the procedure in accordance with the rules.)
4. 法律に則って契約を結んだ。
   (We concluded the contract in accordance with the law.)
5. しきたりに則って結婚式を行った。
   (We held the wedding ceremony in accordance with customary practice.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に則って carries a formal, almost ceremonial nuance and collocates with nouns like 伝統, 規則, 法律, しきたり, スポーツマンシップ — established codes of conduct or traditions — rather than everyday instructions. For casual "following the rules of a game," に従って is more natural; に則って signals a more solemn or official adherence.
