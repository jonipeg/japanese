# ～に加えて {#に加えて}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に加えて

**Furigana form:** ～にくわえて

**Meaning:** "In addition to X" — adds a further element on top of something already mentioned, often intensifying or compounding a situation.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に加えて — 雨に加えて (in addition to the rain)
- 動詞普通形/句 + ことに加えて — 遅れたことに加えて (in addition to being late)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, common in both speech and writing — news reports, formal writing, and everyday explanations of compounding factors.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**のみならず**](grammar-のみならず.md) — "not only X (but also Y)," more formal/literary, emphasizes the surprising scope rather than simple addition.
- **に加え** — same meaning, more clipped/written style often seen in news headlines.
- **ばかりでなく** — "not just X," similar additive nuance, slightly more conversational than のみならず.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 大雨に加えて、強風も吹いていたため、電車が止まった。
   (In addition to heavy rain, strong winds were also blowing, so the trains stopped.)
2. 彼は英語に加えて、フランス語も話せる。
   (In addition to English, he can also speak French.)
3. 物価の上昇に加えて、税金も上がったので、生活が苦しくなった。
   (In addition to rising prices, taxes also went up, making life harder.)
4. この仕事は経験に加えて、高いコミュニケーション能力が求められる。
   (This job requires high communication skills in addition to experience.)
5. 風邪に加えて、頭痛もひどくなってきた。
   (On top of a cold, my headache has also gotten worse.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に加えて adds a complementary factor (often compounding a negative or building toward a cumulative effect) — distinct from だけでなく which more directly contrasts "not just A, also B" as a listing device; mixing them up rarely breaks meaning but shifts emphasis.
