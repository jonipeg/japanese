# ～かわりに {#かわりに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～かわりに（代わりに）

**Furigana form:** ～かわりに

**Meaning:** Two usages: (1) "instead of X" — substitution; (2) "in return for X / while, on the other hand" — expresses a trade-off or balancing contrast between two facts about the same subject.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + の + かわりに
- 動詞普通形 + かわりに

**Typical context:** Written and spoken, neutral register — very common in both everyday conversation and writing.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **代わって** — "on behalf of / replacing," focuses on acting in someone's place (agency), rather than かわりに's balance/trade-off nuance.
- [**反面**](grammar-反面・半面.md) — "on the other hand," a similarly balancing contrast, but more formal/written than かわりに.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 今日は会議に出席できないので、かわりに部下を行かせます。
   (I can't attend the meeting today, so I'll send my subordinate instead.)
2. 手伝ってくれたかわりに、昼食をおごった。
   (In return for helping me, I treated them to lunch.)
3. この部屋は広いかわりに、家賃が高い。
   (This room is spacious, but on the other hand the rent is high.)
4. 砂糖のかわりにはちみつを使った。
   (I used honey instead of sugar.)
5. 彼女は厳しいかわりに、とても面倒見がいい。
   (She's strict, but in return she takes very good care of people.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** The two nuances (plain substitution vs. trade-off/contrast) are distinguished only by context — learners sometimes miss the "balancing two facts about the same subject" reading (e.g., spacious but expensive) in favor of assuming simple replacement every time.
