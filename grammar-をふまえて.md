# ～をふまえて {#をふまえて}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を踏まえて

**Furigana form:** ～をふまえて

**Meaning:** "Based on/considering X" — describes taking a fact, prior experience, or piece of information into account before proceeding with a judgment, plan, or statement.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + を踏まえて
- Example base: 去年の反省 → 去年の反省を踏まえて

**Typical context:** Formal, written/spoken register; very common in business, academic, and official contexts when explaining that a decision or discussion properly accounts for prior information. Literary/formal in tone.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**に基づいて**](grammar-に基づいて.md) — "based on X"; very close in meaning, though に基づいて often implies a more objective, rule-like foundation (data, law, standard), while を踏まえて emphasizes incorporating a prior fact or experience into consideration before acting.
- **を考慮して** — "taking X into consideration"; nearly synonymous and commonly interchangeable, perhaps slightly more general-purpose than the specifically-deliberative を踏まえて.
- **を参考にして** — "using X as a reference"; similar idea of incorporating prior information, but を参考にして implies a looser, optional reference, while を踏まえて implies the information is properly and seriously factored in.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～を無視して ("ignoring X") expresses the reverse — disregarding relevant information rather than accounting for it.

**Example sentences:**
1. 去年の反省を踏まえて今年の計画を立てましょう。 (Let's make this year's plan based on last year's reflections.)
2. 市場調査の結果を踏まえて、新商品の価格を決定した。 (We decided the new product's price based on the market research results.)
3. これまでの経験を踏まえて、今後の対策を考える必要がある。 (We need to think about future countermeasures based on our experience so far.)
4. 専門家の意見を踏まえたうえで、最終的な判断を下した。 (We made the final decision after properly taking the experts' opinions into account.)
5. 現場の声を踏まえて、制度を見直すことになった。 (It was decided to review the system based on feedback from the field.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** を踏まえて always implies a deliberate, thoughtful incorporation of prior information into a following action or decision — it should be followed by a clear subsequent action/judgment that reflects that consideration. Learners sometimes use it loosely like "regarding X," but its core meaning is closer to "having properly taken X into account (before deciding/acting)," so it needs a following clause that shows the result of that consideration.
