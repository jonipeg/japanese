# ～に基づいて {#に基づいて}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に基づいて

**Furigana form:** ～にもとづいて

**Meaning:** "Based on X" — indicates that an action, decision, or creation is grounded in or derived from a particular source, such as data, law, experience, or a principle.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (source/basis noun) + に基づいて (例: データに基づいて、法律に基づいて、経験に基づいて)
- Attributive form: に基づく + 名詞 (例: 事実に基づく物語)

**Typical context:** Formal, mostly written — common in reports, legal/academic writing, news articles, and official explanations of decisions or policies.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**をもとに**](grammar-をもとに(して).md) — "using X as a basis," slightly more flexible/casual, often used for creative works (小説をもとに映画を作る).
- **に従って** — "in accordance with X," focuses on compliance/following rules rather than grounding/derivation.
- **によると** — "according to X (a source of information)," used for citing sources of information rather than grounds for action.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. この政策は、最新の調査データに基づいて作られた。
   (This policy was created based on the latest survey data.)
2. 彼の発言は、長年の経験に基づいている。
   (His remarks are based on years of experience.)
3. 法律に基づいて、適切な処分が行われた。
   (Appropriate action was taken based on the law.)
4. この映画は実話に基づいて制作された。
   (This movie was made based on a true story.)
5. 科学的根拠に基づいた治療法が求められている。
   (Treatment methods based on scientific evidence are required.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に基づいて emphasizes an objective, often official or evidentiary basis (laws, data, facts), making it more formal than をもとに, which can be used more loosely, including for creative adaptations (実話をもとにした映画 is also natural, but に基づいた implies closer fidelity to the source).
