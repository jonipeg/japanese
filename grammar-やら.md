# ～やら {#やら}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～やら

**Furigana form:** ～やら (no special kanji reading)

**Meaning:** Two related uses: (1) "～やら～やら" — listing multiple things/feelings in a chaotic, non-exhaustive way ("what with X and Y..."); (2) "〜やら" (single, often with question word) — expresses uncertainty, "I wonder..." similar to か.

**Conjugation:**
- Listing: 名詞/動詞普通形 + やら + 名詞/動詞普通形 + やら: 泣くやら笑うやら (crying, laughing, and so on)
- Uncertainty: 疑問詞 + 普通形 + やら: 何が起こるやら分からない (I have no idea what will happen)

**Typical context:** Spoken, somewhat casual-to-neutral register; conveys emotional overwhelm or vagueness. Common in storytelling about chaotic or confusing situations.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **〜とか〜とか** — also lists examples, but more casual and simply exemplifying without the "overwhelmed/chaotic" nuance.
- **〜か〜か** — similar uncertainty listing but more neutral/formal.
- **〜のやら〜のやら** — variant emphasizing bewilderment, often used self-deprecatingly.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 引っ越しの日は荷造りやら掃除やらで一日中忙しかった。
   (On moving day, I was busy all day with packing, cleaning, and so on.)
2. 彼が成功するやら失敗するやら、誰にも分からない。
   (Whether he'll succeed or fail, nobody knows.)
3. 子供が熱を出すやら、仕事が忙しいやらで、今週は大変だった。
   (With my kid running a fever and work being busy, this week was tough.)
4. どこへ行ったやら、彼の姿はもう見えなかった。
   (I wonder where he went — he was nowhere to be seen anymore.)
5. 嬉しいやら恥ずかしいやら、複雑な気持ちになった。
   (I felt a complicated mix of happiness and embarrassment.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners often confuse the listing やら〜やら (requires at least two paired items) with the single uncertainty やら (used with question words like 何/どこ/誰). Also, やら listing implies a messy, uncontrolled accumulation of events/emotions — don't use it for simple neutral lists (use や or とか instead).
