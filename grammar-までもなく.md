# ～までもなく {#までもなく}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～までもなく

**Furigana form:** ～までもなく

**Meaning:** "Without even needing to go so far as to X" / "there's no need to even X" — states that performing a given action would be excessive or unnecessary because the matter is already obvious or trivial.

**Conjugation:**
- V辞書形 + までもなく

**Typical context:** Formal-leaning register, usable in both written Japanese and moderately formal speech.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**にはあたらない**](grammar-にはあたらない.md) — similar "unnecessary/unwarranted" nuance, but にはあたらない is about a REACTION (surprise, praise, criticism) being unwarranted, while までもなく is about an ACTION being unnecessary because the point is already obvious.
- [**にかたくない**](grammar-にかたくない.md) — means "not hard to imagine," a different focus entirely (ease of imagining something), not unnecessariness of an action.
- [**ようがない**](grammar-ようがない.md) — means "there's no way to do X" due to lack of means, an impossibility, whereas までもなく is about something being unnecessary, not impossible.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. あの映画はわざわざ映画館まで見に行くまでもないよ。
   (You don't need to go all the way to the theater to see that movie.)
2. 彼が優秀なのは言うまでもない。
   (It goes without saying that he is excellent.)
3. こんな簡単な問題は説明するまでもないだろう。
   (There's no need to even explain such a simple problem.)
4. わざわざ聞くまでもなく、答えは明らかだ。
   (Without even needing to ask, the answer is obvious.)
5. 改めて確認するまでもなく、契約内容は正しい。
   (There's no need to double-check again — the contract content is correct.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** までもなく belongs to the N1 extreme-degree/unnecessariness confusable cluster and is most often mixed up with にはあたらない. The key test: までもなく always attaches to a VERB describing an ACTION being skippable because it's obviously unnecessary (行くまでもない, "no need to even go"), while にはあたらない attaches to a verb describing a REACTION (驚く, 褒める) being unwarranted given the circumstances — one is about skipping an action, the other about an inappropriate emotional/evaluative response.
