# ～をめぐって {#をめぐって}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を巡って

**Furigana form:** ～をめぐって

**Meaning:** "Surrounding, over (the issue of), concerning (a dispute/debate)" — indicates that a topic is the subject of debate, conflict, or differing opinions among multiple parties.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + をめぐって: この問題をめぐって (over/concerning this issue)
- Modifying form: 〜をめぐる + 名詞: 領土をめぐる争い (a dispute over territory)

**Typical context:** Formal, written-and-spoken register — common in news reports, legal/political discussions, and descriptions of conflicts, debates, or negotiations centered on a specific topic.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**に関して**](grammar-について・に関して.md) — more neutral "regarding/concerning X," without the specific implication of conflict/debate.
- [**について**](grammar-について・に関して.md) — general-purpose "about X," neutral and much more casual/common, lacks the adversarial nuance.
- **を争って** — "fighting over X," more explicitly about active competition/conflict, often physical or legal.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. この土地の所有権をめぐって、長年裁判が続いている。
   (A lawsuit over the ownership of this land has continued for many years.)
2. 新しい法案をめぐって、与党と野党が激しく対立している。
   (The ruling and opposition parties are fiercely clashing over the new bill.)
3. 遺産相続をめぐる兄弟間のトラブルは珍しくない。
   (Trouble between siblings over inheritance is not uncommon.)
4. その発言をめぐって、記者会見は大きな波紋を呼んだ。
   (That remark stirred up a major controversy at the press conference.)
5. 環境問題をめぐって、各国の意見は大きく分かれている。
   (Opinions among countries are sharply divided over environmental issues.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** をめぐって specifically implies that the topic is a point of contention, debate, or differing viewpoints among multiple parties — it's not a neutral "about X" like について. Learners sometimes use をめぐって for simple neutral topics with no conflict involved, which sounds odd; reserve it for disputes, negotiations, or controversial subjects.
