# ～を問わず {#を問わず}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を問わず

**Furigana form:** ～をとわず

**Meaning:** "Regardless of, irrespective of" — indicates that a stated distinction or category (age, gender, experience, weather, etc.) makes no difference to what follows.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + を問わず: 年齢を問わず (regardless of age), 経験の有無を問わず (regardless of whether one has experience or not)
- Common with paired opposites: 昼夜を問わず (day or night, regardless), 国籍を問わず (regardless of nationality)

**Typical context:** Formal, written register — common in job postings, official announcements, and formal written contexts stating that a category is irrelevant to eligibility or applicability.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**にかかわらず**](grammar-にかかわらず・にもかかわらず.md) — nearly synonymous, "regardless of," but can also attach after a volitional clause (する・しないにかかわらず); slightly more versatile.
- **も〜も** — simple "both X and Y," without the formal "regardless of distinction" framing.
- **を問いません** — polite verbal form sometimes seen in announcements, functionally equivalent to を問わず used predicatively.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though ～に限って ("limited to X only") contrasts by restricting rather than disregarding a category.

**Example sentences:**
1. 本イベントは年齢を問わず、どなたでも参加できます。
   (This event is open to anyone, regardless of age.)
2. 経験の有無を問わず、やる気のある方を募集しています。
   (We are recruiting motivated people, regardless of whether they have experience.)
3. このホットラインは昼夜を問わず対応しています。
   (This hotline is available around the clock, regardless of day or night.)
4. 国籍を問わず、優秀な人材を採用する方針だ。
   (The policy is to hire talented people regardless of nationality.)
5. 天候を問わず、試合は予定通り行われる。
   (Regardless of the weather, the match will be held as scheduled.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** を問わず is formal and typically appears in official/written contexts (job ads, announcements) rather than casual speech — using it in casual conversation can sound stiff. It requires a noun representing a category/distinction (age, gender, time, weather), not a full clause; for clause-level "regardless of whether X or not," にかかわらず is more flexible (e.g., 雨が降るかどうかにかかわらず).
