# ～をもとに（して） {#をもとに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を基に（して）

**Furigana form:** ～をもとに（して）

**Meaning:** "Based on, using as a basis" — indicates that something (information, data, experience) serves as the foundation or source material for creating, deciding, or judging something else.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + をもとに(して): 実話をもとにして映画を作った (made a movie based on a true story)
- Modifying form: 〜をもとにした + 名詞: 実話をもとにした映画 (a movie based on a true story)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, both spoken and written. Common in describing the source material behind creative works, research, decisions, and designs.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**に基づいて**](grammar-に基づいて.md) — nearly synonymous and more formal/written, often used in official or academic contexts (法律に基づいて — based on the law).
- **を参考に(して)** — "using X as a reference," implies a looser, supplementary source rather than a direct foundation.
- **をベースに** — casual loanword equivalent, common in creative/business contexts.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. この映画は実際にあった事件をもとにして作られた。
   (This movie was made based on an actual incident.)
2. アンケート結果をもとに、新商品の企画を立てた。
   (We planned the new product based on the survey results.)
3. 祖母から聞いた話をもとにして、小説を書いた。
   (I wrote a novel based on stories I heard from my grandmother.)
4. 過去のデータをもとに、将来の需要を予測する。
   (We predict future demand based on past data.)
5. このキャラクターは伝説上の人物をもとにデザインされた。
   (This character was designed based on a legendary figure.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** をもとに implies the referenced material is a direct foundation/source for creating or deciding something — compare with に基づいて, which is more formal and often used for rule-based or official reasoning (laws, regulations, formal criteria), while をもとに is more common for creative works, plans, and general decision-making. Don't confuse with を参考に, which implies the source is merely a reference aid rather than the core foundation.
