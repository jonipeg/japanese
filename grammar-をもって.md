# ～をもって {#をもって}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～をもって／～をもってすれば

**Furigana form:** ～をもって／～をもってすれば

**Meaning:** Two related senses: (1) "by means of X" / "using X" (instrument/means, especially emphatic when intensified as ～をもってすれば, "if one were to use X"); (2) "as of X" / "ending at X" (marking a formal cutoff point or deadline). These are distinct nuances that should not be conflated.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (noun) + をもって
- 名詞 (noun) + をもってすれば (emphatic "means" form)
- Example from source: 書面をもって (by means of a written document) ／ 午後9時をもって終了 (ending as of 9pm)

**Typical context:** Formal, written Japanese — characteristic of official announcements, business correspondence, ceremonial speech, and legal/administrative notices. Rarely used in casual conversation; has a stiff, formal, slightly old-fashioned register. Polite and professional, never rude.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**によって**](grammar-によって・により.md) — "by means of X / due to X"; broader and more common means/cause marker used in everyday formal Japanese, whereas をもって is reserved for more ceremonial/official contexts.
- **を限りに (かぎりに)** — "as of X (marking a cutoff/last occurrence)"; nearly synonymous with the "ending point" sense of をもって, often interchangeable in cutoff announcements (e.g., 本日をもって／を限りに終了).
- [**で**](grammar-で.md) — the plain, everyday particle also used for means ("道具で" = "with a tool") or cutoff points ("今日で終わり" = "ending today"); をもって is simply the formal/ceremonial equivalent.
- [**をもとに**](grammar-をもとに(して).md) — "based on X"; describes a basis or source of information/material, not a means of action or a cutoff point — easily confused due to surface similarity ("を...に/て" structure).

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though ～から ("starting from X") contrasts with the "ending/cutoff" sense of をもって by marking a beginning rather than an end.

**Example sentences:**
1. 審査の結果は書面をもってお知らせします。
   (The results of the review will be announced by means of a written document.)
2. 本日の営業は午後9時をもって終了いたします。
   (Today's business hours will end as of 9:00 PM.)
3. 彼の演説は満場の拍手をもって迎えられた。
   (His speech was greeted with thunderous applause.)
4. 当選者には、後日メールをもってご連絡いたします。
   (Winners will be contacted at a later date by means of email.)
5. 現在の知識をもってすれば、この問題は十分解決可能だ。
   (If one were to use current knowledge, this problem could certainly be solved.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** A margin note (scan1, item 61a/61b) flags that students must not conflate the two senses of をもって: をもって/をもってすれば used for "means" ("using/by means of X") is distinct from をもって used alone for "ending/cutoff point, up to here" — the correct reading depends entirely on context (is X a tool/method, or a point in time?). Misreading a cutoff-point sentence as a "means" sentence (or vice versa) is the key error to avoid.
