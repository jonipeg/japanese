# ～ように（例示）{#ように-example}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ように

**Furigana form:** ～ように

**Meaning:** "Like..., as if..." — used to give an example or draw a comparison, illustrating something by likening it to another thing or situation.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + の + ように: 鳥のように (like a bird)
- 動詞辞書形/ない形 + ように: 彼が言ったように (as he said); 忘れないように見える (looks as if [not] forgetting — comparison use)
- Often paired with 例えば (for example) at the start of the sentence: 例えば、鳥のように自由に飛びたい。

**Typical context:** Spoken and written, neutral register. Common in descriptive and figurative language, similes, and giving illustrative examples.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **〜みたいに** — casual equivalent of ように, used in spoken Japanese.
- **〜らしい** — expresses "seems like/typical of," different nuance (inference/typicality rather than direct comparison).
- **〜同様に** — more formal, "likewise/similarly," used in written contexts.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 鳥のように自由に空を飛べたらいいのに。
   (I wish I could fly freely through the sky like a bird.)
2. 彼女はいつも天使のように優しい。
   (She is always kind like an angel.)
3. 先生がおっしゃったように、練習は裏切らない。
   (Just as the teacher said, practice doesn't betray you.)
4. まるで夢を見ているように、信じられない気持ちだった。
   (It felt unbelievable, as if I were dreaming.)
5. 例えば、水のように形を変えられる柔軟性が必要だ。
   (For example, we need flexibility that can change shape, like water.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Don't confuse this comparison/example use of ように with the purpose ("so that") use of ように (see separate entry) — the example use typically follows a noun+の or describes a concrete comparison/manner, often reinforced with まるで or 例えば, while the purpose use pairs with potential-form or non-volitional verbs and expresses a goal.
