# ～べくもない {#べくもない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～べくもない

**Furigana form:** ～べくもない

**Meaning:** "It is completely impossible/out of the question to X" — strongly denies the possibility of something, often something hoped for.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形 + べくもない (する verbs: する→すべくもない or するべくもない)
- Example base: 望む → 望むべくもない

**Typical context:** Formal, written/literary register; used in essays, formal writing, and elevated speech to flatly deny a hoped-for possibility. Not used in casual conversation.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **はずがない** — "there's no way X"; a more general denial of likelihood/possibility, usable in casual speech, whereas べくもない is restricted to literary register and typically denies something desired or expected.
- [**ようがない**](grammar-ようがない.md) — "there's no way to do X (no method exists)"; focuses on lack of means/method, while べくもない focuses on the impossibility of an outcome or expectation.
- [**べからず**](grammar-べからず・べからざる.md) — a related べく-stem construction meaning "must not" (prohibition), structurally similar but semantically a prohibition rather than an impossibility statement.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; conceptually 容易に～できる ("can easily X") expresses the reverse — that something is readily possible.

**Example sentences:**
1. どんなにがんばっても、これ以上の成績は望むべくもない。 (No matter how hard I try, hoping for better results than this is simply impossible.)
2. 一度失った信頼を取り戻すことは望むべくもない。 (Regaining trust once lost is something one cannot even hope for.)
3. 当時の技術では、宇宙旅行など想像すべくもなかった。 (With the technology of that time, space travel was something not even imaginable.)
4. 彼の実力では、優勝など望むべくもない。 (Given his skill level, hoping to win the championship is out of the question.)
5. 資金が尽きた今、計画の続行は望むべくもない。 (Now that the funds have run out, continuing the plan is simply not possible.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** べくもない almost always pairs with verbs of hoping/wishing/imagining (望む, 想像する, 期待する) — it isn't a general-purpose "impossible" tag for any verb. Learners sometimes try to attach it to arbitrary action verbs, which sounds unnatural; keep it to the "impossible to even hope/imagine" semantic field. Also note べく is the classical conjunctive form of べし, so べくもない is grammatically archaic and should not be conjugated further (no past/negative variants beyond this fixed shape).
