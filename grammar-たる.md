# ～たる(もの) {#たる}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～たる(者)

**Furigana form:** ～たる(もの)

**Meaning:** "Being in the position/status of X (and thus should behave accordingly)" — describes someone as holding a particular status or role, with the implication that they carry a corresponding duty or expected standard of behavior.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (noun) + たる + 名詞 (most often 者/もの, "person")
- Example from source: 政治家たる者 (one who holds the position of a politician)

**Typical context:** Formal, literary/written Japanese — derived from classical grammar (たり copula). Common in moralizing or admonishing statements about duty and expected conduct (e.g., leaders, professionals, public figures). Not used in casual speech; has a somewhat lecturing, old-fashioned, authoritative tone.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**めく**](grammar-めく.md) — "to have the air/semblance of X" (e.g., 春めく, "to feel spring-like"); describes a vague resemblance or seasonal quality, completely unrelated to status/duty.
- **ぽい** — casual suffix "-ish, seeming like X" (e.g., 子供っぽい, "childish"); a colloquial resemblance marker, much more casual than たる's formal status-and-duty nuance.
- **みたいな** — casual "like X / similar to X"; a plain comparison, lacking any sense of formal status or obligation.
- [**にしたところで**](grammar-にしたところで・にしたって.md) — "even granting X / even if we assume X"; a concessive conditional, unrelated to describing a status-based duty.
- [**ばかりに**](grammar-ばかりに.md) — "just because of X (an unfortunate result followed)"; a causal construction expressing regret over a negative consequence, structurally and semantically unrelated to たる.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 政治家たる者が、そんな無責任な発言をするなんて、信じられない。
   (It's unbelievable that someone in the position of a politician would make such an irresponsible statement.)
2. 教師たる者、生徒の手本とならなければならない。
   (One who holds the position of teacher must serve as a model for their students.)
3. 親たる者、子供の安全を最優先に考えるべきだ。
   (One who holds the position of a parent should consider their child's safety the top priority.)
4. リーダーたる者は、どんな時も冷静さを失ってはならない。
   (One who holds the position of leader must never lose their composure, no matter the situation.)
5. 医師たる者、患者の命を何よりも大切にすべきである。
   (One who holds the position of doctor should value a patient's life above all else.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern was MC-confirmed (scan4) and also appeared as a distractor in 文法テスト4回目 Q2/Q12, tested against めく, ぽい, みたいな, にしたところで, and ばかりに. The key tell for たる(もの) is that it always frames a status/role and an implied moral duty ("as someone who holds position X, one must/should..."); none of the distractors carry this status-plus-obligation meaning, so students should look for a noun denoting a role or position followed by an expectation of proper conduct.
