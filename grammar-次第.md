# ～次第 {#次第}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～次第

**Furigana form:** ～しだい

**Meaning:** "As soon as X (then immediately Y)" — attached to a verb stem, indicates that the following action will be carried out immediately upon completion/occurrence of the first.

**Conjugation:**
- Verb (ます stem) + 次第
- 着き次第、ご連絡します (I will contact you as soon as I arrive)
- 準備ができ次第、出発しましょう (Let's depart as soon as preparations are done)

**Typical context:** Formal spoken and written Japanese — business emails, announcements, customer service language. Implies promptness/priority given to the second action.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **たら** — general conditional "once/when," less formal, doesn't emphasize immediacy as strongly.
- [**たとたんに**](grammar-たとたんに.md) — "the moment X happened," focuses on a surprising simultaneity rather than a planned prompt follow-up action.
- **てすぐに** — "right after doing X," more casual equivalent with the same immediacy nuance.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 結果が分かり次第、お知らせいたします。
   (As soon as the results are known, I will let you know.)
2. 空港に到着し次第、電話してください。
   (Please call me as soon as you arrive at the airport.)
3. 準備が整い次第、会議を始めます。
   (We will start the meeting as soon as preparations are complete.)
4. 商品が入荷し次第、発送いたします。
   (We will ship the product as soon as it arrives in stock.)
5. 詳細が決まり次第、改めてご連絡いたします。
   (Once the details are decided, I will contact you again.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 次第 (here) attaches only to the ます-stem of verbs, not the dictionary or た form — learners sometimes mistakenly use the plain past form. This usage must be distinguished from the separate ～次第だ construction (explaining circumstances/"it depends on"), which is a different grammatical pattern despite sharing the same word.
