# ～こそ {#こそ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～こそ

**Furigana form:** ～こそ (no kanji reading needed; always kana)

**Meaning:** "Precisely/especially X" — an emphatic particle that singles out the preceding word as the one true/important thing, often in contrast to other possibilities. Also used in set phrases like こちらこそ ("the pleasure is mine, too") and だからこそ ("precisely because of that").

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + こそ
- 今年こそ (this year, of all years / this year for sure)
- ～てこそ (only by doing ~ can one truly...) — Verb-て + こそ
- だからこそ (precisely because of that)

**Typical context:** Used in both speech and writing; adds emphatic force and can sound assertive or determined. Common in resolutions, rebuttals, and polite exchanges (こちらこそ).

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **は (topic particle)** — plain topic marking without the contrastive emphasis that こそ adds.
- [**さえ**](grammar-さえ.md) — "even X," emphasizes an extreme example rather than singling out the correct/important one.
- [**だけ**](grammar-だけ.md) — "only," limits scope but lacks the assertive "precisely" nuance.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though で も ("even/also") can feel like the inclusive counter-emphasis.

**Example sentences:**
1. 今年こそ、日本語能力試験に合格するぞ。
   (This year, of all years, I will pass the JLPT!)
2. 「ありがとうございました。」「こちらこそ、ありがとうございました。」
   ("Thank you very much." "No, thank YOU.")
3. 困っている時にこそ、友達の大切さがわかる。
   (It's precisely when you're in trouble that you realize how important friends are.)
4. 失敗したからこそ、学べることがある。
   (Precisely because you failed, there are things you can learn.)
5. 努力してこそ、本当の成功が得られる。
   (Only through effort can one achieve true success.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners often confuse こそ with は or さえ; こそ is stronger and more assertive, pointing to "this one specifically" rather than merely topicalizing. Don't overuse it in neutral descriptive sentences — it carries emotional/rhetorical weight.
