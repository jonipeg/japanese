# ～ともあろう者が {#ともあろう者}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ともあろう者が (also ～ともあろう会社が, ～ともあろう人が, etc.)

**Furigana form:** ～ともあろう者(もの)が

**Meaning:** "Someone/something of such standing as X (should not have done this)" — expresses strong criticism directed at a subject with high status, reputation, or responsibility, for behaving in a way unbefitting that status.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (noun denoting a status/role/organization) + ともあろう + 名詞（者／人／会社など）+ が
- Fixed pattern: [prestigious noun] + ともあろう + [者/人/company/etc.] + が + [criticism of an unbefitting action]

**Typical context:** Formal, often written or oratorical register; carries a strongly critical, almost rhetorical-scolding tone. Used to express disbelief/indignation that someone of standing has acted below expectations — not rude in the sense of vulgarity, but pointed and judgmental.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**ならいざしらず**](grammar-ならいざしらず.md) — means "that may be one thing, but..." (contrastive concession); differs because ともあろう者が is a direct criticism of a single high-status subject's action, not a two-case contrast between a lesser and worse scenario.
- [**ならまだしも**](grammar-ならまだしも.md) — means "if it were just X, that would be bearable, but..."; again a tolerance-threshold comparison rather than a direct status-based criticism.
- **にかかって** — means "when it comes to X('s hands/power)"; describes outcomes determined by someone's skill/power (often ironic), unrelated to criticizing someone's status-inappropriate failure.

**Opposite grammar structures:** A conceptual opposite would be さすが ("as expected of X"), which praises someone for living up to their reputation, whereas ともあろう者が criticizes someone for failing to live up to theirs. No fixed single "direct opposite" pattern, but さすが serves as the semantic inverse.

**Example sentences:**
1. 母親ともあろう者が赤ん坊の世話も忘れてパチンコに夢中になるなんて、信じられない。
   (That someone who is supposed to be a mother would forget to care for her baby and get absorbed in pachinko instead is unbelievable.)
2. 大企業ともあろう会社が、こんな初歩的なミスで顧客情報を漏らすとは。
   (That a company of such standing as a major corporation would leak customer data through such a basic mistake...)
3. 教師ともあろう者が、生徒の前であんな発言をするとは呆れてしまう。
   (That someone who is supposed to be a teacher would say such a thing in front of students is exasperating.)
4. 国の代表ともあろう者が、あのような無責任な発言をするべきではない。
   (Someone representing the nation should not make such an irresponsible remark.)
5. 一流ホテルともあろうところが、こんな杜撰な対応をするとは思わなかった。
   (I never imagined that a place of such standing as a first-class hotel would offer such sloppy service.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Confused on graded tests with ならいざしらず, ならまだしも, and にかかって. The key nuance for students: ともあろう者が is specifically a criticism construction aimed at a SINGLE high-status subject failing to meet expectations tied to that status — it is not a two-case comparison (ならいざしらず/ならまだしも) and not about outcomes determined by someone's power or skill (にかかって). Look for a prestigious noun immediately followed by ともあろう + a role noun + が, paired with a clearly disapproving action.
