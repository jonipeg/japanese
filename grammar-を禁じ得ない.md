# ～を禁じ得ない {#を禁じ得ない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～を禁じ得ない

**Furigana form:** ～をきんじえない

**Meaning:** Facing a certain situation, one cannot suppress an emotion welling up inside — "cannot help but feel ~." Formal, written register.

**Conjugation:**
- 名 + を禁じ得ない
- 涙を禁じ得ない — "cannot hold back one's tears"

**Typical context:** Written, formal register — common in editorials, formal essays, and news commentary describing a strong emotional reaction to witnessing an event.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**～に堪えない**](grammar-に堪えない.md) — similar meaning of an overwhelming feeling, but describes an internal emotional state being too strong to contain, whereas 禁じ得ない emphasizes an emotion triggered involuntarily by witnessing something.
- [**～ずにはいられない**](grammar-ないでは・ずにはいられない.md) — broader pattern meaning "cannot help but (do an action)," not restricted to emotion nouns.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite pattern; remaining emotionally unmoved by something would simply be described without this expression.

**Example sentences:**
1. 生々しい戦争の傷跡を目の当たりにし、涙を禁じ得なかった。
   (Witnessing the raw scars of war firsthand, I couldn't hold back my tears.)
2. 犯人の供述を聞き、犯行動機の身勝手さに怒りを禁じ得なかった。
   (Hearing the culprit's testimony, I couldn't suppress my anger at the selfishness of the motive.)
3. かつてあんなに輝いていた彼が荒れた生活をしているのを見て、驚きを禁じ得なかった。
   (Seeing that he, who had once shined so brightly, was now living in disarray, I couldn't help but feel shocked.)
4. 報道で被害者の家族の様子を見て、同情を禁じ得なかった。
   (Seeing the victim's family in the news coverage, I couldn't help feeling sympathy.)
5. 彼の非常識な発言には、不快感を禁じ得ない。
   (I cannot suppress my displeasure at his senseless remarks.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern only attaches to nouns expressing emotion (涙・怒り・驚き・同情・不快感など) — it cannot be used with action verbs or neutral nouns. The subject is normally the speaker/writer (first person) and is frequently omitted, since it reports the writer's own reaction upon witnessing or learning of something.
