# ～ことなく {#ことなく}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ことなく

**Furigana form:** ～ことなく (no special kanji reading)

**Meaning:** "Without doing X" — describes performing an action while completely avoiding or never experiencing another action; formal equivalent of ～ないで.

**Conjugation:**
- Verb (dictionary form) + ことなく
- 休むことなく働き続けた (He kept working without resting)

**Typical context:** Written, formal/literary style — speeches, narration, news reports, biographies. Rare in casual conversation.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ないで** — plain, conversational equivalent ("without doing").
- **ずに** — also formal, interchangeable in most contexts with ことなく, but ずに attaches directly to the verb stem (ない→ず).
- **ことがなく** — closely related; used more for describing a state/experience rather than a manner of action.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct single opposite; contrast with ～て (doing X and then...) shows the included action instead of its absence.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は一度も諦めることなく、最後まで走り続けた。
   (He ran until the end without ever giving up.)
2. その研究者は休むことなく、何年も実験を続けた。
   (The researcher continued the experiments for years without resting.)
3. 誰にも気づかれることなく、彼は静かに去っていった。
   (He quietly left without anyone noticing.)
4. 彼女は迷うことなく、その道を選んだ。
   (She chose that path without hesitation.)
5. 困難な状況でも、彼は希望を失うことなく前進し続けた。
   (Even in difficult circumstances, he kept moving forward without losing hope.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** ことなく is more literary/formal than ないで/ずに and sounds stiff in casual speech. It typically modifies the main clause's manner of action, describing an ongoing negative condition that accompanies the main verb.
