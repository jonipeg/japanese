# ～に沿って {#に沿って}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に沿って

**Furigana form:** ～にそって

**Meaning:** "Along X" (physical: a river, road) or "In line with X" (abstract: a plan, policy, guideline) — indicates following a course, direction, or set of guidelines without deviation.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に沿って — 川に沿って (along the river); 方針に沿って (in line with the policy)
- Noun-modifying form: 名詞 + に沿う + 名詞 — 方針に沿う行動 (actions in line with the policy)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, common in both speech and writing — physical descriptions (roads/rivers) and business/policy contexts (plans, guidelines, requests).

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **に従って** — "in accordance with X," overlaps for rules/policies, but に従って can also express proportional change, which に沿って cannot.
- [**をもとに**](grammar-をもとに(して).md) — "based on X (as a foundation)," implies X as a starting material rather than a path/guideline to stay aligned with.
- **通りに** — "exactly as X," stricter and more literal adherence than に沿って, which allows general alignment.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～に反して ("contrary to X") is a direct opposite for the abstract "in line with" sense.

**Example sentences:**
1. この道は川に沿って続いている。
   (This road continues along the river.)
2. 会社の方針に沿って、新しい計画を立てた。
   (We drew up a new plan in line with the company's policy.)
3. お客様のご要望に沿った商品を提供したいと考えています。
   (We hope to offer products that are in line with our customers' requests.)
4. 海岸線に沿って、桜の木が植えられている。
   (Cherry trees are planted along the coastline.)
5. 政府の指針に沿って、対策が進められている。
   (Measures are being carried out in line with the government's guidelines.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に沿って implies staying within/following an established line or direction (physical path or abstract guideline) without deviating, whereas に従って can additionally express a proportional, gradual change — a nuance に沿って does not carry.
