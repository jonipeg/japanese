# ～にいたっても／にいたるまで {#にいたっても・にいたるまで}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**1. Kanji version:** ～に至っても／～に至るまで

**2. Furigana version:** ～にいたっても／～にいたるまで

**3. Meaning:** にいたっても: even reaching the extreme point of X (still the outcome is surprising/unchanged); にいたるまで: all the way up to and including X (emphasizing the full extent of a range).

**4. Conjugation (connection form):** 動詞辞書形／名詞 + に至る(って／るまで)

**5. Usage context:** Written, formal/literary register, often used for dramatic or emphatic extremes in essays and formal speeches.

**6. Similar grammar structures:**
- にかぎって — confirmed tested distractor; means "especially/only in the case of X [something bad happens]," unrelated to "reaching an extreme point."
- にそくして — confirmed tested distractor; means "in line with/based on," unrelated in meaning.
- にもまして — confirmed tested distractor; means "even more than X," a comparative intensifier, a different function from にいたる's "reaching an extreme/limit."
- をかわきりに — confirmed tested distractor (Q14, student wrongly picked this); means "starting with X [and a series follows]," marking the START of a sequence, the opposite orientation from にいたっても's marking of an END/extreme point.

**7. Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; をかわきりに (starting with X) functions as a loose structural opposite in terms of sequence position (start vs. extreme end).

**8. Example sentences:**
1. 妻が離婚を決意するに至って初めて夫は自分の非に気がついた。(It was only when his wife reached the point of deciding on divorce that the husband finally realized his own fault.)
2. 今回のテストはどれも悪かったが、数学に至っては5点しかとれなかった。(This test went badly across the board, but when it came to math, I only managed to score 5 points.)
3. 彼の無責任な行動は、ついに解雇に至るまでの事態を引き起こした。(His irresponsible behavior eventually escalated to the point of getting him fired.)
4. 交渉は決裂に至っても、双方とも譲る気配はなかった。(Even when the negotiations reached a total breakdown, neither side showed any sign of yielding.)
5. 子供から大人に至るまで、幅広い世代に支持されている作品だ。(This is a work supported by a wide range of generations, from children all the way up to adults.)

**9. Common mistakes / key nuances:** Confirmed student confusion (Q14): confused にいたっても with をかわきりに — をかわきりに marks the START of a sequence ("starting with X, then..."), while にいたっても marks reaching an EXTREME or final point ("even when it comes down to X..."). Also confirmed tested against several unrelated distractors (にかぎって, にそくして, にもまして) in Q3, indicating this is a heavily-drilled rote distinction cluster rather than a single pairwise confusion.
