# ～から見ると／～から言うと／～からすると {#から見ると・言うと・すると}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～から見ると／～から言うと／～からすると

**Furigana form:** ～からみると／～からいうと／～からすると

**Meaning:** "Judging from X / from the standpoint of X" — indicates the basis or evidence from which a conclusion or inference is drawn.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + から見ると／から言うと／からすると + 判断・推量の表現

**Typical context:** Written and spoken, neutral-to-formal register — common in opinions, analyses, and essays when stating the grounds for a judgment.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**にとって**](grammar-にとって.md) — "for X (from X's standpoint)," which focuses on impact or feeling for a person rather than evidentiary basis for a judgment.
- **から判断すると** — "judging from X," a more explicit, formal synonym often used in written analysis.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼の話し方から見ると、相当怒っているようだ。
   (Judging from the way he talks, he seems quite angry.)
2. 専門家の立場から言うと、この計画には無理がある。
   (From an expert's standpoint, this plan is unreasonable.)
3. 天気予報からすると、明日は晴れそうだ。
   (Judging from the weather forecast, it seems it will be sunny tomorrow.)
4. 彼女の表情から見ると、何か心配事があるらしい。
   (Judging from her expression, she seems to have some worry.)
5. 過去のデータから言うと、来月は売り上げが伸びるだろう。
   (Judging from past data, sales will likely increase next month.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** The three variants are largely interchangeable, but 見ると leans toward visually observed evidence, 言うと toward verbal/stated judgment, and すると is the most neutral/general of the three — learners often use them indiscriminately without noticing this subtle difference in evidence type.
