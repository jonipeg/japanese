# ～ともなると／ともなれば {#ともなると・ともなれば}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ともなると／ともなれば

**Furigana form:** ～ともなると／ともなれば

**Meaning:** "Once it reaches the stage/level of X, (then naturally...)" — expresses that once a situation reaches a certain significant status, age, scale, or role, a corresponding natural consequence follows.

**Conjugation:**
- [動-辞書形][名] + ともなると
- [動-辞書形][名] + ともなれば (interchangeable conditional variant)
- Example: 社会人ともなれば、自分の言葉に責任を持つべきだ。

**Typical context:** Formal/written register, commonly used in essays and formal speech discussing life stages, social roles, or significant milestones (becoming a working adult, a parent, a manager, a certain season, etc.). Politeness-neutral but stylistically elevated.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**となると**](grammar-となると.md) — "if it comes to X / when it's a matter of X," a broader conditional that doesn't necessarily imply reaching a notable stage or milestone — ともなると specifically frames X as a significant threshold.
- **にもなると** — a near-synonymous variant emphasizing reaching a certain degree/amount (年齢にもなると).
- **ぎみ** — unrelated; means "a tendency toward X" (風邪ぎみ), not a stage-based consequence.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 社会人ともなれば、自分の言葉に責任を持つべきだ。
   (Once you're a working member of society, you should take responsibility for your own words.)
2. 一流企業の社長ともなると、毎日のスケジュールは分刻みだ。
   (Once you're the president of a top-tier company, your daily schedule is planned down to the minute.)
3. 子どもも高校生ともなれば、親があれこれ口を出すべきではない。
   (Once a child is in high school, parents shouldn't meddle in every little thing.)
4. ベテラン選手ともなると、プレッシャーのかかる場面でも冷静でいられる。
   (Once you're a veteran athlete, you can stay calm even in high-pressure moments.)
5. 真冬ともなれば、この地方は雪で道路が通れなくなることも珍しくない。
   (Once it's the dead of winter, it's not unusual for roads in this region to become impassable due to snow.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** A margin note links this pattern to とはいえ in discussion flow, but とはいえ (≈ けれども, "that said / however") is a concessive connector with a completely different function — don't confuse the two just because they appear near each other in study notes. The defining feature of ともなると/ともなれば is the sense of "reaching a notable threshold," so if the sentence doesn't describe some kind of escalation in status, scale, or stage, a simpler conditional like となると is more appropriate.
