# ～につれて {#につれて}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に連れて

**Furigana form:** ～につれて

**Meaning:** "As X, Y (gradually)..." — describes two changes that progress in parallel, where one change accompanies or is caused by the other, both unfolding gradually over time.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形 (change/progress verb) + につれて (例: 年を取るにつれて)
- 名詞 (change-implying noun) + につれて (例: 時間の経過につれて)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, common in both written explanations (science, social commentary) and spoken descriptions of gradual change.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**にともなって**](grammar-にともなって.md) — "accompanying X," similar but often emphasizes cause-and-effect between a major event and its consequence, less purely gradual/parallel than につれて.
- [**とともに**](grammar-とともに.md) — "along with X," broader use (can mean simultaneous or gradual), less specifically about proportional progression.
- **に従って** — nearly interchangeable with につれて, slightly more formal and can also mean "in accordance with (rules)."

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 年を取るにつれて、体力が衰えていく。
   (As one gets older, physical strength gradually declines.)
2. 都市化が進むにつれて、自然環境が失われていった。
   (As urbanization progressed, the natural environment was gradually lost.)
3. 日が暮れるにつれて、気温が下がってきた。
   (As the sun set, the temperature gradually dropped.)
4. 経験を積むにつれて、仕事に自信が持てるようになった。
   (As I gained experience, I gradually became confident in my work.)
5. 技術が発展するにつれて、生活はますます便利になっている。
   (As technology develops, life is becoming increasingly convenient.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** につれて requires a verb or noun expressing change/progression on both sides (not a static fact) — you cannot use it for a single sudden event. It's distinct from に従って in that に従って can also mean "in accordance with a rule/instruction" (規則に従って), a meaning につれて does not share.
