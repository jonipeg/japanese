# ～にともなって {#にともなって}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に伴って

**Furigana form:** ～にともなって

**Meaning:** "Accompanying X / as a result of X" — indicates that one change or event occurs together with, or as a consequence of, another change, often on a larger/societal scale.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (change-implying noun) + に伴って (例: 人口の増加に伴って)
- 動詞辞書形 + のに伴って (例: 経済が発展するのに伴って)
- Attributive form: に伴う + 名詞 (例: 引っ越しに伴う手続き)

**Typical context:** Formal, mostly written — common in news reports, academic writing, and official announcements describing societal, economic, or technical changes.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**につれて**](grammar-につれて.md) — similar "as X, Y gradually," but につれて emphasizes proportional/gradual parallel change rather than cause-effect; にともなって can imply the second change is a direct consequence.
- [**とともに**](grammar-とともに.md) — "together with X," broader and can describe simultaneous events without implying causation.
- **の結果** — "as a result of X," more explicitly causal and less about gradual parallel progression.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 人口の増加に伴って、住宅不足が深刻化している。
   (As the population increases, the housing shortage is becoming more serious.)
2. 技術の進歩に伴って、私たちの生活は大きく変化した。
   (Accompanying technological progress, our lives have changed greatly.)
3. 台風の接近に伴い、電車の運行が中止された。
   (As the typhoon approached, train service was suspended.)
4. 社員数の増加に伴って、オフィスを拡張することになった。
   (As the number of employees increased, we decided to expand the office.)
5. 円安に伴って、輸入品の価格が上昇している。
   (Accompanying the weak yen, the prices of imported goods are rising.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にともなって is more formal and written than につれて, and is typically used for large-scale, societal, or official changes rather than personal gradual experiences (e.g., aging, gaining experience) — for those, につれて sounds more natural. The attributive form に伴う directly modifies a following noun (伴う手続き = "accompanying procedures").
