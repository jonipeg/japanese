# ～に堪える／～に堪えない {#に堪える・に堪えない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～に堪える／～に堪えない

**Furigana form:** ～にたえる／～にたえない

**Meaning:** に堪える = good enough to withstand being ~ed, i.e., "worthy of ~ing" (e.g. worthy of being appreciated/discussed); に堪えない = in such a poor or terrible state that it cannot bear being ~ed — "not worth ~ing," "unbearable to ~."

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形・名詞する + に堪える／に堪えない
- 鑑賞に堪える — "good enough to withstand being appreciated"

**Typical context:** Written, formal register — used in critical/evaluative writing about art, arguments, and quality, as well as in expressions of something being unbearable to watch, listen to, or read.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **に足る（にたる）** — very similar positive meaning, "worthy of ~," but used more broadly across many verbs/nouns.
- **見るに忍びない（みるにしのびない）** — close to the negative use of に堪えない, "too pitiful/painful to bear watching," but carries more emotional/sympathetic nuance.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct single opposite pattern; the affirmative と negative senses of に堪える itself form the contrast (worthy of vs. not worth / unbearable).

**Example sentences:**
1. 優れた児童文学は、大人の鑑賞にも堪えるものだ。
   (Excellent children's literature is good enough to withstand being appreciated by adults too.)
2. この説はまだ証拠が少なく、詳細な議論に堪えるものではない。
   (This theory still has too little evidence, and isn't solid enough to withstand detailed discussion.)
3. 人の悪口は聞くに堪えない。
   (Other people's badmouthing is unbearable to listen to.)
4. こういうスキャンダル記事は読むに堪えない。
   (This kind of scandal article is unbearable to read.)
5. 彼の演技は批評家の評価に堪えるレベルだった。
   (His acting was at a level that could withstand critics' scrutiny.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern only attaches naturally to a limited set of verbs/nouns related to perception and evaluation — 見る・聞く・鑑賞・批判・議論 and similar. Don't try to use it freely with arbitrary verbs. Note that に堪えない here (the "unbearable/not worthy" sense) is a different usage from the separate ～に堪えない pattern that attaches to emotion nouns (感激に堪えない, etc.) to mean "cannot suppress the feeling of ~."
