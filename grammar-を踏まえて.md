# ～を踏まえて {#を踏まえて}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～を踏まえて

**Furigana form:** ～をふまえて

**Meaning:** "Based on / taking X into account" — to do something while treating X (prior information, opinions, results, or experience) as a premise to build on.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + を踏まえて／を踏まえた＋名詞
- 現状を踏まえて — "taking the current situation into account"

**Typical context:** Formal written/spoken register — common in business proposals, academic writing, and planning contexts where prior data or opinions inform a decision.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**に基づいて**](grammar-に基づいて.md) — "based on X," emphasizes X as the foundation/grounds, often used with data, laws, or facts.
- **を考慮して** — "taking X into consideration," broader and can include subjective factors like feelings.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～を無視して ("ignoring X") describes proceeding without regard for prior information.

**Example sentences:**
1. 前回の反省点を踏まえて、次の企画案を練ろう。
   (Let's work out the next proposal, taking into account the points we reflected on last time.)
2. 消費者の意見を踏まえた商品開発をしていきたいと考えています。
   (We want to carry out product development that takes consumer opinions into account.)
3. 現状を踏まえて、計画を見直す必要がある。
   (We need to revise the plan, taking the current situation into account.)
4. これまでの経験を踏まえてアドバイスします。
   (I'll give advice based on my experience so far.)
5. 調査結果を踏まえて、新しい方針を決定した。
   (We decided on a new policy based on the survey results.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** を踏まえて implies building forward from existing information (feedback, results, experience) as a stepping stone, rather than strictly citing it as legal/logical grounds (which is closer to に基づいて). It frequently appears in business and academic contexts to show that a conclusion properly considers prior input.
