# ～限りでは {#限りでは}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～限りでは

**Furigana form:** ～かぎりでは

**Meaning:** "As far as ~ (within the limits of my knowledge/experience/information)" — used to hedge a statement by limiting it to what the speaker personally knows, has seen, or has heard.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形/た形 + 限りでは — 例: 私が知っている限りでは (as far as I know)
- 見る/聞く/調べる等の動詞 + 限りでは — 例: 調べた限りでは (as far as I've researched)

**Typical context:** Both spoken and written, neutral-to-formal — common when giving an opinion cautiously, in reports, reviews, and conversations where the speaker wants to avoid overstating certainty.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **によると** — "according to," cites an external source directly rather than the speaker's own limited perspective.
- [**わけではない**](grammar-わけではない.md) — denies a blanket statement, different function (not a hedge on personal knowledge).
- **と思われる** — "it seems/is thought that," a softer inference without tying it to the speaker's specific experience.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though 確実に言えることは (what can be said for certain) contrasts by expressing full confidence.

**Example sentences:**
1. 私が調べた限りでは、このレストランが一番評判がいい。
   (As far as I've researched, this restaurant has the best reputation.)
2. 聞いた限りでは、プロジェクトは順調に進んでいるようだ。
   (As far as I've heard, the project seems to be going smoothly.)
3. 私が知っている限りでは、彼はまだ結婚していない。
   (As far as I know, he isn't married yet.)
4. 資料を読んだ限りでは、特に問題はなさそうだ。
   (As far as I've read in the documents, there don't seem to be any particular problems.)
5. 見た限りでは、この商品は品質がとても良さそうだ。
   (As far as I can see, this product's quality looks very good.)
6. 経験した限りでは、この方法が一番効率的だ。
   (As far as my experience goes, this method is the most efficient.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 限りでは must be paired with a verb of perception/knowledge (知る, 聞く, 見る, 調べる, 読む), unlike に限り which attaches to nouns for restriction — learners sometimes misapply the noun-restriction pattern here.
