# ～かたわら {#かたわら}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～傍ら

**Furigana form:** ～かたわら

**Meaning:** "While also doing ~ / alongside ~" — indicates that someone carries out a secondary, ongoing activity (often a job, study, or hobby) in parallel with a main activity, usually over a long period of time.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形 (verb dictionary form) + かたわら
- 名詞の (noun + の) + かたわら
- Example: 会社員として働くかたわら、小説を書いている。 (While working as a company employee, he also writes novels.)

**Typical context:** Formal, written register — common in biographies, resumes, newspaper articles, and formal spoken introductions. Not used in casual conversation.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **一方で** — "on the other hand / while" — describes two contrasting or parallel situations, broader in scope, not limited to one person's two activities.
- [**つつ**](grammar-つつ.md) — "while doing" — connects two simultaneous actions by the same subject, but typically short-term/momentary actions rather than long-term parallel pursuits.
- [**ながら**](grammar-ながら.md) — "while doing" — the casual, general-purpose version for simultaneous actions, usable for both short and long-term activities but less formal.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though 専念する ("to devote oneself exclusively to one thing") contrasts in spirit.

**Example sentences:**
1. 会社員として働くかたわら、小説を書いている。
   (While working as a company employee, he also writes novels.)
2. 彼女は主婦業のかたわら、ボランティア活動にも力を入れている。
   (Alongside her housework, she also puts effort into volunteer activities.)
3. 大学で教えるかたわら、研究論文の執筆を続けている。
   (While teaching at university, he continues writing research papers.)
4. 彼は医者として働くかたわら、途上国での医療支援活動にも参加している。
   (While working as a doctor, he also participates in medical support activities in developing countries.)
5. 彼女は子育てのかたわら、オンラインで英語を教えている。
   (Alongside raising her children, she teaches English online.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners often confuse かたわら with ながら; かたわら is reserved for formal writing and implies a long-term, substantial secondary pursuit (like a career or serious hobby), not momentary simultaneous actions like 音楽を聞きながら勉強する.
