## 合わせ (あわせ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** combination, matching (noun/suffix)

**Similar words:**
- [組み合わせ](vocab-16.md#組み合わせ-くみあわせ)（くみあわせ）— "combination" in a more explicit, standalone noun sense, often used for lists of pairings/options.
- セット — a loanword "set," similar concept of items grouped together, more casual/commercial.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 色の合わせが絶妙だ。— The color combination/matching is exquisite.
2. 問い合わせの件でご連絡しました。— I'm contacting you regarding your inquiry (問い合わせ, a compound using 合わせ).
3. 待ち合わせの場所を決めよう。— Let's decide on the meeting place (待ち合わせ, another compound).

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 合わせ rarely stands fully alone in modern speech — it typically appears as the second half of compound nouns (問い合わせ "inquiry," 待ち合わせ "meeting up," 組み合わせ "combination") derived from the verb 合わせる.

## 合わせる (あわせる)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** to combine, put together, match, adjust (to align with something)

**Similar words:**
- 混ぜる（まぜる）— "to mix," implies blending substances together, whereas 合わせる is broader (combining, aligning, matching timing/opinions).
- 調整する（ちょうせいする）— "to adjust/coordinate," overlaps with the "adjust to align" sense of 合わせる (時間を合わせる) but is more formal/technical.
- 一致させる（いっちさせる）— "to make consistent/matching," overlaps with aligning opinions or schedules, more formal phrasing.

**Opposite words:**
- [分ける](vocab-36.md#分ける-わける)（わける）— to separate, divide

**Example sentences:**
1. 二人の意見を合わせて計画を立てた。— We combined our opinions and made a plan.
2. 時計の時間を合わせてください。— Please set/adjust the clock's time.
3. 色を合わせて選んだ服装だ。— This outfit was chosen by matching the colors.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 合わせる is extremely versatile — combining things, matching rhythm/timing (タイミングを合わせる), aligning opinions (意見を合わせる), or literally pressing palms together (手を合わせる) — context determines the specific nuance each time.

## 慌ただしい (あわただしい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** busy, hurried, hectic

**Similar words:**
- 忙しい（いそがしい）— general "busy," doesn't necessarily imply the frantic, rushed atmosphere that 慌ただしい conveys.
- せわしない — very close synonym, emphasizing restless busyness, almost interchangeable with 慌ただしい.
- バタバタする — a casual/onomatopoeic expression for being in a chaotic rush, more colloquial than 慌ただしい.

**Opposite words:**
- のんびり（のんびり）— leisurely, relaxed

**Example sentences:**
1. 年末は慌ただしい日々が続く。— Hectic days continue at the end of the year.
2. 慌ただしく出発の準備をした。— I hurriedly prepared for departure.
3. 慌ただしい毎日の中で少し休みたい。— I want to rest a little amid these hectic daily routines.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 慌ただしい emphasizes the atmosphere of rush/commotion (not just having a lot to do like 忙しい) — it's often used to describe a period or scene (年末の慌ただしさ) rather than a person's subjective feeling of being busy.

## 哀れ (あわれ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** pity, pitiful, miserable (noun/na-adjective)

**Similar words:**
- 可哀想（かわいそう）— the everyday casual word for "poor thing/pitiable," while 哀れ is more literary/formal and can also describe a deep, somber pathos.
- 悲惨（ひさん）— "tragic/miserable," emphasizes severity of suffering, often in disaster/tragedy contexts, stronger than 哀れ.
- 惨め（みじめ）— "wretched/miserable," focuses on the degrading, humiliating quality of a situation, overlapping with but more intense than 哀れ.

**Opposite words:**
- [幸福](vocab-19.md#幸福-こうふく)（こうふく）— happiness

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼の哀れな姿に涙が出た。— His pitiful appearance brought me to tears.
2. 哀れを誘う話だった。— It was a story that evoked pity.
3. 彼女は哀れにも一人で最期を迎えた。— Pitifully, she met her end alone.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 哀れ carries a literary, somewhat classical register (appearing in poetry, traditional literature, 物の哀れ "the pathos of things") — in casual speech, 可哀想 is far more common for expressing everyday sympathy.

## 案 (あん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a plan, proposal, idea

**Similar words:**
- [計画](vocab-17.md#計画する-けいかく)（けいかく）— "plan," more structured/detailed and often with concrete steps, whereas 案 can be a rougher, preliminary proposal/idea.
- 提案（ていあん）— "proposal," the act of proposing an 案; often 案 is the content and 提案する is the verb of presenting it.
- アイデア — a casual loanword for "idea," less formal/structured than the more business-oriented 案.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 新しい案を考えてみた。— I came up with a new proposal.
2. この案に賛成の人は手を挙げてください。— Those in favor of this proposal, please raise your hand.
3. いくつかの案の中から一つを選んだ。— I chose one from among several proposals.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 案 commonly appears in compounds (提案、案内、議案、予算案) — as a standalone word it means a tentative plan/proposal still open for discussion, not a finalized decision.

## 安易 (あんい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** easy, easygoing, facile (often with a negative nuance of being too simplistic)

**Similar words:**
- 簡単（かんたん）— neutral "easy/simple," without the critical nuance that 安易 often carries (implying something is done carelessly or without enough thought).
- 気軽（きがる）— "lighthearted/casual," positive connotation for approaching something without undue stress, unlike the often-critical 安易.
- 手軽（てがる）— "convenient/easy to do," neutral-to-positive, often used for simple methods or products, contrasting with the negative judgment in 安易.

**Opposite words:**
- [慎重](vocab-21.md#慎重-しんちょう)（しんちょう）— careful, prudent

**Example sentences:**
1. 安易な解決策に頼るべきではない。— One shouldn't rely on an easy/facile solution.
2. 安易に人を信じるのは危険だ。— Trusting people too easily/readily is dangerous.
3. 安易な考えで始めた事業は失敗しやすい。— Businesses started with a facile mindset tend to fail.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 安易 almost always carries a critical nuance of "too easy/thoughtless," unlike neutral 簡単 — using 安易 to simply mean "this task is easy" without any criticism would sound off; reserve it for judging a choice or approach as insufficiently careful.

## 案外 (あんがい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** unexpectedly, surprisingly (contrary to expectation)

**Similar words:**
- 意外（いがい）— very close synonym "unexpected/surprising," largely interchangeable with 案外 in many contexts, though 意外 can also function more as a na-adjective describing surprise itself.
- 思ったより（おもったより）— "more than expected," a more explicit comparative phrase, less idiomatic than 案外.
- 存外（ぞんがい）— a slightly more formal/literary synonym for 案外.

**Opposite words:**
- 予想通り（よそうどおり）— as expected

**Example sentences:**
1. この問題は案外簡単だった。— This problem was surprisingly easy.
2. 彼は案外優しい人だった。— He turned out to be unexpectedly kind.
3. 案外早く終わってよかった。— I'm glad it finished unexpectedly quickly.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 案外 is typically used adverbially before an adjective/verb (案外簡単, 案外早く) to express that reality differed from expectation — it's very close to 意外 but slightly more colloquial/common in everyday conversation.

## 暗記 (あんき)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** memorization, learning by heart

**Similar words:**
- [記憶](vocab-02.md#記憶-きおく)（きおく）— "memory" in general (the faculty or the act of remembering), broader than the specific deliberate act of memorizing implied by 暗記.
- 覚える（おぼえる）— the common verb "to memorize/learn," less formal/noun-like than 暗記 which is often used as 暗記する in a more academic/study context.
- 丸暗記（まるあんき）— "rote memorization," an intensified form emphasizing memorizing without understanding.

**Opposite words:**
- 忘却（ぼうきゃく）— forgetting, oblivion

**Example sentences:**
1. 単語を暗記するのが苦手だ。— I'm not good at memorizing vocabulary.
2. 試験のために歴史年表を暗記した。— I memorized the historical timeline for the exam.
3. 丸暗記ではなく理解して覚えるべきだ。— One should learn by understanding rather than rote memorization.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 暗記 specifically implies deliberate, often academic, memorization (for tests, speeches, scripts) — for casual "I remember" (recalling something naturally), 覚えている is the more natural phrase, not 暗記している.

## アンケート (アンケート)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a questionnaire, survey

**Similar words:**
- [調査](vocab-27.md#調査-ちょうさ)（ちょうさ）— "survey/investigation" in a broader sense, which may or may not use a written questionnaire; アンケート specifically refers to the written/form-based survey tool.
- 世論調査（よろんちょうさ）— "public opinion poll," a specific formal type of survey, often conducted via アンケート or interviews.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 顧客満足度についてアンケートを実施した。— We conducted a questionnaire on customer satisfaction.
2. アンケートにご協力ください。— Please cooperate with (fill out) this questionnaire.
3. アンケート結果を分析してレポートを作成した。— I analyzed the survey results and created a report.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** アンケート comes from French "enquête" but in Japanese specifically means a questionnaire/survey form, not a general "inquiry" or "investigation" (which would be 調査) — don't confuse it with the English "inquiry."

## アンコール (アンコール)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** an encore

**Similar words:**
- 再演（さいえん）— "re-performance/revival," refers to restaging an entire show again later, distinct from the immediate "one more song" request of アンコール.
- カーテンコール — "curtain call," the related but distinct event of performers returning to bow, sometimes preceding an アンコール.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 観客はアンコールを求めて拍手を続けた。— The audience kept clapping, calling for an encore.
2. バンドはアンコールに応えてもう一曲演奏した。— The band responded to the encore by playing one more song.
3. アンコールの声が鳴り止まなかった。— The calls for an encore wouldn't stop.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** アンコール comes from French and is used almost exclusively for live music/theater performance contexts, often with 〜を求める (to call for an encore) or アンコールに応える (to respond to an encore) as set collocations.

## 暗殺 (あんさつ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** assassination

**Similar words:**
- 殺人（さつじん）— general "murder," broader and not specifically politically/socially motivated like 暗殺, which targets prominent figures.
- テロ — "terrorism," can overlap when an assassination is part of a terrorist act, but テロ is broader and can target groups/property too.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 大統領が暗殺される事件が起きた。— An incident occurred in which the president was assassinated.
2. 歴史上、多くの指導者が暗殺されてきた。— Throughout history, many leaders have been assassinated.
3. 暗殺計画が未然に防がれた。— The assassination plot was foiled before it happened.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 暗殺 specifically refers to the secretive, politically/socially motivated killing of a prominent person (leader, public figure) — it's not used for ordinary murder of a private individual, which would simply be 殺人.

## 暗算 (あんざん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** mental arithmetic, calculating in one's head

**Similar words:**
- 計算（けいさん）— general "calculation," which may be done with pen/paper or a calculator, unlike 暗算 which specifically means doing it without any aid, in one's head.
- 筆算（ひっさん）— "written/paper calculation," the direct contrast to 暗算 (mental calculation).

**Opposite words:**
- 筆算（ひっさん）— calculation using paper and pen

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は暗算が得意だ。— He's good at mental arithmetic.
2. この程度の計算なら暗算でできる。— I can do a calculation of this level mentally.
3. そろばんを習うと暗算力が上がる。— Learning the abacus improves one's mental calculation ability.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 暗算 specifically contrasts with calculating using tools (paper, calculator, abacus) — it emphasizes the "no external aid" aspect, often linked to abacus (そろばん) training in Japan as a traditional skill.

## 暗示 (あんじ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a suggestion, hint, implication

**Similar words:**
- [示唆](vocab-03.md#示唆-しさ)（しさ）— "suggestion/implication," a more formal/academic synonym, often used in writing and analysis.
- ヒント — a casual loanword "hint," much lighter/more playful than the more serious or psychological connotation 暗示 can carry (e.g., self-suggestion, hypnosis).
- [暗に](#暗に-あんに)（あんに）— the related adverb "implicitly," describing how something is suggested rather than the suggestion itself.

**Opposite words:**
- 明言（めいげん）— explicit statement, clear declaration

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼の言葉には暗示が含まれていた。— His words contained an implication.
2. 自己暗示をかけて緊張をほぐした。— I used self-suggestion to ease my nervousness.
3. その映画の結末は暗示に富んでいた。— The movie's ending was rich with suggestion/implication.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 暗示 has a specific psychological usage (自己暗示 "self-suggestion," 催眠暗示 "hypnotic suggestion") beyond the general "hint" meaning — it's more formal/technical than casual ヒント.

## 安心 (あんしん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** relief, peace of mind; to feel relieved/reassured

**Similar words:**
- 安堵（あんど）— "relief," slightly more literary/formal, often describing the specific moment of relief after resolved tension, while 安心 is more general ongoing peace of mind.
- 安らぎ（やすらぎ）— "tranquility/peace," a calmer, more emotional/spiritual sense than the practical reassurance of 安心.
- [油断](vocab-35.md#油断-ゆだん)（ゆだん）— "carelessness/letting one's guard down," a related but distinct concept sometimes warned against alongside 安心 (安心しすぎて油断する).

**Opposite words:**
- [不安](vocab-32.md#不安-ふあん)（ふあん）— anxiety, unease

**Example sentences:**
1. 無事に着いたと聞いて安心した。— I felt relieved to hear you arrived safely.
2. 安心して任せてください。— Please rest assured and leave it to me.
3. 保険に入っていると安心だ。— Having insurance gives peace of mind.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 安心 is a する-verb noun (安心する = to feel relieved), and is often used in the causative/request form 安心してください ("please be at ease/don't worry") — don't confuse it with 安全 (safety, a state of being free from danger), which describes an objective condition rather than a feeling.

## 安静 (あんせい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** rest, quiet (especially medical recuperation)

**Similar words:**
- [休養](vocab-15.md#休養-きゅうよう)（きゅうよう）— "rest and recuperation" generally, broader and not necessarily medically prescribed like 安静.
- 静養（せいよう）— "recuperating in quiet," very close synonym, often used interchangeably, slightly more about recovering from illness in a peaceful environment.
- 安静にする — the common collocation meaning "to stay still/rest" as medically instructed.

**Opposite words:**
- 安静にする（あんせいにする）— N/A (self-reference); 活動的（かつどうてき）— active

**Example sentences:**
1. 医者に安静にするよう言われた。— The doctor told me to rest/stay still.
2. 手術後はしばらく安静が必要だ。— After surgery, a period of rest is necessary.
3. 絶対安静の状態が続いている。— A state of complete bed rest continues.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 安静 is specifically a medical/health term implying physically refraining from activity to recover (often 安静にする, 絶対安静 "complete bed rest") — it's not used for general relaxation or vacation-style rest, which would be 休養 or 休憩.

## 安定 (あんてい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** stability

**Similar words:**
- [安心](#安心-あんしん)（あんしん）— "peace of mind," a subjective feeling, distinct from 安定 which describes an objective state of being steady/unchanging.
- 固定（こてい）— "fixed/fastened," more about physical immobility than the broader conceptual steadiness of 安定.
- [バランス](vocab-02.md#バランス) — "balance," overlaps when describing equilibrium, but 安定 specifically emphasizes a lack of fluctuation over time.

**Opposite words:**
- 不安定（ふあんてい）— instability

**Example sentences:**
1. 経済の安定を目指して政策を進めている。— Policies are being advanced aiming for economic stability.
2. 彼は安定した収入を得ている。— He earns a stable income.
3. 椅子の脚が安定していないので危ない。— The chair's legs aren't stable, so it's dangerous.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 安定 applies broadly to economics, emotions, physical objects, and relationships (安定した関係 = "a stable relationship") — it's a na-adjective/noun (安定した, 安定している), not to be confused with the feeling-based 安心.

## 案内 (あんない)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** guidance, information, invitation

**Similar words:**
- [ガイド](vocab-11.md#ガイド) — a loanword for "guide" (person or service), often used for tourism specifically, while 案内 is broader (includes written information, directions, invitations).
- 招待（しょうたい）— "invitation," specifically the act of inviting someone to an event, a narrower sense than the general-purpose 案内.
- [誘導](vocab-35.md#誘導-ゆうどう)（ゆうどう）— "guidance/leading (physically)," more mechanical/procedural, e.g., guiding traffic or crowds, versus the more general helpfulness of 案内.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 駅まで案内してもらった。— I was guided to the station.
2. 結婚式の案内状が届いた。— The wedding invitation arrived.
3. 観光案内所で地図をもらった。— I got a map at the tourist information center.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 案内 covers physically guiding someone, providing informational guidance, and sending invitations (案内状) — it's one of the most versatile words in this set, so context (案内する人/案内所/案内状) clarifies which sense applies.

## 暗に (あんに)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** implicitly, indirectly, secretly (suggesting something without stating directly)

**Similar words:**
- それとなく — "indirectly/tactfully," very close synonym, slightly more casual/conversational than 暗に.
- 遠回しに（とおまわしに）— "in a roundabout way," emphasizes the indirect phrasing/approach more than the hidden intent.
- 暗示的に（あんじてきに）— "suggestively," more formal/academic phrasing related to 暗示.

**Opposite words:**
- 明確に（めいかくに）— clearly, explicitly

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は暗に辞職をほのめかした。— He implicitly hinted at resigning.
2. 暗に非難されているような気がした。— I felt as though I was being implicitly criticized.
3. その発言は暗に彼を批判するものだった。— That remark was an implicit criticism of him.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 暗に modifies verbs of communication (ほのめかす, 示す, 批判する) to indicate the message was conveyed indirectly/without stating it outright — it's an adverb, not standalone, and typically pairs with verbs of suggesting or hinting.

## 案の定 (あんのじょう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** as expected, sure enough (often regarding an anticipated negative outcome)

**Similar words:**
- 思った通り（おもったとおり）— "just as I thought," a more neutral/plain phrase without the dramatic "told-you-so" flavor of 案の定.
- やっぱり — casual "as I thought/after all," very commonly used in speech, similar nuance but more conversational than the slightly more literary 案の定.
- 予想通り（よそうどおり）— "as predicted," more neutral/formal, usable for both positive and negative expected outcomes.

**Opposite words:**
- 意外にも（いがいにも）— unexpectedly

**Example sentences:**
1. 案の定、彼は遅刻してきた。— Sure enough, he showed up late.
2. 案の定、計画は失敗に終わった。— As expected, the plan ended in failure.
3. 天気予報通り、案の定雨が降り出した。— Just as the weather forecast said, it started raining as expected.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 案の定 typically carries a nuance of "I knew this bad/undesirable thing would happen" — it's usually used for outcomes the speaker anticipated with some worry or skepticism, not neutral/positive surprises.

## 安否 (あんぴ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** safety, well-being (of a person, especially after a disaster or during separation)

**Similar words:**
- 無事（ぶじ）— "safe and sound," describes the confirmed state of being okay, while 安否 refers to the unknown status being inquired about or confirmed.
- [健康](vocab-18.md#健康-けんこう)（けんこう）— "health," focuses on physical wellness rather than the broader "are they safe/alive" concern of 安否.
- 生死（せいし）— "life or death," a more extreme/urgent framing than the general concern of 安否.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 地震の後、家族の安否が確認できず心配した。— After the earthquake, I was worried because I couldn't confirm my family's safety.
2. 安否確認の電話をかけた。— I made a phone call to check on their safety/well-being.
3. 行方不明者の安否が気遣われている。— Concerns are being raised about the well-being of the missing person.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 安否 is almost always used in the context of uncertainty after a disaster, accident, or during travel/separation (安否確認 "safety confirmation," 安否が気遣われる) — it's not used for routine, everyday health inquiries.

#vocabulary
#to-review

## 粗筋 (あらすじ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** an outline, summary, synopsis (of a story)

**Similar words:**
- 要約（ようやく）— "summary," broader and applicable to any text (reports, articles), not specifically narrative works like 粗筋.
- [概要](vocab-11.md#概要-がいよう)（がいよう）— "overview/outline," more formal/general, often used for reports or plans rather than stories specifically.
- ダイジェスト — a loanword "digest," a condensed version, overlapping with 粗筋 for stories but often implying a more complete abridged retelling.

**Opposite words:**
- 詳細（しょうさい）— details, particulars

**Example sentences:**
1. この映画の粗筋を教えてください。— Please tell me the synopsis of this movie.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 粗筋 specifically refers to summaries of stories/narratives (novels, movies, dramas) — for summarizing non-narrative content like a business report, 要約 or 概要 is more appropriate.

**Example sentences:**

2. いっそのこと、全部話してしまったほうがいい。— It would be better to just tell everything, rather than hold back.
3. こんなに苦しいなら、いっそ休んでしまいたい。— If it's this painful, I'd rather just take a break entirely.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** いっそ (often as いっそのこと) suggests choosing a more drastic/extreme option as actually preferable to a frustrating middle ground — it implies resignation or a decisive "might as well" attitude, not a simple preference comparison like むしろ.

## 一層 (いっそう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** still more, all the more, even more so

**Similar words:**
- もっと — a simple, casual "more," without 一層's slightly more formal, intensifying nuance (often used with comparative change already in progress).
- ますます — "more and more," similar progressive-intensification nuance, often interchangeable with 一層 in many contexts.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 今後は一層努力するつもりだ。— I intend to work even harder from now on.
2. 雨が降り出し、寒さが一層厳しくなった。— As the rain started, the cold became even more severe.
3. 両者の関係は一層深まった。— The relationship between the two deepened all the more.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 一層 is more formal/written than もっと and often appears in set phrases like 一層努力する (to make even greater effort) — it typically modifies an already-present degree/trend, emphasizing intensification rather than introducing a new comparison from scratch.

## 一体 (いったい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** on earth, in the world, whatever (emphatic question word); also: one body, unity

**Similar words:**
- 一体全体（いったいぜんたい）— an emphatic extension of 一体, used for even stronger rhetorical emphasis in questions.
- 全く（まったく）— "completely, totally," can express exasperation similarly but isn't tied to question words like 一体 is.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 一体 has two very different uses: as an emphatic adverb before question words (一体何, 一体どこ — "what/where on earth") expressing frustration/confusion, and as a noun meaning "one body/unity" (一体となる, to become one) — context makes clear which sense applies.

## 一旦 (いったん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** once, for a moment, temporarily (as a first step before something else)

**Similar words:**
- 一度（いちど）— "once," simply counts an occurrence; 一旦 implies a temporary pause/completion of one step before moving to a next action.
- とりあえず — "for now, for the time being," similar temporary/provisional nuance, often interchangeable in casual speech.

- None notable

3. 会議を一旦中断しましょう。— Let's pause the meeting for a moment.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 一旦 emphasizes a temporary break or completed first step in a sequence (一旦〜してから, "once you've done X, then...") — it's not simply "once" in the sense of frequency (一度), but about a provisional pause/transition point in a process.

## 一致 (いっち)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** agreement, accord, coincidence (matching/corresponding)

**Similar words:**
- 合意（ごうい）— "mutual agreement" reached through discussion/negotiation, more about consensus-building; 一致 is broader and can describe facts/data/opinions simply aligning without negotiation.
- 賛成（さんせい）— "approval/agreement," specifically about agreeing with a proposal, narrower than the general matching sense of 一致.

**Opposite words:**
- [相違](vocab-23.md#相違-そうい)（そうい）— discrepancy, difference

**Example sentences:**
1. 二人の意見が一致した。— The two people's opinions aligned.
2. 指紋が一致したため、犯人が特定された。— Because the fingerprints matched, the culprit was identified.
3. 彼らの証言は一致していない。— Their testimonies don't match.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 一致 covers both abstract agreement (opinions matching) and literal/forensic matching (data, fingerprints, numbers) — this dual usage is broader than English "agreement," which usually implies people as the subject.

## 一定 (いってい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** fixed, definite, set, constant; to fix/standardize

**Similar words:**
- 固定（こてい）— "fixed/fastened in place," more literal/physical; 一定 is broader, often describing a consistent standard, amount, or rule.
- 一様（いちよう）— "uniform," emphasizes sameness across instances, while 一定 emphasizes a fixed, unchanging standard or quantity.

**Opposite words:**
- 不定（ふてい）— indefinite, unfixed

**Example sentences:**
1. 一定の条件を満たせば申請できる。— You can apply if you meet certain fixed conditions.
2. 気温が一定に保たれている。— The temperature is kept constant.
3. 収入が一定しないので不安だ。— I feel uneasy because my income isn't stable/fixed.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 一定 frequently appears as 一定の〜 (a certain/fixed amount of ~, e.g., 一定の基準, "a set standard") — this usage as "a certain degree/amount of" is a common collocation that doesn't map directly onto a single English word.
