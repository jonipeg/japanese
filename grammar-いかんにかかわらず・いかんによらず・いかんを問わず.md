# ～いかんにかかわらず／～いかんによらず／～いかんを問わず {#いかんにかかわらず・いかんによらず・いかんを問わず}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～いかんにかかわらず／～いかんによらず／～いかんを問わず

**Furigana form:** ～いかんにかかわらず／～いかんによらず／～いかんをとわず

**Meaning:** "Regardless of ~" / "No matter what ~ may be" — states that the outcome or situation in question is not affected or influenced by how some variable factor (content, result, condition, etc.) turns out. A formal, stiff expression typically found in written notices, rules, and announcements.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞（＋の）＋いかんにかかわらず／いかんによらず
- 名詞（＋の）＋いかんを問わず
- 内容のいかんにかかわらず — "regardless of the content"
- 結果いかんによらず — "regardless of the result" (の is sometimes dropped after certain nouns)

**Typical context:** Formal written register — common in official announcements, company policies, contracts, and public notices (e.g. "regardless of academic background, age, or experience"). Rarely used in casual speech.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**～を問わず**](grammar-を問わず.md) — "regardless of/not distinguishing between ~," very close in meaning and often interchangeable with ～いかんを問わず, but attaches more broadly (including to binary pairs like 男女を問わず) without needing a noun expressing "variability."
- **～に関わらず** — "regardless of ~," a more general and slightly less formal synonym; いかんにかかわらず is a stiffer, more literary variant built on the noun いかん ("how things turn out").
- **～によらず** alone — "irrespective of ~," can be used more broadly without いかん, e.g. 理由によらず.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a structure like ～次第で ("depending on ~") expresses the reverse idea — that the outcome does change depending on the factor in question.

**Example sentences:**
1. 内容のいかんにかかわらず、個人情報の問い合わせにはお答えしておりません。
   (Regardless of the content, we do not respond to inquiries about personal information.)
2. 明日の試合の結果いかんによらず、優勝できないことは決まってしまった。
   (Regardless of the outcome of tomorrow's match, it's already settled that we can't win the championship.)
3. 当社は学歴・年齢・過去の実績のいかんを問わず、初任給は一律です。
   (At our company, starting salary is the same for everyone, regardless of educational background, age, or past achievements.)
4. 理由のいかんにかかわらず、遅刻は遅刻として扱われます。
   (Regardless of the reason, being late is treated as being late.)
5. 天候のいかんによらず、イベントは予定通り開催されます。
   (Regardless of the weather, the event will be held as scheduled.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern only attaches to nouns describing something with inherent variability or a range of possible outcomes (content, result, academic background, weather, etc.) — it does not attach to simple yes/no or fixed binary pairs the way ～を問わず can. The clause that follows must state that the preceding factor has no bearing on the result; it cannot be followed by a sentence showing the factor actually mattered.
