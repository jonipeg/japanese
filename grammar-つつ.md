# ～つつ {#つつ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～つつ

**Furigana form:** ～つつ

**Meaning:** Two related uses: (1) "while doing X" — two actions happening simultaneously by the same subject; (2) "although/even while X, Y" — a concessive sense where the main clause contradicts or qualifies what the つつ-clause would suggest.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞ます形 + つつ (verb stem + つつ)
- Example base: 歩く → 歩きつつ話す；わかる → わかりつつ（も）

**Typical context:** Formal, written/spoken register; more literary and composed than ながら, common in essays, speeches, and formal narration. Not used in casual conversation, where ながら is preferred for the simultaneous-action sense.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**ながら**](grammar-ながら.md) — "while doing X"; the everyday, casual-to-neutral equivalent for simultaneous action, usable in any register, whereas つつ is reserved for more formal/written contexts.
- **つつも** — an emphasized variant that specifically highlights the concessive sense ("even while knowing X, still Y"), used when つつ's contrastive meaning needs to be made unambiguous.
- **ながらも** — "while/even though X, nonetheless Y"; the concessive counterpart built on ながら, nearly synonymous with つつも but slightly more common in modern speech.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a simple sequential connector (～てから、"after doing X") would contrast with つつ's emphasis on simultaneity rather than sequence.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は景色を眺めつつ、故郷のことを思い出していた。 (Gazing at the scenery, he was recalling thoughts of his hometown.)
2. 体に悪いとわかりつつ、つい夜食を食べてしまう。 (Even knowing it's bad for my health, I end up eating a late-night snack anyway.)
3. 彼女は笑顔を見せつつも、内心は不安でいっぱいだった。 (Even while showing a smiling face, inside she was filled with anxiety.)
4. 経済は回復しつつある。 (The economy is in the process of recovering.)
5. 彼は反省しつつ、同じ過ちを繰り返した。 (Even while reflecting on his mistake, he repeated the same error.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** つつ has two distinct senses that learners often conflate: the neutral simultaneous-action sense (closer to ながら) and the concessive "even though/despite" sense (closer to のに) — context (especially a following clause that contradicts expectation, or the presence of も) usually signals which is meant. Also note the special pattern ～つつある, meaning "is in the process of becoming/doing X" (経済は回復しつつある), which is a distinct fixed usage describing an ongoing gradual change, not a simultaneous dual action.
