# ～おかげで {#おかげで}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～お陰で

**Furigana form:** ～おかげで

**Meaning:** "Thanks to X" — attributes a good result to a positive cause or helper. Can also be used ironically to sarcastically blame something for a bad result ("thanks a lot to X...").

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + の + おかげで
- 動詞普通形（た形／る形）+ おかげで
- Sentence-final: ～おかげだ／～おかげか

**Typical context:** Spoken and written, neutral-to-polite register — extremely common in everyday conversation, speeches of gratitude, and writing. The fixed polite greeting おかげさまで ("thanks to you/everyone") is used even without specifying a cause.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ため（に）** — neutral cause-and-effect connector that works for both good and bad outcomes, without おかげで's built-in positive framing.
- [**せいで**](grammar-せいで.md) — the negative-outcome counterpart, "because of X (bad result)" — directly opposite in polarity to おかげで's normal usage.

**Opposite grammar structures:** [**せいで**](grammar-せいで.md) — blames a negative outcome on a cause, the mirror image of おかげで's usual positive framing.

**Example sentences:**
1. 先生のおかげで、試験に合格しました。
   (Thanks to my teacher, I passed the exam.)
2. 友達が手伝ってくれたおかげで、早く終わった。
   (Thanks to my friend helping, I finished early.)
3. 彼のアドバイスのおかげで、問題が解決した。
   (Thanks to his advice, the problem was solved.)
4. 毎日雨が降るおかげで、全然出かけられない。
   (Thanks a lot to the daily rain, I can't go out at all.)
5. 丈夫な体のおかげで、病気一つしたことがない。
   (Thanks to my strong body, I've never once been sick.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Using おかげで for a plainly bad outcome without intending sarcasm sounds odd — use せいで instead unless irony is intended. おかげさまで is a fixed polite set phrase used as a greeting response, distinct from the full おかげで construction.
