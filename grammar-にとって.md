# ～にとって {#にとって}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～にとって

**Furigana form:** ～にとって

**Meaning:** "For X / from X's standpoint" — evaluates a situation, object, or event from the perspective of a particular person or entity, usually followed by a judgment (good/bad/important/difficult, etc.).

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (person/entity) + にとって (例: 私にとって、学生にとって、日本にとって)
- Attributive form: にとっての + 名詞 (例: 私にとっての幸せ)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, used widely in both speech and writing whenever giving a subjective evaluation tied to a specific standpoint.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**に対して**](grammar-に対して.md) — marks a target of action/attitude, not an evaluative standpoint; different function despite superficial similarity.
- **の立場から見ると** — "from the standpoint of X," more explicit/formal paraphrase of the same idea.
- **には** — can sometimes substitute loosely in casual speech (私には難しい) but is less precise about "standpoint."

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. この試験は私にとって非常に重要だ。
   (This exam is extremely important to me.)
2. 子供にとって、遊びは学びの一部である。
   (For children, play is part of learning.)
3. 彼にとって、仕事よりも家族のほうが大切だ。
   (For him, family is more important than work.)
4. この決断は会社にとって大きな転機となった。
   (This decision became a major turning point for the company.)
5. 外国人にとって、日本語の敬語は難しいと言われる。
   (It's said that honorific Japanese is difficult for foreigners.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にとって must be followed by an evaluative or judgmental statement (important, difficult, good, meaningful, etc.), not a simple action performed by that person — "私にとって勉強する" is incorrect; use には or が instead for simple actions. Don't confuse with に対して, which marks the target of an attitude/action rather than an evaluative perspective.
