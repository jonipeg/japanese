# ～ばかりだ {#ばかりだ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ばかりだ

**Furigana form:** ～ばかりだ

**Meaning:** "All that's left is to ~ / nothing remains but to ~" — indicates that all preparations are complete and only one final step or event remains. Can also mean a situation is continuously progressing in one direction (e.g., worsening).

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形 (verb dictionary form) + ばかりだ
- Example: パーティーの準備は全て整いました。あとは客の到着を待つばかりです。 (All the preparations for the party are complete. All that's left now is to wait for the guests to arrive.)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal spoken and written Japanese; common in announcements, reports, and formal descriptions of a process nearing completion.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**一方だ**](grammar-一方(で)・一方だ.md) — "continuously ~ (in one direction)" — overlaps with the "ongoing trend" sense of ばかりだ (悪化するばかりだ vs 悪化する一方だ), but 一方だ is used only for that trend meaning, not the "final step remaining" meaning.
- **だけだ** — "it's just ~" — can express a similar "only ~ remains" idea but lacks the nuance of a completed process leading up to the final step.
- **のみだ** — a more formal/written equivalent of ～ばかりだ in the "nothing left but to ~" sense.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though まだ始まったばかりだ ("it has only just begun") contrasts in spirit by indicating the beginning rather than the final stage.

**Example sentences:**
1. パーティーの準備は全て整いました。あとは客の到着を待つばかりです。
   (All the preparations for the party are complete. All that's left now is to wait for the guests to arrive.)
2. 論文はほぼ書き終え、あとは先生に提出するばかりだ。
   (The thesis is almost entirely written; all that remains is to submit it to the professor.)
3. 引っ越しの荷造りも終わり、あとはトラックが来るのを待つばかりだ。
   (The packing for the move is finished too; all that's left is to wait for the truck to arrive.)
4. 面接の準備は万全で、あとは本番に臨むばかりだ。
   (The interview preparation is perfect; all that's left is to face the real thing.)
5. 景気は悪化するばかりで、回復の兆しが見えない。
   (The economy just keeps getting worse, with no sign of recovery.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** ばかりだ has two distinct uses — "only the final step remains" (completed preparations) and "a situation keeps progressing in one direction" (悪化するばかり). Learners sometimes conflate this with ～たばかり ("just finished doing ~"), which is an unrelated pattern marking recency of a past action.
