# ～ことか {#ことか}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ことか

**Furigana form:** ～ことか (no special kanji reading)

**Meaning:** "How (much)...!" — a rhetorical exclamatory expression used with question words like 何度, どんなに, どれほど to emphasize the high degree of an emotion or situation, without expecting an actual answer.

**Conjugation:**
- 何度／どんなに／どれほど + Verb/い-adj (plain form) + ことか
- 何度言ったことか (How many times have I said it!)
- どんなに嬉しかったことか (How happy I was!)

**Typical context:** Mostly spoken and semi-formal writing (essays, letters, narration); conveys strong emotional emphasis — frustration, nostalgia, joy, worry.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ことだろう** — similar exclamatory nuance, slightly more literary/speculative ("how... it must be").
- **なんと～だろう** — a more classic exclamatory construction using なんと.
- [**ものか**](grammar-ものか・もんか.md) — different nuance; expresses strong denial/refusal ("as if I would!").

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a flat declarative statement without ことか simply states the fact without emotional emphasis.

**Example sentences:**
1. 何度注意したことか、彼はまだ遅刻してくる。
   (How many times have I warned him, and yet he still comes late!)
2. あの時、どんなに心配したことか。
   (How worried I was at that time!)
3. 子供が無事に生まれて、どれほど嬉しかったことか。
   (How happy I was when the baby was born safely!)
4. 一人で海外生活をして、どれほど苦労したことか。
   (How much I struggled living abroad alone!)
5. 彼女がどれだけ努力してきたことか、誰も知らない。
   (No one knows just how much effort she has put in!)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners sometimes try to answer the rhetorical question literally — but ことか is not a real question, it's an exclamation of degree. Must be paired with a degree word (何度, どんなに, どれほど, どれだけ) for the sentence to make sense.
