# ～に応じて {#に応じて}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に応じて

**Furigana form:** ～におうじて

**Meaning:** "According to," "in response to," "depending on" — indicates that an action, amount, or treatment varies or is adjusted to match a given condition, situation, or request.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に応じて
- 名詞 + に応じた + 名詞 (attributive form): 収入に応じた税金 (taxes adjusted according to income)
- 収入に応じて、税金が変わる。(Taxes change according to income.)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, common in both written and spoken Japanese — frequently used in business, legal, and administrative contexts (pricing, policy, eligibility) as well as everyday explanations.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **に従って** — "in accordance with/as X progresses," can overlap but often implies following a rule or gradual change rather than matching a variable condition.
- [**によって**](grammar-によって・により.md) — "depending on/by means of," broader usage covering cause, agent, or variation, less specifically about responsive adjustment.
- **次第で** — "depending on," emphasizes that the outcome is determined by/contingent on the preceding factor.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 成績に応じて、クラスが分けられる。
   (Classes are divided according to grades.)
2. お客様のニーズに応じて、サービスを提供しています。
   (We provide services according to customers' needs.)
3. 天候に応じて、イベントの内容を変更します。
   (We will change the event content depending on the weather.)
4. 年齢に応じた運動量が推奨されている。
   (An amount of exercise suited to one's age is recommended.)
5. 収入に応じて、支払う税金の額が異なる。
   (The amount of tax paid differs according to income.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に応じて implies a proportional or adaptive response to a varying condition — it's not used for simple cause-and-effect (that's で or によって). Learners sometimes misuse it when a fixed rule is being followed rather than an adjustable matching; in that case に従って is more appropriate.
