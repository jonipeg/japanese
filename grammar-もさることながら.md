# ～もさることながら {#もさることながら}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～もさることながら

**Furigana form:** ～もさることながら *(然ることながら — conventionally written in kana here)*

**Meaning:** "Not to mention X, Y is also true" / "X is notable, but Y is even more so" — acknowledges that X is impressive/true, but emphasizes that Y deserves equal or greater attention.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (noun) + もさることながら
- The noun represents the first, already-acknowledged notable point, followed by a clause introducing the second (often more emphasized) point.

**Typical context:** Formal-to-neutral written and spoken register; commonly used in reviews, evaluations, and formal commentary (e.g., praising both a product's features and its design, or a person's two notable qualities). Not rude; a polished, slightly literary way of building toward a stronger point while still crediting the first.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **いかんによらず／いかんにかかわらず／をとわず** — these all mean "regardless of X," a completely different concessive-exclusion meaning (dismissing the relevance of a factor), unlike もさることながら, which affirms both points as relevant rather than dismissing either.

**Opposite grammar structures:** A conceptual opposite would be ～ならいざしらず or ～はさておき, both of which involve setting aside or contrasting away a point rather than crediting it while elevating a second one. No direct single "opposite" pattern exists, since もさることながら is fundamentally additive-with-emphasis rather than contrastive-exclusive.

**Example sentences:**
1. 結婚するには愛情もさることながら、経済力のある人を選ぶべきだ。
   (When it comes to marriage, love matters, but you should also choose someone with financial stability.)
2. この店は味もさることながら、店員の接客態度も素晴らしい。
   (This restaurant's food is great, but the staff's hospitality is wonderful too.)
3. 彼女の美しさもさることながら、その知性には誰もが驚かされる。
   (Her beauty is notable, but everyone is even more struck by her intelligence.)
4. この製品は価格の安さもさることながら、耐久性の高さが評価されている。
   (This product's low price is notable, but its high durability is what's really praised.)
5. 新人賞の受賞もさることながら、彼の今後の活躍にさらに期待が高まっている。
   (Winning the newcomer award is notable, but expectations for his future success are rising even more.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** The source lists いかんによらず／いかんにかかわらず／をとわず as a separate "merged cluster" tested alongside this entry in the same test batch (文法12回目/宿題12), though structurally these two groups mean very different things — students should not confuse the additive, two-point-emphasis function of もさることながら with the dismissive "regardless of X" function of the いかん cluster. A key nuance: もさることながら always implies BOTH points are true and worth noting, with the second receiving greater emphasis — it never dismisses or excludes the first point as irrelevant.
