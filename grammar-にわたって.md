# ～にわたって {#にわたって}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に渡って

**Furigana form:** ～にわたって

**Meaning:** "Over the course of X / spanning X" — indicates that an event, condition, or process continuously covers an extensive range of time, space, or scope.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (duration/range noun) + にわたって (例: 10年にわたって、全国にわたって、3回にわたって)
- Attributive form: にわたる + 名詞 (例: 長期にわたる工事)

**Typical context:** Formal, mostly written — common in news reports, official announcements, and formal narration describing large-scale or long-lasting events.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**を通して**](grammar-を通して・を通じて.md) — "throughout X," similar duration/span sense but can also mean "via/through a medium," broader usage.
- **の間** — "during X," more neutral and general, lacks the nuance of covering an extensive/notable range.
- [**にかけて**](grammar-にかけて.md) — "from X to Y (roughly spanning)," used for a looser, less precisely bounded range, often of time or geography.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. この工事は3年にわたって行われた。
   (This construction project was carried out over the course of three years.)
2. 台風の影響は、関東から東北地方にわたって広がった。
   (The typhoon's effects spread across the Kanto to Tohoku regions.)
3. 彼女は10年間にわたって、ボランティア活動を続けている。
   (She has continued volunteer work for over ten years.)
4. 会議は5時間にわたって続いた。
   (The meeting continued for a span of five hours.)
5. 長期にわたる不況が、多くの企業に影響を与えた。
   (The long-lasting recession affected many companies.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にわたって is reserved for notably long or extensive spans — using it for a short/trivial duration (e.g., "studied for 2 にわたって hours") sounds odd; plain の間 or で would be more natural there. The attributive form にわたる directly modifies a following noun (長期にわたる工事 = "long-spanning construction").
