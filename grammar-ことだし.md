# ～ことだし {#ことだし}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ことだし

**Furigana form:** ～ことだし

**Meaning:** "Given the fact that X..." — a mild, casual reason-giving expression, often used just before announcing a decision or suggestion, softening the justification rather than stating it bluntly.

**Conjugation:**
- Verb/い形容詞/な形容詞の普通形 + ことだし
- Example: もう遅いことだし、そろそろ帰ろうか (It's getting late, so let's head home soon).

**Typical context:** Spoken, casual register. Common in everyday conversation when proposing a plan or decision based on an observed circumstance — has a soft, conversational, non-confrontational tone.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **から** — "because X," a direct, simple reason-giving conjunction without the softening nuance of ことだし.
- **ので** — "because X," a softer and more formal/polite reason-giving conjunction than から, but still more direct than ことだし.
- **し** — the base listing-reason particle ことだし is built from (combining one or more reasons with し); ことだし specifically frames the reason as "the fact that X," adding a slightly more deliberate, gentle justification before a suggestion.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. もう遅いことだし、そろそろ帰ろうか。
   (It's getting late, so let's start heading home, shall we?)
2. 天気もいいことだし、散歩にでも行きましょうか。
   (Since the weather's nice, why don't we go for a walk?)
3. せっかく集まったことだし、みんなで写真を撮りませんか。
   (Since we've all gathered here, why don't we take a photo together?)
4. 子どもも寝たことだし、少しゆっくりお茶でも飲もう。
   (Now that the kids are asleep, let's relax and have some tea.)
5. 仕事も一段落したことだし、今日は早めに休むことにする。
   (Since work has settled down for now, I'll go ahead and rest early today.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Students sometimes substitute the more direct から or ので, missing the softer, more conversational "well, given that..." framing that ことだし provides — it typically introduces a reason as one of possibly several contributing factors, rather than presenting it as the single decisive cause. It almost always leads into a suggestion, invitation, or decision (often with ～ましょう、～ませんか、～ことにする), rather than a flat statement of fact.
