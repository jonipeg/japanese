# ～からある／～からする {#からある・からする}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～からある／～からする

**Furigana form:** ～からある／～からする

**Meaning:** Emphasizes that a quantity, weight, size, or price is remarkably large — "as much/large as X" (e.g., 100万からする "costs as much as a million yen"; 100キロからある "weighs as much as 100 kilos").

**Conjugation:**
- [Number + Noun] + からある — used for quantity/size/weight emphasis
- [Number + Noun] + からする — used specifically for price/value emphasis

**Typical context:** Used in both spoken and written Japanese; register-neutral, with an emphatic, impressed, or incredulous tone — often used to express surprise at how large/expensive something is.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **以上** — "X or more," a neutral numeric statement without emphatic surprise.
- [**も**](grammar-にかけては・も.md) — the basic emphatic particle for large quantities (100人も来た), simpler and more general-purpose than からある/からする.
- **近く** — "nearly X," expresses approximation rather than emphatic scale.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct grammatical opposite, but conceptually contrasts with しか～ない ("only as little as X"), which emphasizes smallness instead of largeness.

**Example sentences:**
1. この絵は50万円からするそうだ。
   (I hear this painting costs as much as 500,000 yen.)
2. 彼は、100キロからある荷物を軽々と持ち上げた。
   (He effortlessly lifted a load weighing as much as 100 kilograms.)
3. あのマンションは1億円からする高級物件らしい。
   (That apartment is apparently a luxury property costing as much as 100 million yen.)
4. 祖父の蔵には、200年からある古い家具が残されている。
   (In my grandfather's storehouse, old furniture as much as 200 years old remains.)
5. この橋は全長2キロからある巨大な建造物だ。
   (This bridge is a massive structure spanning as much as 2 kilometers.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** A margin note clarifies these are a complementary (not fully synonymous) pair: からある emphasizes quantity/size/weight, while からする emphasizes price/value — mixing them up (e.g., using からする for a weight, or からある for a price) is the most common error. Always check whether the number refers to money (からする) or a physical quantity/measurement (からある).
