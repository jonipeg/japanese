# ～にかわって {#にかわって}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に代わって

**Furigana form:** ～にかわって

**Meaning:** "In place of X / on behalf of X" — indicates that someone/something takes over a role, action, or position previously held by another.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + にかわって — 社長にかわって (in place of the president)
- Related noun form: 名詞 + に代わる + 名詞 — 石油に代わるエネルギー (energy to replace oil)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, common in both speech and writing — announcements, speeches, business contexts ("on behalf of so-and-so, I will...").

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **の代わりに** — near-synonym, slightly more casual/common in everyday speech; にかわって is more formal/written.
- **をかわりに** (less standard) — rarely used; の代わりに is the standard casual form.
- **に代表されて** — "represented by X," different nuance of representation rather than substitution.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 入院中の社長にかわって、副社長が挨拶をした。
   (In place of the hospitalized president, the vice president gave the greeting.)
2. 今日は担任の先生にかわって、私が授業を行います。
   (Today, in place of the homeroom teacher, I will conduct the class.)
3. 石油にかわる新しいエネルギー源の開発が進んでいる。
   (Development of new energy sources to replace oil is progressing.)
4. 父にかわって、長男が会社を継ぐことになった。
   (In place of the father, the eldest son is going to take over the company.)
5. 従来の紙の地図にかわって、今ではスマホのアプリが使われている。
   (In place of traditional paper maps, smartphone apps are now used.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にかわって implies a genuine substitution/takeover of role, not just "instead of" in a casual sense — learners sometimes use it interchangeably with の代わりに in contexts too casual for its formal register. Also distinguish にかわって (verb-like, "replacing") from に代わる (noun-modifying, "that replaces").
