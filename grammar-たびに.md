# ～たび(に) {#たびに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～たび(に) / ～度(に)

**Furigana form:** ～たび(に) (度 read as たび in this expression)

**Meaning:** "Every time, whenever X happens, Y also happens" — describes a recurring correlation between two events.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形 (verb, dictionary form) + たびに
- 名詞 + の + たびに

**Typical context:** Neutral, used in both spoken and written Japanese.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ときはいつも** — "whenever," more colloquial equivalent.
- [**につれて**](grammar-につれて.md) — "as X progresses, Y changes gradually" — a different nuance of gradual co-change rather than a repeated discrete event.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though 一度だけ ("only once") contrasts with the repeated nuance of たびに.

**Example sentences:**
1. この曲を聞くたびに、学生時代を思い出す。
   (Every time I hear this song, I remember my school days.)
2. 彼女は旅行のたびに、お土産を買ってきてくれる。
   (Every time she travels, she buys me a souvenir.)
3. 会うたびに、彼は元気になっているように見える。
   (Every time I see him, he looks like he's getting more energetic.)
4. 大雨が降るたびに、この道路は浸水する。
   (Every time it rains heavily, this road floods.)
5. 台風が来るたびに、電車が止まる。
   (Every time a typhoon comes, the trains stop.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** たびに requires the preceding verb to be in dictionary form, even when the event it describes is in the past — learners mistakenly use the た-form here, which is incorrect.
