# ～に恥じない {#に恥じない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～に恥じない

**Furigana form:** ～にはじない

**Meaning:** "Worthy of X / not shameful in light of X" — describes a person's conduct, work, or achievement as living up to a name, title, reputation, or standard without disgracing it.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に恥じない (+ 名詞)
- 名に恥じない料理 — "food worthy of (living up to) the name"

**Typical context:** Formal, written register; used in commendations, speeches, and formal writing praising someone's conduct as matching their position, title, or principles.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **～にふさわしい** — "suitable/fitting for X," a broader comparison of appropriateness, not specifically about avoiding shame or disgrace.
- **～に見合う** — "to match/be commensurate with X," focuses on proportionality rather than honor.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～を汚す ("to disgrace/tarnish [a name]") expresses the opposite outcome — failing to live up to and instead damaging a reputation.

**Example sentences:**
1. 五つ星レストランの名に恥じない料理を出す。
   (They serve food worthy of a five-star restaurant's name.)
2. 貧乏でも、良心に恥じない生き方をしたい。
   (Even if poor, I want to live in a way that doesn't shame my conscience.)
3. チャンピオンの名に恥じない見事な試合だった。
   (It was a magnificent match worthy of a champion's name.)
4. 彼は会社の代表として、恥じない態度で交渉に臨んだ。
   (He approached the negotiations with conduct befitting his role as the company's representative.)
5. 先輩の教えに恥じないよう、精一杯努力するつもりだ。
   (I intend to do my best so as not to disgrace what my senior taught me.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Most often used attributively, modifying a following noun (Xに恥じない + noun), rather than standing alone as a plain predicate. Don't confuse with the simple adjective 恥ずかしい ("embarrassing") — に恥じない is specifically about not disgracing a name, title, or principle, and carries a formal, commendatory tone.
