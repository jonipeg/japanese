# ～っぽい {#っぽい}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～っぽい

**Furigana form:** ～っぽい (no kanji)

**Meaning:** "-ish, seems like, prone to" — a casual suffix attached to nouns, adjective stems, or verb stems to indicate a tendency or resemblance.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞／な形容詞語幹／い形容詞語幹／動詞ます形 + っぽい (conjugates like an い-adjective: っぽくない、っぽかった)

**Typical context:** Casual spoken Japanese.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ような** — "seems like," more neutral and usable in formal contexts.
- [**がち**](grammar-がち.md) — "tends to, prone to" (usually negative tendency) — focuses on frequency of occurrence rather than resemblance/quality.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼はまだ子供っぽいところがある。
   (He still has a childish side to him.)
2. この布は安っぽく見える。
   (This fabric looks cheap.)
3. 最近、忘れっぽくなった気がする。
   (I feel like I've become forgetful lately.)
4. 彼女は怒りっぽい性格だ。
   (She has a quick-tempered personality.)
5. この水、少し白っぽい色をしている。
   (This water has a slightly whitish color.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** っぽい is casual and can sound slightly negative or dismissive (e.g., 安っぽい "cheap-looking"); learners should avoid it in formal writing, where ような or らしい are more appropriate alternatives.
