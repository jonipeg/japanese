# ～ずくめ {#ずくめ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ずくめ

**Furigana form:** ～ずくめ

**Meaning:** "Entirely/all X" — describes something as being composed entirely of one quality or item, e.g., いいことずくめ ("nothing but good things"), 黒ずくめ ("dressed entirely in black").

**Conjugation:**
- N + ずくめ

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal register, usable in both speech and writing; a moderately common, flexible expression (not as heavily literary as much of this cluster).

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**まみれ**](grammar-まみれ.md) — describes being covered in a physical, typically unpleasant substance (泥まみれ, "covered in mud") with an almost always negative connotation, whereas ずくめ describes something being entirely composed of one quality and can be positive (いいことずくめ) or neutral.
- [**だらけ**](grammar-だらけ.md) — similar to まみれ in being usually negative/messy (間違いだらけ, "full of mistakes"), closer to まみれ's negativity than ずくめ's neutral-to-positive flexibility.
- [**っぱなし**](grammar-っぱなし.md) — describes leaving an action in an ongoing, neglected state, a completely different concept (continuation/neglect) from ずくめ's idea of total composition.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 今年は昇進や結婚など、いいことずくめの一年だった。
   (This year was full of nothing but good things, like a promotion and getting married.)
2. 彼はいつも黒ずくめの格好をしている。
   (He always dresses entirely in black.)
3. この契約には規則ずくめで、自由がほとんどない。
   (This contract is nothing but rules, with almost no freedom.)
4. 新居は最新設備ずくめで、とても快適だ。
   (The new house is packed entirely with the latest equipment and is very comfortable.)
5. 彼女の話は自慢ずくめで、少々うんざりした。
   (Her story was nothing but bragging, and I got a bit tired of it.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** ずくめ is part of the N1 "entirely/covered in X" confusable cluster and is most commonly mixed up with まみれ, since both describe being "entirely" something. The key distinction: まみれ is almost always about a PHYSICAL, unpleasant substance covering someone/something (泥まみれ, 汗まみれ), while ずくめ is about an ABSTRACT or categorical composition — everything in a situation being of one type — and crucially can carry a POSITIVE meaning (いいことずくめ) that まみれ never can.
