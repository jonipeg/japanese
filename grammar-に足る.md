# ～に足る {#に足る}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～に足る

**Furigana form:** ～にたる

**Meaning:** Indicates that something or someone is sufficiently worthy to merit a particular action or evaluation — "worthy of ~," "enough to ~," "deserving of ~."

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形・名詞する + に足る
- 信頼するに足る — "worthy of being trusted"

**Typical context:** Written, formal/literary register — common in essays, newspaper commentary, and formal written evaluations of people, ideas, or evidence.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **に値する（にあたいする）** — almost interchangeable meaning "worthy of ~"; slightly more common in general formal writing.
- **に堪える（にたえる）** — also means "worthy of withstanding ~" but is restricted to a smaller set of verbs like 鑑賞・批判・議論.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct antonym pattern; negation is normally expressed by putting the sentence itself in negative form (e.g., ～とは思えない) rather than attaching ～に足らない directly after a noun.

**Example sentences:**
1. 次の首相は国民の代表と言うに足る人物であってほしい。
   (I hope the next prime minister will be someone worthy of being called a representative of the people.)
2. インターネットで得たその情報は、信頼に足るものとは思えない。
   (I don't think that information obtained from the internet is something worthy of trust.)
3. だれかが離婚したとかしないとかなど、取るに足らないニュースだ。
   (News about whether someone did or didn't get divorced is a trivial matter not worth paying attention to.)
4. この論文は学会で発表するに足る内容だ。
   (This paper has content worthy of being presented at an academic conference.)
5. 彼の実績は信頼するに足るものだった。
   (His track record was something worthy of trust.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern most often modifies a noun directly (～と言うに足る人物). For a negative meaning, don't simply attach に足らない to the noun being modified — instead, negate the sentence-final verb as in example 2 (信頼に足るものとは思えない, not 信頼に足らないもの). The idiom 取るに足らない is a fixed expression meaning "not worth bothering with / insignificant."
