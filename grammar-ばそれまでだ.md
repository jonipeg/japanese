# ～ばそれまでだ {#ばそれまでだ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～ばそれまでだ

**Furigana form:** ～ばそれまでだ

**Meaning:** Says that if a certain thing happens, everything else becomes meaningless or futile, and nothing more can be done about it — "if X happens, that's the end of it" / "once X, it's all over."

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞ば形 + それまでだ
- 死んでしまえばそれまでだ — "once you die, that's the end of everything"

**Typical context:** Written and spoken Japanese, neutral-to-reflective register — common in philosophical or cautionary statements, often paired with ～ても for an "even if... but if... then it's all over" structure.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **～たら終わりだ** — nearly synonymous, "if X, it's over," slightly more casual and concrete than the somewhat more reflective/philosophical ～ばそれまでだ.
- **～てしまえばそれまでだ** — frequently combined form emphasizing the finality and irreversibility of the triggering action.
- **～と言われればそれまでだ** — a fixed idiomatic use meaning "if you put it that way, there's nothing more to say," conceding a point while implying the conversation has reached a dead end.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a phrase like ～ても何とかなる ("even if X happens, things can still be worked out") would instead express that a setback doesn't have to be final.

**Example sentences:**
1. 人間、死んでしまえばそれまでだ。生きているうちにやりたいことをやろう。
   (Once a person dies, that's the end — let's do what we want to do while we're alive.)
2. いくら練習の時上手にできても、本番でうまくいかなければそれまでだ。
   (No matter how well you do in practice, if it doesn't go well on the day itself, that's the end of it.)
3. どんなに立派なホールを作っても、十分に活用されなければそれまでだ。
   (No matter how splendid a hall you build, if it isn't put to good use, it's all for nothing.)
4. 好みの問題と言われればそれまでだが、わたしはこのレストランの内装はなんとなく好きになれない。
   (If you tell me it's just a matter of taste, there's nothing more to say, but I just can't warm to this restaurant's interior.)
5. せっかく貯めたお金も、使ってしまえばそれまでだ。計画的に使おう。
   (Even money you worked hard to save is gone for good once you spend it — let's use it wisely.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern is frequently used in the combined structure "～ても…ばそれまでだ" ("even if X, if Y happens, it's all over"), contrasting an effort or condition with a decisive negative outcome. The fixed idiomatic phrase ～と言われればそれまでだ ("if you put it that way, there's nothing more to say") is worth memorizing separately, since its meaning (conceding defeat in an argument) is a specialized extension of the core "it's all over" sense.
