# ～を～として {#を-として}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を～として

**Furigana form:** ～を～として (no special kanji reading)

**Meaning:** "Treating X as Y, with X as/in the capacity of Y" — assigns a role, status, or category to something/someone; frequently used to state a designated function or position.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞A + を + 名詞B + として: 彼を代表として選んだ (chose him as the representative)
- Common variants: 〜を〜とする (as a modifying/defining clause), 〜を〜とした (past/modifying form): 学生を対象とした調査 (a survey targeting/aimed at students)

**Typical context:** Formal, written register — common in official documents, reports, announcements, and academic writing to specify roles, purposes, or target groups.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **〜として** (without を) — simply marks a role/capacity for the subject itself (彼は医者として働く — he works as a doctor), without the "designating X as Y" transitive nuance.
- [**〜をもって**](grammar-をもって.md) — "by means of/with," more about means/timing, used in very formal contexts (本日をもって — as of today).
- **〜に(は)〜とする** — less common variant with similar designation nuance.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 会議では彼を議長として選出した。
   (At the meeting, they elected him as the chairperson.)
2. この研究は中高生を対象として行われた。
   (This research was conducted targeting middle and high school students.)
3. 会社は環境保護を経営方針の一つとしている。
   (The company treats environmental protection as one of its management policies.)
4. 政府はこの問題を最優先課題として扱っている。
   (The government is treating this issue as the top-priority task.)
5. 彼女はボランティア活動を生きがいとして毎日頑張っている。
   (She treats volunteer work as her purpose in life and works hard at it every day.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** を～として is transitive — it designates one thing (marked by を) as serving the role of another (として) — distinct from plain ～として, which simply describes the capacity of the sentence's own subject. Learners sometimes drop を and only use として where a clear "treating X as Y" relationship (with two distinct nouns) is intended, muddying the meaning.
