# ～にしろ/にしても/にせよ {#にしろ・にしても・にせよ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～にしろ/にしても/にせよ

**Furigana form:** ～にしろ/にしても/にせよ

**Meaning:** "Even if / whether it be X..." — a concessive construction, often paired to list contrasting alternatives ("whether A or B, [Y is still true]"), or used singly to mean "granting X, nevertheless..."

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞/普通形 + にしろ・にしても・にせよ — 行くにしろ行かないにしろ (whether you go or not)
- Paired form: A にしろ、B にしろ — common listing pattern

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, more common in writing and formal speech; にせよ is the most formal/literary of the three, にしても is the most conversational.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**ても**](grammar-たとえ~ても.md) — general concessive "even if," simpler and more basic; にしろ/にしても/にせよ adds a more rhetorical, often listing nuance.
- [**であれ**](grammar-であれ・であろうと.md) — "be it X," very formal/literary, overlaps closely with にせよ in register.
- [**とはいえ**](grammar-とはいえ.md) — "that said / even though X," used to pivot after stating a fact, not for listing alternatives.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 行くにしろ行かないにしろ、早く返事をしてほしい。
   (Whether you go or not, I'd like a quick answer.)
2. 冗談にしても、あの言い方はひどい。
   (Even granting it was a joke, that way of speaking was awful.)
3. 理由が何であるにせよ、遅刻は遅刻だ。
   (Whatever the reason may be, being late is being late.)
4. 給料が安いにしろ、この仕事にはやりがいがある。
   (Even though the pay is low, this job is rewarding.)
5. 賛成するにしても反対するにしても、理由を説明すべきだ。
   (Whether you agree or disagree, you should explain your reasons.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** All three are largely interchangeable in meaning, differing mainly in formality (にせよ > にしろ > にしても, from most to least formal/literary); learners should match register to context rather than assuming free interchangeability in very casual or very formal writing.
