# ～のもとで・のもとに {#のもとで・のもとに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～の下で・の下に

**Furigana form:** ～のもとで・のもとに

**Meaning:** "Under X (guidance, supervision, influence, or condition)" — indicates that an action or situation takes place under the influence, authority, guidance, or stated condition of something/someone. のもとで emphasizes the setting/environment of an ongoing action; のもとに often emphasizes the condition or basis under which something is done.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (person/authority/condition) + のもとで／のもとに (例: 先生の指導のもとで、法のもとに、同じ条件のもとで)

**Typical context:** Formal, used in both speech and writing — common in academic, legal, and official contexts (education, law, organizational structure).

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **の指導で** — "under the guidance of X," more specific/narrow paraphrase limited to guidance/teaching contexts.
- [**に基づいて**](grammar-に基づいて.md) — "based on X," overlaps with the "condition" sense of のもとに but focuses on evidentiary/logical grounding rather than authority/environment.
- **の下に置かれて** — literal "placed under X," more concrete/physical paraphrase of being under someone's control.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は有名な教授の指導のもとで、研究を続けている。
   (He continues his research under the guidance of a famous professor.)
2. すべての国民は法のもとに平等である。
   (All citizens are equal under the law.)
3. 厳しい監督のもとで、選手たちは厳しい練習を重ねた。
   (Under a strict coach, the players repeated rigorous training.)
4. 同じ条件のもとで実験を行い、結果を比較した。
   (The experiment was conducted under the same conditions, and the results were compared.)
5. 両親の保護のもとで、子供たちは安心して育つことができる。
   (Under their parents' protection, children can grow up feeling safe.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** のもとで and のもとに are often interchangeable, but のもとで tends to describe an ongoing environment/setting for an action (指導のもとで研究する = "doing research under guidance"), while のもとに more often expresses a fixed principle or condition (法のもとに平等 = "equal under the law"). Don't confuse 下で/下に here with the literal spatial "under" (木の下で休む), which is a separate, concrete usage of 下.
