# ～際に／に際して {#に際して}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～際に／～に際して

**Furigana form:** ～さいに／～にさいして

**Meaning:** "On the occasion of X / when X (formal)" — marks a formal, often significant or official occasion/moment as the time frame for the main action, such as ceremonies, procedures, or important transitions.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + の際に／に際して
- Verb (dictionary form) + 際に／に際して
- ご利用の際には、会員証をご提示ください (When using [the service], please present your membership card)
- 開会に際して、一言ご挨拶申し上げます (On the occasion of the opening, I'd like to say a few words)

**Typical context:** Formal written and spoken Japanese — official announcements, business documents, ceremonies, instructions on forms/signage.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ときに** — plain, general "when," usable in casual contexts; lacks the formal/official register.
- **場合に** — "in the case of," focuses on conditional scenarios rather than a specific formal occasion.
- **にあたって** — nearly synonymous with に際して, used for important/formal junctures, slightly more common in speeches.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. ご契約の際には、身分証明書が必要です。
   (When signing the contract, identification is required.)
2. 新しいプロジェクトを始めるに際して、皆様にご協力をお願いいたします。
   (On the occasion of starting this new project, I ask for everyone's cooperation.)
3. 卒業式に際して、校長先生がスピーチをされた。
   (On the occasion of the graduation ceremony, the principal gave a speech.)
4. 海外へ出発する際に、パスポートを忘れずに持っていってください。
   (When departing overseas, please be sure to bring your passport.)
5. 式典の開催に際しまして、心より感謝申し上げます。
   (On the occasion of holding this ceremony, I offer my heartfelt thanks.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 際に／に際して is noticeably more formal than ときに and should not be used in casual daily conversation (e.g., don't say 食べる際に for "when eating lunch with a friend" — use ときに instead). It's reserved for formal, often one-time or significant occasions.
