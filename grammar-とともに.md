# ～とともに {#とともに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～と共に

**Furigana form:** ～とともに

**Meaning:** "Together with X" (accompaniment) or "As X happens, Y also happens" (simultaneous change/proportional co-occurrence).

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + とともに — 家族とともに (together with family)
- 動詞辞書形 + とともに — 時間が経つとともに (as time passes)
- 名詞 + であるとともに — 教師であるとともに (while being a teacher, [also]...)

**Typical context:** Formal, written — news reports, academic writing, formal speeches. Less common in casual conversation (一緒に is used instead for plain accompaniment).

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**につれて**](grammar-につれて.md) — "as X, so Y," strictly about gradual proportional change; cannot mean simple accompaniment.
- **に伴って** — "accompanying X," very similar to the change-nuance of とともに, slightly more formal/technical.
- **一緒に** — plain, casual "together with," used in everyday speech.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は家族とともに新しい町へ引っ越した。
   (He moved to a new town together with his family.)
2. 技術の発展とともに、人々の生活は大きく変化した。
   (As technology developed, people's lives changed greatly.)
3. 彼女は優れた研究者であるとともに、良き教育者でもある。
   (She is an excellent researcher as well as a good educator.)
4. 年を取るとともに、体力が落ちてきた。
   (As I've gotten older, my physical strength has declined.)
5. 地震とともに、大きな津波が発生した。
   (Along with the earthquake, a huge tsunami occurred.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** とともに has two distinct uses (accompaniment vs. parallel change) that learners sometimes don't distinguish; also, unlike につれて, とともに can be used for simultaneous/sudden co-occurrence (not just gradual change), as in the earthquake/tsunami example.
