# ～極まる／～極まりない {#きわまる・きわまりない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～極まる／～極まりない

**Furigana form:** ～きわまる／～きわまりない

**Meaning:** "Extremely X, in the extreme" — intensifies a na-adjective to its utmost degree, often describing a strongly negative quality (失礼極まる態度, "an extremely rude attitude"). Despite the "ない" in きわまりない, it is NOT a literal negation — both forms mean the same intensified "extremely X."

**Conjugation:**
- な-adjective stem + 極まる
- な-adjective stem + 極まりない (emphatic variant, same meaning as 極まる)

**Typical context:** Formal, written, literary register, often used in criticism or commentary with a strong, judgmental tone.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**のきわみ**](grammar-のきわみ.md) — attaches to emotion/state nouns (感動の極み) to name a peak state reached, whereas きわまる attaches to na-adjective stems (失礼極まる) to intensify a quality being described.
- [**かぎりだ**](grammar-かぎりだ.md) — expresses the speaker's own subjective personal feeling at its peak (うらやましい限りだ), whereas きわまる objectively intensifies a quality in a third party or situation.
- [**にたえない**](grammar-にたえる・にたえない.md) — describes an inability to endure something, a reaction, rather than きわまる's direct intensification of a quality itself.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. コンビニで店員に失礼極まる態度をとられ、頭にきた。
   (I got angry after being treated with extremely rude behavior by a convenience store clerk.)
2. 彼の発言は無責任極まりないものだった。
   (His remark was extremely irresponsible.)
3. この計画は危険極まる内容を含んでいる。
   (This plan contains extremely dangerous elements.)
4. そのやり方は非常識極まりないと批判された。
   (That method was criticized as being utterly absurd/senseless.)
5. 彼女の対応は冷淡極まるものだった。
   (Her response was extremely cold/unfeeling.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** きわまる/きわまりない is a core member of the N1 extreme-degree cluster and is frequently confused with のきわみ because they share the same kanji 極. The critical distinction: のきわみ attaches to NOUNS of emotion/state (感動の極み), while きわまる/きわまりない attaches to NA-ADJECTIVE STEMS (失礼極まる, 危険極まりない) — mixing up the attachment pattern is the single most common test error in this pair.
