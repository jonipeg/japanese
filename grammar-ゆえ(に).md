# ～ゆえ（に） {#ゆえ（に）}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～ゆえ（に）

**Furigana form:** ～ゆえ（に）

**Meaning:** "Because of X" / "For the reason of X" — a literary, formal way of stating a cause or reason. Written-language style.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞（-の）・普通形（ナ形容詞-な／-である・名詞-である） + ゆえ（に）
- 慣れないことゆえ — "because [this] is something I'm not used to"
- 国民の信頼が得られなかったゆえに — "because the trust of the citizens could not be obtained"

**Typical context:** Formal written Japanese — letters, official statements, speeches, and literary prose; not used in everyday conversation.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**～がゆえに**](grammar-がゆえに.md) — a closely related, slightly more literary variant often used with na-adjectives/nouns in である form, emphasizing the cause even more strongly.
- **～ため（に）** — the everyday, neutral equivalent for stating cause and purpose, usable in both speech and writing.
- **～ので** — the ordinary conversational reason marker, far more casual than ゆえ（に）.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a concessive structure like ～にもかかわらず ("despite X") would instead describe a result occurring in spite of a given circumstance, rather than because of it.

**Example sentences:**
1. 慣れないことゆえ、数々の不手際、どうぞお許しください。
   (Please forgive the various mistakes, as this is something I am not used to.)
2. 理想ばかり申し上げたゆえ、実践が伴わないと思われたかもしれません。
   (Because I spoke only of ideals, you may have thought my words weren't backed by action.)
3. 国民の信頼が得られなかったゆえに、新しい政策は再検討しなければならなくなった。
   (Because the trust of the citizens could not be won, the new policy had to be reconsidered.)
4. 経験不足ゆえに、今回の判断には多くの時間を要した。
   (Owing to a lack of experience, this decision required a great deal of time.)
5. 彼は正直であるがゆえに、時に人を傷つける発言をしてしまう。
   (Precisely because he is honest, he sometimes ends up saying things that hurt others.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern belongs strictly to formal written or ceremonial speech — using it in casual daily conversation would sound stiff and unnatural. It frequently appears in apologetic or self-reflective formal writing (letters, reports) and pairs well with である-form nouns and na-adjectives when expressed as ～であるがゆえに.
