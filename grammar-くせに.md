# ～くせに {#くせに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～くせに

**Furigana form:** ～くせに

**Meaning:** "Even though / despite X" — used with a critical, contemptuous, or mocking tone to highlight a contradiction between a fact and someone's behavior or attitude.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞／い形容詞普通形 + くせに
- な形容詞 + な + くせに
- 名詞 + の + くせに

**Typical context:** Spoken, casual-to-blunt register — carries a negative emotional tone (criticism, irritation, sarcasm); avoid in formal or polite contexts.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **のに** — "even though," more neutral and can express surprise or regret without necessarily being critical or contemptuous.
- [**にもかかわらず**](grammar-にかかわらず・にもかかわらず.md) — "despite X," the formal/neutral equivalent, lacking くせに's emotional bite.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 下手なくせに、人に教えたがる。
   (Even though he's bad at it, he insists on teaching others.)
2. 子供のくせに、生意気なことを言うな。
   (Don't talk back like that — you're just a kid!)
3. 知っているくせに、知らないふりをした。
   (Even though she knew, she pretended not to know.)
4. お金がないくせに、高いものばかり買う。
   (Even though he has no money, he only buys expensive things.)
5. できないくせに、できると言い張る。
   (Even though he can't do it, he insists that he can.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** くせに almost always implies criticism or contempt toward the subject — using it about oneself or in a neutral way can sound strange unless clearly self-deprecating. Don't confuse it with the more neutral のに, which is appropriate in polite/formal contexts where くせに is not.
