# ～ずには済まない／～ないでは済まない {#ずにはすまない・ないではすまない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ずには済まない／～ないでは済まない

**Furigana form:** ～ずにはすまない／～ないではすまない

**Meaning:** "Can't get away without doing X" — social, moral, or practical pressure makes it impossible to avoid doing X; the matter won't be considered resolved/settled unless X is done.

**Conjugation:**
- V-ない形(remove ない) + ずには済まない
- V-ない形 + では済まない

**Typical context:** Formal written/spoken register, used when describing social consequences, obligations, or moral pressure to act.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**ずにはおかない**](grammar-ずにはおかない.md) — expresses that an outcome is bound to happen due to natural force, emotion, or strong determination, whereas ずにはすまない expresses a social/moral obligation that makes avoiding an action impossible ("you won't be let off without doing it").
- [**ないものでもない**](grammar-ないものでもない.md) — a hedge expressing that something is "not impossible," a much weaker and more tentative claim than ずにはすまない's strong sense of unavoidable obligation.
- **ずにすむ/ないですむ** — the direct conceptual opposite: manage to avoid an action without any negative consequence.

**Opposite grammar structures:** **ずにすむ／ないですむ** — "manage to avoid doing X [without consequence]," the direct opposite of being unable to get away without doing X.

**Example sentences:**
1. 今回の件はこちらが全面的に悪いのだから、謝らないではすまない。
   (Since we're entirely at fault this time, we can't get away without apologizing.)
2. 会社に大きな損害を与えたのだから、責任を取らずにはすまないだろう。
   (Having caused the company such major damage, he surely can't avoid taking responsibility.)
3. これだけ約束を破ったら、弁明せずにはすまない。
   (After breaking so many promises, there's no way to avoid giving an explanation.)
4. 周りに迷惑をかけた以上、きちんと説明しないではすまない。
   (Having caused this much trouble for everyone, I can't get away without a proper explanation.)
5. 子どもを傷つけてしまった以上、謝罪せずにはすまない。
   (Since I ended up hurting the child, I can't avoid apologizing.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** ずにはすまない is a prominent member of the N1 "can't help but" cluster and is most often confused with ずにはおかない. The key test: ずにはすまない is about the SUBJECT being unable to escape an obligation imposed by social/moral consequence (謝らないではすまない, "I can't get away without apologizing"), while ずにはおかない is about an outcome being GUARANTEED to occur due to an external force or strong emotional drive, often with the subject causing an effect on someone else rather than fulfilling an obligation themselves.
