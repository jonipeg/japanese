# ～にひきかえ {#にひきかえ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に引き換え

**Furigana form:** ～にひきかえ

**Meaning:** "In contrast to X" — highlights a stark contrast between two things, often with an implicit value judgment (one is better/worse, more/less than the other).

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞・い形容詞・な形容詞 の名詞修飾型 + の + にひきかえ (i.e., the noun-modifying form of verbs/adjectives + の + にひきかえ)
- 名詞 (noun) + にひきかえ
- Example from source: サッカーにひきかえ (in contrast to soccer)

**Typical context:** Formal-to-neutral, written and spoken Japanese — common in comparative commentary (news, essays, conversation about differing outcomes/situations). Carries an evaluative tone, often implying surprise or irony at the contrast. Not rude, but can sound slightly judgmental or pointed when comparing people.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**に対して**](grammar-に対して.md) — "in contrast to/regarding X"; a more neutral contrast marker that can simply present two facts side by side without necessarily implying a value judgment, whereas にひきかえ often carries an evaluative "surprisingly different" nuance.
- **一方で** — "on the other hand"; also marks contrast, but is more neutral/balanced in tone and commonly used to present two sides of the same topic without strong judgment, unlike にひきかえ's sharper, sometimes ironic contrast.
- **反面 (はんめん)** — "while on the other hand (a flip side of the same subject)"; typically contrasts two aspects of the same thing/person (a pro and a con), whereas にひきかえ contrasts two separate things/people.
- **それに比べて** — "compared to that"; a plain, common comparative phrase, similar in function but more casual/conversational, lacking にひきかえ's slightly literary, pointed tone.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a conceptual contrast would be ～と同様に ("similarly to X"), which emphasizes similarity rather than contrast.

**Example sentences:**
1. シンガポールではサッカーにひきかえ、野球の人気は今ひとつだ。
   (In contrast to soccer in Singapore, baseball's popularity is lackluster.)
2. 兄が優秀なのにひきかえ、弟はいつも遊んでばかりいる。
   (In contrast to his excellent older brother, the younger brother is always just goofing around.)
3. 去年の豊作にひきかえ、今年は記録的な不作だった。
   (In contrast to last year's bumper harvest, this year was a record-breaking poor harvest.)
4. 昔の活気ある商店街にひきかえ、今はシャッター通りと化している。
   (In contrast to the once-lively shopping street, it has now become a row of shuttered shops.)
5. 彼女の明るい性格にひきかえ、彼は無口で暗い印象を与える。
   (In contrast to her cheerful personality, he comes across as quiet and gloomy.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This entry notes it was confirmed twice in scan4's digest (reinforced), suggesting it's a heavily tested pattern. A key nuance for learners: にひきかえ almost always implies the speaker's evaluative surprise at the gap between the two things being compared — it's rarely used for a flat, neutral comparison. Students should distinguish it from に対して (neutral contrast) by checking whether the sentence conveys a sense of "surprisingly/strikingly different."
