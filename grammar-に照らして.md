# ～に照らして {#に照らして}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～に照らして

**Furigana form:** ～にてらして

**Meaning:** "In light of / judging from X" — to evaluate or judge a matter by comparing it against a standard such as law, rules, experience, or precedent.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に照らして／に照らした＋名詞 (base verb: 照らす)
- 国際法に照らして — "judging in light of international law"

**Typical context:** Formal, written register — common in legal, judicial, and academic contexts where a judgment is made by referencing an external standard.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**に基づいて**](grammar-に基づいて.md) — "based on," broader; used for facts, data, or policy as a foundation rather than a point of comparison.
- [**に即して**](grammar-に即して.md) — close in meaning, but に即して emphasizes matching/conforming to a standard, while に照らして emphasizes comparing and judging against it.
- **から見て** — "judging from," more casual and broader in scope.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a phrase like ～を無視して ("ignoring X") would describe judging without reference to any standard.

**Example sentences:**
1. 自分の経験に照らして後輩にアドバイスした。
   (I gave advice to my junior, judging from my own experience.)
2. 国家間の紛争は国際法に照らした判断で解決すべきだ。
   (Disputes between nations should be resolved through judgments made in light of international law.)
3. 規則に照らして、彼の行動は問題ないと判断された。
   (Judging by the rules, his conduct was deemed acceptable.)
4. 良識に照らして考えれば、その発言は不適切だ。
   (Judging by common sense, that remark is inappropriate.)
5. 過去の判例に照らして、今回の判決が下された。
   (This ruling was handed down in light of past precedents.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に照らして is almost always followed by a verb of judgment (判断する, 考える, 見る), since its core meaning is "measuring X against a standard." Don't confuse it with に即して, which is about conforming to actual conditions rather than making a comparative judgment.
