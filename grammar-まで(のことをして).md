# ～まで(のことをして) {#まで-のことをして}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～まで(のことをして)

**Furigana form:** ～まで(のことをして)

**Meaning:** "To go so far as to ~ / to the extent of doing ~" — expresses that an action is an extreme, often inappropriate or surprising measure, usually framed negatively (implying the speaker finds it excessive or distasteful to go that far).

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞て形 (verb te-form) + まで
- Example: 客を騙してまで売上成績を伸ばしたいとは思いません。 (I don't want to increase my sales figures to the extent of deceiving customers.)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal spoken and written Japanese; often used in statements of personal principle, criticism, or moral judgment about going to extreme lengths.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **てでも** — "even if it means doing ~" — similar extreme-measure nuance, often used with volitional intent (何をしてでも vs 何かをしてまで), slightly more proactive/determined in tone.
- [**さえ**](grammar-さえ.md) — "even ~" — marks an extreme example within a list or scale, but doesn't specifically frame an action as a measure taken to achieve a goal.
- **にしてまで** — a stronger variant emphasizing sacrifice (犠牲にしてまで, "even at the cost of ~").

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though 当然のように ("as a matter of course") contrasts in spirit by framing an action as unremarkable rather than extreme.

**Example sentences:**
1. 客を騙してまで売上成績を伸ばしたいとは思いません。
   (I don't want to increase my sales figures to the extent of deceiving customers.)
2. 彼は借金をしてまで新しい車を買った。
   (He went so far as to take on debt just to buy a new car.)
3. 健康を害してまで働く必要はないと思う。
   (I don't think it's necessary to work to the point of damaging your health.)
4. 友達を裏切ってまで出世したいとは思わない。
   (I don't want to get ahead in my career to the extent of betraying my friends.)
5. 彼女は家族に嘘をついてまで、その秘密を守ろうとした。
   (She tried to protect that secret even to the extent of lying to her family.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners sometimes confuse ～まで with ～までに (a deadline marker); the two are unrelated. ～まで(のことをして) specifically implies going to an extreme, often ethically questionable, length — it carries an inherent critical or surprised nuance, unlike neutral degree expressions.
