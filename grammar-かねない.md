# ～かねない {#かねない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～かねない

**Furigana form:** ～かねない

**Meaning:** "Might well (do something bad) / there's a risk of X" — expresses concern that an undesirable or dangerous outcome could occur.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞ます形（stem）+ かねない
- 事故になりかねない — "could well lead to an accident"
- 誤解を招きかねない — "could well invite misunderstanding"

**Typical context:** Written and spoken, neutral-to-formal register — common in warnings, news reports, and cautionary advice.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**恐れがある**](grammar-恐れがある.md) — "there is a fear/risk that," more formal, typically used in official warnings and reports.
- **かもしれない** — general possibility that can be positive, negative, or neutral; weaker and more casual than かねない, which specifically flags a negative risk.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; かねる (cannot bring oneself to do) is a visually similar but unrelated-meaning counterpart, not a true antonym.

**Example sentences:**
1. そんな運転は事故を起こしかねない。
   (Driving like that could well cause an accident.)
2. 彼の発言は誤解を招きかねない。
   (His remark could well invite misunderstanding.)
3. 無理なダイエットは健康を害しかねない。
   (An extreme diet could well harm your health.)
4. この問題を放置すれば、大きなトラブルになりかねない。
   (If we leave this problem unaddressed, it could well turn into a big issue.)
5. そのニュースは株価の下落を招きかねない。
   (That news could well trigger a stock price drop.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** かねない is used only for negative/undesirable possibilities — using it for a good outcome (e.g. 彼は成功しかねない) sounds wrong. Be careful not to confuse it with the unrelated かねる.
