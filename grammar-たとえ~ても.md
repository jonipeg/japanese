# たとえ～ても {#たとえても}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** たとえ～ても

**Furigana form:** たとえ～ても (no additional kanji readings needed)

**Meaning:** "Even if, even though" — a concessive construction where たとえ emphasizes a hypothetical or extreme condition before the concessive ても/でも clause.

**Conjugation:**
- たとえ + 動詞て形 + も
- たとえ + い形容詞 (くて) + も
- たとえ + な形容詞／名詞 + でも

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, used in both spoken and written Japanese to emphasize hypothetical extremes.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ても/でも (alone)** — the plain concessive, without the added emphasis たとえ provides.
- **どんなに～ても** — "no matter how much," used for degree.
- **いくら～ても** — "no matter how much," similar to どんなに, often interchangeable.

**Opposite grammar structures:** もし～たら (a plain conditional "if," without the concessive "even" nuance).

**Example sentences:**
1. たとえ失敗しても、諦めずに挑戦し続けるつもりだ。
   (Even if I fail, I intend to keep trying without giving up.)
2. たとえ雨が降っても、試合は予定通り行われる。
   (Even if it rains, the match will be held as scheduled.)
3. たとえどんなに辛くても、最後までやり遂げたい。
   (No matter how hard it is, I want to see it through to the end.)
4. たとえ君が反対でも、私はこの計画を進めるつもりだ。
   (Even if you're against it, I intend to proceed with this plan.)
5. たとえ給料が低くても、好きな仕事を続けたい。
   (Even if the pay is low, I want to continue doing the work I love.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** たとえ requires the ても/でも clause to complete the concession; the sentence is still grammatical without たとえ, but loses its emphatic "even if" nuance. Learners often forget the correct conjugation before ても — くて for い-adjectives, でも for な-adjectives and nouns.
