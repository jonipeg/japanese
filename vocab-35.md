## 夕刊 (ゆうかん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** an evening paper (newspaper edition)

**Similar words:**
- [朝刊](vocab-27.md#朝刊-ちょうかん)（ちょうかん）— "morning paper," the counterpart edition published in the morning.
- 新聞（しんぶん）— general term "newspaper," without specifying the edition timing.

**Opposite words:**
- [朝刊](vocab-27.md#朝刊-ちょうかん)（ちょうかん）— morning edition

**Example sentences:**
1. 父は毎日夕刊を読む。— My father reads the evening paper every day.
2. 夕刊にその事件の詳細が載っていた。— Details of the incident were printed in the evening paper.
3. 最近は夕刊を購読する人が減っている。— The number of people subscribing to evening papers has decreased recently.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 夕刊 specifically refers to the evening edition of a newspaper (as opposed to 朝刊, morning edition)—many Japanese newspapers historically published both editions daily, a system less familiar to learners from countries without this practice.

## 勇気 (ゆうき)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** courage, bravery

**Similar words:**
- 度胸（どきょう）— "guts, nerve," more about boldness/daring in a specific tense moment than general courage.
- [大胆](vocab-25.md#大胆-だいたん)（だいたん）— "bold, daring," describes a trait/manner rather than the abstract quality of courage.
- 勇敢（ゆうかん）— "brave, valiant," an adjective describing someone's action/character, while 勇気 is the noun "courage" itself.

**Opposite words:**
- [臆病](vocab-03.md#臆病-おくびょう)（おくびょう）— cowardice

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼女に告白する勇気が出なかった。— I couldn't muster the courage to confess my feelings to her.
2. 勇気を出して挑戦してみよう。— Let's summon up courage and give it a try.
3. その行動には大きな勇気が必要だった。— That action required great courage.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 勇気 is the abstract noun "courage," commonly used with 出す (勇気を出す, "to muster courage"); don't confuse it with 勇敢 (brave, an adjective describing a person or action) which describes the quality demonstrated rather than the inner resource itself.

## 夕暮れ (ゆうぐれ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** evening, dusk, twilight

**Similar words:**
- 黄昏（たそがれ）— more literary/poetic synonym for dusk/twilight.
- 夕方（ゆうがた）— more everyday, casual term for "evening," less poetic than 夕暮れ.
- 日暮れ（ひぐれ）— very close synonym, "nightfall/dusk," often interchangeable with 夕暮れ.

**Opposite words:**
- [夜明け](#夜明け-よあけ)（よあけ）— dawn

**Example sentences:**
1. 夕暮れの空がオレンジ色に染まっている。— The evening sky is dyed orange.
2. 夕暮れ時に散歩するのが好きだ。— I like taking a walk at dusk.
3. 夕暮れとともに気温が下がってきた。— The temperature dropped as evening fell.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 夕暮れ has a more poetic, literary flavor than the everyday 夕方; it's often used in descriptive/narrative writing (novels, lyrics) to evoke the visual and emotional atmosphere of dusk, rather than simply stating the time of day.

## 友好 (ゆうこう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** friendship, amity (especially between groups/nations)

**Similar words:**
- [友情](#友情-ゆうじょう)（ゆうじょう）— "friendship," used for personal relationships between individuals, while 友好 is typically used for nations/organizations.
- 親善（しんぜん）— "goodwill," similar formal usage for diplomatic/international relations.
- 和親（わしん）— "amity, peace," a more historical/formal term for peaceful relations between states.

**Opposite words:**
- 敵対（てきたい）— hostility, antagonism

**Example sentences:**
1. 両国は友好関係を築いている。— The two countries are building friendly relations.
2. 友好的な雰囲気の中で会談が行われた。— The talks were held in a friendly atmosphere.
3. 友好都市として姉妹提携を結んだ。— They formed a sister-city partnership as friendship cities.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 友好 is specifically used for relations between nations, organizations, or cities (友好関係, 友好都市), not for personal friendships between individuals—for that, 友情 is the correct word.

## 融資 (ゆうし)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** financing, a loan, lending of funds

**Similar words:**
- 貸付（かしつけ）— "lending, loan," a more general/neutral term for lending money.
- ローン — loanword "loan," often used for personal/consumer loans (car loan, mortgage).
- 投資（とうし）— "investment," distinct from 融資 as it implies ownership stake rather than a repayable loan.

**Opposite words:**
- 返済（へんさい）— repayment

**Example sentences:**
1. 銀行から融資を受けて事業を始めた。— I started the business with financing from the bank.
2. 中小企業向けの融資制度がある。— There's a financing system for small and medium-sized businesses.
3. 融資の審査に時間がかかっている。— The loan screening is taking time.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 融資 specifically refers to financial institutions (banks) lending money to businesses/individuals for a purpose (often business financing), distinct from ローン which is more commonly used for everyday consumer loans like car loans or mortgages.

## 優秀 (ゆうしゅう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** excellence, outstanding, superior (ability/quality)

**Similar words:**
- [優れる](vocab-22.md#優れる-すぐれる)（すぐれる）— the verb form "to excel, be superior," while 優秀 is the na-adjective describing excellence.
- 卓越（たくえつ）— "excellence, preeminence," more formal/literary, implies being far above others.
- [素晴らしい](vocab-22.md#素晴らしい-すばらしい)（すばらしい）— "wonderful, splendid," broader praise term, not limited to ability/competence.

**Opposite words:**
- [劣る](vocab-10.md#劣る-おとる)（おとる）— to be inferior

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は優秀な成績で卒業した。— He graduated with excellent grades.
2. この会社は優秀な人材を求めている。— This company is looking for talented personnel.
3. 彼女は優秀なエンジニアだ。— She is an excellent engineer.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 優秀 is commonly used to describe people's abilities/performance (優秀な学生, 優秀な人材) rather than objects or situations; it emphasizes competence and achievement rather than general positive quality (for which 素晴らしい is more apt).

## 優勝 (ゆうしょう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a victory, championship, winning first place

**Similar words:**
- 勝利（しょうり）— "victory," a more general term for winning, not necessarily implying first place in a tournament.
- 優勝する（ゆうしょうする）— the verb form, "to win the championship."
- 制覇（せいは）— "conquest, domination," often used for winning a championship/tournament with a stronger connotation of total dominance.

**Opposite words:**
- 敗退（はいたい）— elimination, defeat

**Example sentences:**
1. チームは全国大会で優勝した。— The team won the championship at the national tournament.
2. 彼は三年連続で優勝している。— He has won the championship three years in a row.
3. 優勝おめでとうございます。— Congratulations on winning the championship.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 優勝 specifically means winning first place/the championship in a competition, distinct from 勝利 which just means "a win" (could be any match, not necessarily the whole tournament); don't use 優勝 for a single game win within a larger series.

## 友人 (ゆうじん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** friend, companion (formal register)

**Similar words:**
- 友達（ともだち）— the everyday, casual word for "friend," much more commonly used in speech.
- [知人](vocab-27.md#知人-ちじん)（ちじん）— "acquaintance," a more distant relationship than a true friend.
- 仲間（なかま）— "companion, comrade," emphasizes shared activity/purpose rather than general friendship.

**Opposite words:**
- 敵（てき）— enemy

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は私の大切な友人です。— He is my precious friend.
2. 友人の結婚式に出席した。— I attended my friend's wedding.
3. 古くからの友人と再会した。— I reunited with an old friend.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 友人 is more formal/written than 友達, appropriate for speeches, writing, or formal introductions; in casual daily conversation, 友達 is the natural choice, so using 友人 with close friends in casual speech can sound overly formal.

## 夕立 (ゆうだち)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a sudden shower (typically in summer evening)

**Similar words:**
- にわか雨（にわかあめ）— "sudden shower," a near-synonym usable any season, while 夕立 specifically implies a summer evening shower.
- 雷雨（らいう）— "thunderstorm," often accompanies a 夕立 but specifically emphasizes thunder/lightning.
- スコール — loanword "squall," often used for tropical sudden downpours.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 夏の午後、突然夕立が降ってきた。— A sudden shower came down suddenly on a summer afternoon.
2. 夕立のあとは涼しくなる。— It gets cooler after a summer shower.
3. 夕立に降られて濡れてしまった。— I got caught in a sudden shower and got soaked.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 夕立 specifically refers to the classic Japanese summer phenomenon of a brief, intense shower in the late afternoon/evening, often with thunder; it's seasonally and culturally specific, unlike the more generic にわか雨 which can occur in any season.

## 誘導 (ゆうどう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** guidance, inducement, leading (someone/something), derivation

**Similar words:**
- [案内](vocab-07.md#案内-あんない)（あんない）— "guidance, information," more about showing the way/providing information, less about actively directing.
- 導く（みちびく）— "to lead, guide," the verb form with a broader, often more positive/mentoring connotation.
- 誘導尋問（ゆうどうじんもん）— "leading question," a specific compound showing the manipulative potential of 誘導.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 係員が避難誘導を行った。— The staff member conducted evacuation guidance.
2. 彼は巧みに話を誘導した。— He skillfully steered the conversation.
3. この公式は別の式から誘導される。— This formula is derived from another equation.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 誘導 can have a neutral sense (避難誘導, guiding people during an evacuation) or a more manipulative/leading nuance (誘導尋問, leading question; 話を誘導する, to steer a conversation)—context determines whether it's benign guidance or subtle manipulation.

## 有能 (ゆうのう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** able, capable, competent

**Similar words:**
- [優秀](#優秀-ゆうしゅう)（ゆうしゅう）— emphasizes excelling above others, often academically; 有能 is more about practical job competence.
- 堪能（たんのう）— skilled/proficient at a specific skill (esp. language); narrower in scope than 有能.
- できる — casual, spoken equivalent of "capable," lacks the formal register of 有能.

**Opposite words:**
- 無能（むのう）— incompetent, incapable

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は有能な社員として評価されている。— He is regarded as a capable employee.
2. この会社は有能な人材を求めている。— This company is seeking capable talent.
3. 有能なリーダーがいれば、チームはもっとうまくいく。— With a capable leader, the team would do much better.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 有能 describes competence in a professional/organizational context (employees, leaders); it sounds odd applied to everyday skills like cooking — use 上手 or できる there instead.

#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** elegance, grace; graceful

**Similar words:**
- [優雅](vocab-02.md#優雅-ゆうが)（ゆうが）— elegant and unhurried, often implies leisure/refinement in lifestyle; 優美 focuses more on beauty of form/movement.

## 郵便 (ゆうびん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** mail, postal service

**Similar words:**
- 郵送（ゆうそう）— the act of mailing/sending something by post, a verb-like noun.
- 手紙（てがみ）— a letter itself, not the system/service.

**Similar words:**

- 強力（きょうりょく）— strong/powerful in terms of force or effect, more physical/mechanical.

## 夜 (よ)

#to-review

**Meaning:** a night, evening

**Similar words:**
- [夜](#夜-よ)（よる）— the much more common reading/usage of the same kanji for "night"; よ is an older/poetic or compound-only reading.
- 晩（ばん）— evening/night, used in everyday compounds like 今晩 (tonight).

**Example sentences:**
1. 夜（よ）もすがら星を眺めていた。— I gazed at the stars the whole night through.
2. 夜（よ）の静けさが心地よい。— The stillness of the night is pleasant.
3. 長き夜（よ）を一人で過ごした。— I spent the long night alone.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** The reading よ (as opposed to よる) appears mainly in classical/poetic expressions and fixed compounds (夜もすがら, 夜半 "よわ") — in everyday modern speech, 夜 is read よる.

## 夜明け (よあけ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** dawn, daybreak

**Similar words:**
- [明け方](vocab-06.md#明け方-あけがた)（あけがた）— a near-synonym for "the time around dawn," very close in meaning and usage.
- 日の出（ひので）— sunrise specifically (the moment the sun appears), narrower than 夜明け which covers the broader dawn period.

**Opposite words:**
- [夕暮れ](#夕暮れ-ゆうぐれ)（ゆうぐれ）— dusk, evening twilight

**Example sentences:**
1. 夜明けとともに鳥が鳴き始めた。— Birds began singing at daybreak.
2. 夜明け前に出発した。— We departed before dawn.
3. 長い戦いの夜明けが訪れた。— The dawn of a long struggle arrived (figuratively: a new beginning).

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 夜明け is also used figuratively for "the dawn/beginning of a new era" (新時代の夜明け) — don't limit its use to literal sunrise only.

## 良い/いい (よい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** good, excellent, fine, lucky, fortunate

**Similar words:**
- いい — the colloquial spoken form of 良い, used constantly in everyday conversation; 良い is used more in writing and formal/conjugated forms.
- [素晴らしい](vocab-22.md#素晴らしい-すばらしい)（すばらしい）— "wonderful/splendid," a stronger degree of "good" than 良い.

**Opposite words:**
- 悪い（わるい）— bad

**Example sentences:**
1. 今日は天気が良い。— The weather is good today.
2. それはいい考えだね。— That's a good idea.
3. 良い結果が出せて安心した。— I felt relieved to get a good result.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 良い and いい are the same word; いい is irregular and cannot conjugate directly (you must switch to 良い for past/negative forms: 良かった, 良くない, not いかった/いくない) — a classic beginner trap.

## よいしょ
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** heave-ho (exertion exclamation); also: to jolly/flatter someone

**Similar words:**

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** よいしょ has two very different uses — a physical exertion sound effect (like "heave-ho" or "oof") and, as よいしょする, a verb meaning "to flatter/butter up" someone — context makes clear which is meant.

- 衣料品店（いりょうひんてん）— a clothing store in general, not specifically Western-style.

**Example sentences:**

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 洋品店 is a somewhat old-fashioned term for a shop selling Western-style clothing/accessories, often evoking small, traditional neighborhood stores rather than modern fashion boutiques — it may sound dated to younger speakers.

## 翌~ (よく~)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** following, next (prefix for time)

**Similar words:**
- 次の（つぎの）— "next," a general everyday equivalent, while 翌~ is more formal/written and specifically temporal.
- 来~（らい~）— "next/coming" (来年, 来週), used for future time relative to now, whereas 翌~ is relative to a reference point in a narrative.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 翌~ is used relative to a point already established in the narrative (not necessarily "next" from today), unlike 来~ which always means "next" relative to the present moment — important distinction in storytelling/reports.
