# ～でもなんでもない {#でもなんでもない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～でも何でもない

**Furigana form:** ～でもなんでもない

**Meaning:** "Not X at all / is nothing of the sort" — flatly refutes that something/someone is a particular noun/category, usually correcting a false assumption.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞／な形容詞 + でもなんでもない
- Example base: 恋人 → 恋人でもなんでもない

**Typical context:** Spoken, casual-to-neutral register; used to firmly correct a mistaken assumption or rumor, often with mild irritation or emphasis.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**くもなんともない**](grammar-くもなんともない.md) — the parallel form for i-adjectives (e.g., 怖くもなんともない); functions identically but attaches to a different word class.
- **ではない** — plain "is not X"; a neutral negation, lacking the emphatic, rebuttal-driven "not X in the slightest" force of でもなんでもない.
- **なんかじゃない** — casual "it's not like X at all"; similar dismissive/refuting tone, often interchangeable in very casual speech.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; conceptually まさに～だ ("is exactly X") would affirm the category instead of refuting it.

**Example sentences:**
1. 違いますよ。川口さんは私の恋人でもなんでもありません。 (That's wrong. Mr. Kawaguchi is not my boyfriend at all.)
2. それは問題でもなんでもない、ただの誤解だ。 (That's not a problem at all, just a misunderstanding.)
3. 彼は専門家でもなんでもないのに、知ったかぶりをする。 (He's not an expert at all, yet he acts like he knows everything.)
4. この薬は効果があるどころか、薬でもなんでもない偽物だった。 (Far from being effective, this medicine turned out to be a total fake, not medicine at all.)
5. 彼女との関係は秘密でもなんでもなく、みんな知っている。 (My relationship with her isn't a secret at all — everyone knows.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Like くもなんともない, this construction works best as a direct rebuttal to an assumption, rumor, or label someone has attached to the subject — it reads oddly as a flat, context-free statement. Learners should remember it attaches to nouns/na-adjectives (でもなんでもない), while the i-adjective counterpart takes a different conjugation shape (くもなんともない) — mixing the two forms is a common error.
