# ～でなくてなんであろう {#でなくてなんであろう}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～でなくてなんであろう

**Furigana form:** ～でなくてなんであろう

**Meaning:** "If this isn't X, what is!" — a rhetorical, emphatic assertion that something is unmistakably X, used for dramatic effect.

**Conjugation:**
- N + でなくてなんであろう(か) — a fixed literary rhetorical-question form, often preceded by これが/それこそが to point at the thing being emphatically named

**Typical context:** Very formal, highly literary/rhetorical register, used in essays and speeches for dramatic emphasis. Virtually never used in casual spoken Japanese.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ではないか** — a milder rhetorical confirmation ("isn't it true that...?"), far more commonly spoken and usable in everyday conversation than でなくてなんであろう's dramatic, exclusively literary register.
- [**にほかならない**](grammar-にほかならない.md) — a declarative equivalent, "is nothing other than X," expressing the same core idea without the rhetorical question form — more measured and academic in tone than でなくてなんであろう's exclamatory flourish.
- [**きわまりない**](grammar-きわまる・きわまりない.md) — a different grammatical function (intensifying a quality directly via a na-adjective) rather than でなくてなんであろう's rhetorical nominal assertion.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 美しい妻とかわいい子供のいる生活、これが幸せでなくてなんであろう。
   (A life with a beautiful wife and an adorable child — if this isn't happiness, what is!)
2. 命をかけて他人を救う、これが勇気でなくてなんであろう。
   (Risking one's life to save another — if this isn't courage, what is!)
3. 何十年も研究を続けた末の発見、これが執念でなくてなんであろう。
   (A discovery after decades of continued research — if this isn't tenacity, what is!)
4. 見返りを求めず与え続けるその姿、これが愛でなくてなんであろう。
   (That figure who keeps giving without seeking anything in return — if this isn't love, what is!)
5. 一瞬にしてすべてを失ったこの光景、これが悲劇でなくてなんであろう。
   (This sight of losing everything in an instant — if this isn't tragedy, what is!)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** でなくてなんであろう is the most overtly rhetorical/dramatic member of the N1 extreme-degree cluster, and students often mistake it for the much milder ではないか because both are technically rhetorical questions. The key distinction: ではないか is usable in ordinary spoken confirmation ("isn't that right?"), while でなくてなんであろう is reserved for formal, written, highly emphatic declarations, almost always paired with これが/それこそが naming an abstract concept (幸せ, 勇気, 愛) — using it in casual speech would sound bizarrely theatrical.
