# ～いかんだ {#いかんだ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～いかんだ

**Furigana form:** ～いかんだ

**Meaning:** "It all depends on X" — expresses that whether something happens, or how a situation turns out, hinges entirely on the nature or condition of the preceding noun.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + いかんだ（名詞+の+いかんだ／いかんで／いかんでは、の形でもよく使われる）
- 試験の結果いかんで、合否が決まる — "whether you pass or fail depends on the exam results"

**Typical context:** Formal written register — news reports, official announcements, and business contexts discussing how outcomes depend on conditions such as support, results, or attitude.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**～次第だ**](grammar-次第だ.md) — "depends on X," very similar in meaning and considerably more common/less formal; いかんだ is more literary and formal.
- **～にかかっている** — "hinges on X," a similar dependency nuance with a slightly more dramatic, weighty register.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; stating that something is fixed regardless of conditions (～にかかわらず決まっている, "decided regardless of X") contrasts with this pattern's conditional dependency.

**Example sentences:**
1. 世界選手権大会をこの国で開催できるかどうかは、国民の協力いかんだ。
   (Whether this country can host the world championship depends entirely on the cooperation of its people.)
2. 筆記試験はパスした。あしたの面接の結果いかんで採用が決まるそうだ。
   (I passed the written exam. Apparently whether I get hired depends on how tomorrow's interview goes.)
3. 申し込み者数いかんでは、ツアーを中止しなければならないかもしれない。
   (Depending on the number of applicants, we may have to cancel the tour.)
4. 支持率いかんでは、今の政権も長くは続かないだろう。
   (Depending on approval ratings, the current administration may not last much longer.)
5. 今後の対応いかんによって、会社の信頼は大きく左右されるだろう。
   (Depending on how the company responds from here on, its credibility could be greatly affected.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern attaches to nouns that can vary in degree or kind (考え方, 成績, 態度, 結果, etc.), not to fixed, binary facts. What follows describes a possible or variable outcome, not something already certain. The ～いかんでは variant specifically introduces one of several possible scenarios that could occur depending on how things go.
