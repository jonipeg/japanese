# ～にもまして {#にもまして}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～にも増して

**Furigana form:** ～にもまして

**Meaning:** "More than X / even more than X" — compares a current degree or state against a stated reference point, asserting it surpasses even that.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + にも増して
- Example base: いつも → いつにも増して

**Typical context:** Formal-to-neutral, usable in both speech and writing; often used to emphasize that a current situation exceeds even an already high/notable prior benchmark (e.g., "more than ever before").

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **よりも** — plain "more than X"; the everyday, general-purpose comparison marker, while にも増して carries a slightly more formal, emphatic "surpassing even this already notable reference" nuance.
- **以上に** — "more than X / beyond X"; close in meaning and fairly common, somewhat more neutral/plain than にも増して's more literary flavor.
- **ひときわ** — "especially/notably more so"; an adverbial intensifier emphasizing a standout degree, often paired with にも増して for extra emphasis (いつにも増してひときわ美しい).

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～ほどではない ("not quite as much as X") expresses the reverse — falling short of a reference point rather than surpassing it.

**Example sentences:**
1. 今年の夏はいつにも増して暑い。 (This summer is hotter than ever before.)
2. 彼は以前にも増して仕事に打ち込んでいる。 (He's throwing himself into his work more than ever before.)
3. 困難な時期だからこそ、何にも増して家族の支えが大切だ。 (Precisely because it's a difficult time, family support matters more than anything else.)
4. 彼女は結婚してから、以前にも増して美しくなった。 (Since getting married, she has become more beautiful than ever before.)
5. 今回のプロジェクトは、これまでにも増して慎重な準備が求められる。 (This project requires more careful preparation than ever before.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にも増して always requires a stated reference point for comparison (いつ, 以前, これまで, 何) — it cannot stand alone meaning simply "a lot," since the whole point is surpassing a named baseline. Learners sometimes treat it as a plain intensifier like とても, but its core grammatical function is comparative, built on 増す ("to increase"), literally meaning "increasing even beyond X."
