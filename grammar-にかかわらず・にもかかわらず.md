# ～にかかわらず／にもかかわらず {#にかかわらず・にもかかわらず}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に関わらず／にも関わらず

**Furigana form:** ～にかかわらず／にもかかわらず

**Meaning:** にかかわらず = "regardless of X" (X being a range/choice, often paired items like 有無/晴雨); にもかかわらず = "despite X," "in spite of X" (expressing an unexpected or contrary result given the preceding fact).

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞／疑問詞＋か＋どうか + にかかわらず: 天気にかかわらず (regardless of the weather); 参加するかどうかにかかわらず (regardless of whether you participate)
- 名詞／普通形 + にもかかわらず: 雨にもかかわらず、試合は行われた。(Despite the rain, the game took place.)

**Typical context:** Formal, written and spoken; common in announcements, notices, and formal writing. にもかかわらず is often used to highlight a surprising or admirable contrast.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**を問わず**](grammar-を問わず.md) — "regardless of," very close to にかかわらず in the "regardless of a range of options" sense.
- **のに** — "even though/despite," more casual counterpart to にもかかわらず, with a nuance of complaint/surprise.
- **ながらも** — "although," also concessive but tied to the state of the subject rather than an external fact.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though から／ので (straightforward cause-effect) contrasts with the "despite" logic of にもかかわらず.

**Example sentences:**
1. 年齢にかかわらず、誰でも応募できます。
   (Regardless of age, anyone can apply.)
2. 天候にかかわらず、イベントは予定通り開催されます。
   (Regardless of the weather, the event will be held as scheduled.)
3. 彼は忙しいにもかかわらず、手伝ってくれた。
   (Despite being busy, he helped me.)
4. 努力したにもかかわらず、結果は出なかった。
   (Despite my efforts, I didn't get results.)
5. 経験の有無にかかわらず、応募を歓迎します。
   (Regardless of whether you have experience or not, we welcome applications.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にかかわらず is used with a range/pair of options (年齢, 天候, 有無, かどうか) to mean "no matter which," while にもかかわらず always follows a stated fact and means "despite that fact" — mixing these up is a very common N2 error. Note the different kanji readings are identical (関わらず) but usage/meaning differ based on the presence of も and sentence structure.
