# ～にしたら {#にしたら}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～にしたら

**Furigana form:** ～にしたら

**Meaning:** "From the standpoint of X / if it were X('s perspective)" — presents a situation as it would appear from a particular person's point of view, often used to show empathy or explain why someone might feel a certain way.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (person) + にしたら — 親にしたら (from a parent's standpoint)
- Variants: ～にすれば、～にしてみれば (same meaning, slightly different nuance of "if one tried putting oneself in that position")

**Typical context:** Neutral, spoken and written — used in everyday conversation and essays when considering another person's feelings or viewpoint.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**にとって**](grammar-にとって.md) — "for X (regarding impact/importance)," more about effect/relevance than imagined perspective/feeling.
- **の身になって** — "putting oneself in X's shoes," more explicit and emotionally empathetic phrasing.
- **にしてみれば** — near-synonym, slightly softer, "if you think about it from X's side."

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 親にしたら、子供がいくつになっても心配なものだ。
   (From a parent's standpoint, they worry about their children no matter how old they get.)
2. 彼にしたら、あの発言は許せなかったのだろう。
   (From his standpoint, that remark was probably unforgivable.)
3. 初心者にしたら、この説明は難しすぎる。
   (From a beginner's standpoint, this explanation is too difficult.)
4. 子供にしたら、毎日勉強させられるのは苦痛だろう。
   (From a child's point of view, being made to study every day must be painful.)
5. 会社にしたら、コスト削減は避けられない課題だ。
   (From the company's standpoint, cost-cutting is an unavoidable issue.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にしたら always takes a person/entity as its subject (whose perspective is being adopted), not an inanimate condition — don't confuse with にすると, which can describe a more mechanical "if we go by X" conclusion. The nuance is empathetic reasoning about another's likely feelings or view.
