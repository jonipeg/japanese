# ～っけ {#っけ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～っけ

**Furigana form:** ～っけ (no kanji)

**Meaning:** Casual sentence-final particle used to confirm or recall forgotten information — "wasn't it...?", "was it...?"

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞／い形容詞 plain form (past or non-past) + っけ
- 名詞／な形容詞 + だった／だ + っけ

**Typical context:** Very casual spoken Japanese, used with close friends or family, often in self-directed musing.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **よね** — seeking confirmation generally, without the specific "forgotten/recalled information" nuance.
- **かな** — wondering/musing, but not specifically about recalling forgotten facts.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 明日は何時に集合だったっけ?
   (What time were we supposed to meet tomorrow again?)
2. この映画、もう見たっけ?
   (Did I already watch this movie?)
3. 彼の誕生日、いつだったっけ?
   (When was his birthday again?)
4. あれ、鍵はどこに置いたっけ?
   (Huh, where did I put my keys again?)
5. 今日って何曜日だっけ?
   (What day is it today again?)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** っけ is strictly casual/informal and inappropriate in formal or business contexts. Learners sometimes forget the required だった before っけ when attaching it to nouns or na-adjectives (e.g., 静かだったっけ, not 静かっけ).
