# ～わけがない {#わけがない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～訳がない

**Furigana form:** ～わけがない (訳 read as わけ)

**Meaning:** "There's no way that..., it's impossible that..." — expresses strong confidence/conviction that something is logically impossible or extremely unlikely, based on the speaker's reasoning.

**Conjugation:**
- 普通形 (plain form) + わけがない
- 動詞/い形容詞普通形 + わけがない: できるわけがない (there's no way it's possible)
- な形容詞 + な + わけがない: 簡単なわけがない (there's no way it's easy)
- 名詞 + の + わけがない / である + わけがない: 本物のわけがない (there's no way it's genuine)

**Typical context:** Spoken and written, neutral-to-slightly emphatic register. Used to firmly deny a possibility, often in argument or rebuttal.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**わけではない**](grammar-わけではない.md) — a softer partial negation ("it's not that..."), very different meaning from わけがない's strong denial of possibility.
- **はずがない** — nearly synonymous, "there's no way/it shouldn't be possible," slightly more about expectation than logic.
- **〜ないだろう/〜ないでしょう** — more tentative, less emphatic denial.

**Opposite grammar structures:** [**わけだ**](grammar-わけだ.md) (logical conclusion, affirms something follows naturally) is a structural counterpart in the わけ family, though not a direct semantic opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. こんな短時間で全部終わるわけがない。
   (There's no way we can finish everything in such a short time.)
2. 彼が嘘をつくわけがない。彼はそんな人じゃない。
   (There's no way he'd lie — he's not that kind of person.)
3. 初心者がプロに勝てるわけがない。
   (There's no way a beginner can beat a professional.)
4. あんなに勉強していたのだから、試験に落ちるわけがない。
   (Since he studied that much, there's no way he'd fail the exam.)
5. この値段で本物のダイヤモンドなわけがない。
   (There's no way a genuine diamond would be this price.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Don't confuse わけがない (strong logical impossibility) with わけではない (partial, softer negation meaning "it doesn't mean that..."). わけがない flatly denies possibility with conviction; わけではない merely qualifies a previous statement without total denial.
