# ～かぎりだ {#かぎりだ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～限りだ

**Furigana form:** ～かぎりだ

**Meaning:** "Extremely X" — used with emotion adjectives to express the speaker's own feeling at its peak (うらやましい限りだ, "I'm extremely envious"; 嬉しい限りだ, "I'm so happy").

**Conjugation:**
- い-adjective/な-adjective + 限りだ

**Typical context:** Formal-leaning register, appearing in both written contexts and somewhat formal spoken expression of personal feelings strongly.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**のきわみ**](grammar-のきわみ.md) — describes the peak of an emotion noun in a more formal, objective-sounding way (感動の極み), compared to かぎりだ's adjective-based, more personal exclamation.
- [**のいたり**](grammar-のいたり.md) — a humble, self-referential extreme feeling tied to ceremonial fixed phrases (光栄の至り), narrower in application than かぎりだ's more general personal feeling expression.
- [**てやまない**](grammar-てやまない.md) — expresses a continuous, ongoing wish via a verb, in contrast to かぎりだ's momentary intense feeling expressed via an adjective.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 働かなくても生活できるなんて、うらやましい限りだ。
   (Being able to live without working — how extremely enviable.)
2. 皆様に祝っていただき、嬉しい限りです。
   (Being celebrated by everyone like this makes me extremely happy.)
3. こんな大失敗をしてしまうとは、情けない限りだ。
   (Making such a huge blunder is extremely pathetic.)
4. 一人で海外赴任するなんて、心細い限りだ。
   (Being posted abroad alone is extremely unsettling.)
5. 若くして成功するとは、羨ましい限りだ。
   (Succeeding at such a young age is extremely enviable.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** かぎりだ is a frequent member of the N1 extreme-degree cluster and is often confused with のきわみ and のいたり because all three translate loosely as "extremely X" or "the height of X." The key test: かぎりだ attaches to an ADJECTIVE (うらやましい, 嬉しい) to express the SPEAKER'S OWN current feeling, while のきわみ/のいたり attach to EMOTION NOUNS (感動, 光栄) in a more formal, ceremonial, or objective description — mixing up adjective vs. noun attachment is the most common error.
