# ～におけるN {#におけるN}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に於けるN

**Furigana form:** ～におけるN

**Meaning:** "N in/at X" — marks a domain, location, time, or field within which a following noun is being discussed; functions as a formal attributive version of "in X."

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + における + 名詞 (noun + における + noun)
- Example base: 情報通信社会 → 情報通信社会における重要な課題

**Typical context:** Formal, written register; extremely common in academic writing, reports, essays, and formal speeches to specify the context/domain of a discussion. Rare in casual speech.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **での** — plain "in/at X" attributive marker (e.g., 学校での生活, "life at school"); the everyday, casual-to-neutral equivalent, while における is distinctly more formal and written.
- [**において**](grammar-において.md) — the adverbial (non-attributive, sentence-medial) counterpart used before a verb rather than directly modifying a following noun (e.g., 会議において発表する, "present at the meeting"); における is used specifically when the phrase directly modifies a noun.
- **にかんする** — "concerning/regarding X"; also formal and noun-modifying, but にかんする specifies the topic/subject matter of the following noun, while における specifies the domain/context/location within which the following noun exists.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a general, unmarked topic reference (ただのN, "just N" without context specification) would contrast with における's function of anchoring N to a specific domain.

**Example sentences:**
1. 個人情報をいかに守るかは情報通信社会における重要な課題だ。 (How to protect personal information is an important issue in the information and communication society.)
2. 江戸時代における文化の発展について論じる。 (This discusses the development of culture during the Edo period.)
3. 日本における少子化問題は深刻さを増している。 (The declining birthrate problem in Japan is becoming increasingly serious.)
4. ビジネスにおける信頼関係は何よりも重要だ。 (Trust relationships in business are more important than anything else.)
5. この研究は医療現場における課題を明らかにした。 (This research clarified issues within the context of medical practice.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** における is reserved for formal, written-style noun modification and should not be substituted casually for での in everyday speech, where it sounds stiff and overly academic. Learners should also distinguish における (modifies a following noun directly) from において (modifies a following verb/clause) — mixing up where each attaches is a very common error on grammar tests.
