# ～に至っては {#に至っては}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～に至っては

**Furigana form:** ～にいたっては

**Meaning:** "When it comes to this extreme example (among several)..." — singles out the most extreme case within a broader negative trend and comments on how bad or striking that specific case is.

**Conjugation:**
- 名 + に至っては
- Aデパートに至っては — "when it comes to Department Store A, specifically"

**Typical context:** Formal written register — essays and commentary that cite an extreme case within a larger negative trend, often with a tone of exasperation.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**～ともなると**](grammar-ともなると・ともなれば.md) — also frames "when it comes to X" as an extreme or special case, but doesn't require the case to be negative.
- **とりわけ／特に** — plain "especially" — lacks the "most extreme representative example drawn from a larger group" structure that に至っては has.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a softening phrase like 中には～もある ("some among them are...") would hedge rather than single out an extreme case.

**Example sentences:**
1. デパートの閉店が相次いでいる。Aデパートに至ってはすでに三つの支店が閉店した。
   (Department stores are closing one after another. Department Store A, in particular, has already closed three of its branches.)
2. 毎年この地方は洪水の被害を受ける。長崎県に至っては今年もう3回目だ。
   (This region suffers flood damage every year. Nagasaki Prefecture, for one, has already been hit three times this year.)
3. わたしは理数系の科目が不得意だった。物理に至っては全く理解できなかった。
   (I was never good at science and math subjects. Physics, in particular, I couldn't understand at all.)
4. 最近の若者はテレビを見なくなった。十代に至っては、ほとんどテレビを持っていない家庭も多い。
   (Young people nowadays have stopped watching TV. Among teenagers in particular, many households don't even own one.)
5. この工場は人手不足に悩んでいる。夜勤に至っては、担当できる社員が一人もいない状態だ。
   (This factory is plagued by a labor shortage. When it comes to the night shift specifically, there isn't a single employee who can cover it.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Requires a broader negative context to have already been established (a general trend), and then singles out the single worst or most extreme example from within it — it isn't used to introduce an isolated example with no larger trend behind it. Carries a tone of exasperation or amazement at how bad the extreme case turns out to be.
