# ～に応えて {#に応えて}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に応えて

**Furigana form:** ～にこたえて

**Meaning:** "In response to X" — indicates an action taken to meet, fulfill, or answer an expectation, request, demand, or wish.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に応えて — 期待に応えて (in response to/meeting expectations)
- Noun-modifying form: 名詞 + に応える + 名詞 — 期待に応える結果 (results that meet expectations)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, common in speeches, news, and business/sports contexts — e.g., athletes responding to fans' expectations, companies responding to customer demand.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **にこたえる (答える vs 応える)** — 答える is for literally "answering" a question; 応える is for "responding to/meeting" an expectation, request, or feeling — different kanji, different nuance.
- **を受けて** — "in response to/following X (an event or statement)," more neutral and event-triggered rather than expectation-fulfilling.
- [**に沿って**](grammar-に沿って.md) — "in line with X (a plan/policy)," expresses conformity rather than active response/fulfillment.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though 期待を裏切る ("betray expectations") is a thematic opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 観客の声援に応えて、選手たちは全力でプレーした。
   (In response to the crowd's cheers, the players gave their all.)
2. 消費者のニーズに応えて、新しい商品を開発した。
   (In response to consumer needs, we developed a new product.)
3. 先生の期待に応えようと、彼は毎日遅くまで勉強した。
   (Trying to meet his teacher's expectations, he studied late every day.)
4. 多くの要望に応えて、営業時間を延長することになった。
   (In response to many requests, we decided to extend business hours.)
5. 彼女は両親の期待に応えて、医学部に合格した。
   (Meeting her parents' expectations, she got into medical school.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Be careful not to confuse 応える (meet/respond to an expectation or feeling) with 答える (answer a question) — they're both read こたえる but have distinct kanji and usage; に応えて always implies actively fulfilling or living up to something, not just giving a verbal reply.
