# ～限り / ～に限り / ～に限る {#限り・に限り・に限る}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～限り / ～に限り / ～に限る

**Furigana form:** ～かぎり / ～にかぎり / ～にかぎる

**Meaning:**
- **限り** — "as long as / to the extent that" (sets a boundary/condition — covered in depth separately for its ～限り(は) conditional usage).
- **に限り** — "limited to / only in the case of" — formal, often seen on signs/notices restricting something to a specific group or time.
- **に限る** — "the best thing is to ~ / nothing beats ~" — an opinion that X is simply the best option, often used casually.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に限り — 例: 本日に限り (limited to today only)
- 名詞 + に限る — 例: 夏はビールに限る (in summer, nothing beats beer)
- 動詞辞書形 + に限る — 例: 疲れた時は寝るに限る (when tired, the best thing is to sleep)

**Typical context:** に限り is formal and written (signs, official notices, sales promotions). に限る is more casual/spoken, used to state a personal strong opinion or common wisdom.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**だけ**](grammar-だけ.md) — simple "only," lacks the formal/restrictive nuance of に限り.
- **が一番だ** — "is the best," a plainer way to express the same idea as に限る without the idiomatic flavor.
- [**しかない**](grammar-しかない.md) — "have no choice but," different nuance (lack of alternatives rather than personal preference).

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though 以外は (except for) contrasts with the restrictive に限り.

**Example sentences:**
1. 本日に限り、全商品を20%引きにします。
   (Only today, all items are 20% off.)
2. この割引券は平日に限り使用できます。
   (This discount coupon can only be used on weekdays.)
3. 疲れた時は、温かいお風呂に入るに限る。
   (When you're tired, nothing beats taking a warm bath.)
4. 夏の暑い日はやっぱり冷たいビールに限る。
   (On hot summer days, nothing beats a cold beer.)
5. この会員特典は新規登録者に限り適用されます。
   (This membership benefit applies only to new registrants.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners confuse に限り (restrictive condition, formal/neutral) with に限る (subjective "best option," informal opinion) — they look similar but serve very different functions; に限り never expresses a personal preference.
