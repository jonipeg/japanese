# ～さえ {#さえ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～さえ

**Furigana form:** ～さえ (no special kanji reading)

**Meaning:** "Even X" — emphasizes an extreme or surprising example to imply that if even this case applies, other (more expected) cases apply even more strongly.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + さえ
- Verb (ます stem) + さえする／Verb-て + さえいる
- 子供でさえ分かる (Even a child can understand)
- 彼は挨拶さえしなかった (He didn't even greet [us])

**Typical context:** Both speech and writing; used to express surprise, criticism, or emphasis on a minimal/extreme threshold.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**も**](grammar-にかけては・も.md) — general "also/even," weaker and more neutral emphasis than さえ.
- [**すら**](grammar-すら.md) — nearly synonymous with さえ, slightly more literary/formal, often used in negative or critical statements.
- [**まで**](grammar-まで(のことをして).md) — "even to the extent of X," emphasizes going further than expected, similar nuance but different connotation (extent rather than minimal example).

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentessences:**
1. 彼はお礼の言葉さえ言わなかった。
   (He didn't even say a word of thanks.)
2. そんなことは子供でさえ知っている。
   (Even a child knows something like that.)
3. 忙しすぎて、昼ご飯を食べる時間さえなかった。
   (I was so busy that I didn't even have time to eat lunch.)
4. 彼女は自分の名前さえ忘れてしまったようだ。
   (It seems she has even forgotten her own name.)
5. 先生にさえ分からない問題を、彼は解いてしまった。
   (He solved a problem that even the teacher couldn't understand.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** さえ highlights an extreme/minimal case to imply "if this, then surely everything else too" — distinct from さえ～ば (conditional "if only"), which is a separate pattern. Don't confuse the emphatic さえ with the "as long as" construction さえ～ば.
