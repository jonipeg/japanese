# ～からして {#からして}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～からして

**Furigana form:** ～からして

**Meaning:** "Judging from just this one example / even X alone (suggests the rest follows suit)" — cites a single representative example as evidence for a broader (often critical) judgment.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + からして

**Typical context:** Written and spoken, neutral-to-slightly-critical register — commonly used when pointing out a flaw or forming a negative impression based on one telling example.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **から見ても** — "even judging from X," a similar evidentiary function, but less emphatic as "the very first piece of evidence."
- **だけでも** — "just by X alone," general emphasis on a single sufficient piece of evidence, without からして's specifically judgmental tone.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. あの態度からして、彼はやる気がないのだろう。
   (Judging just from that attitude, he probably has no motivation.)
2. この店は名前からして怪しい。
   (This shop is suspicious judging from its name alone.)
3. 彼の言葉遣いからして、育ちの良さが分かる。
   (Judging just from his choice of words, you can tell he was raised well.)
4. 第一印象からして、この計画はうまくいかないと思った。
   (Just from the first impression, I thought this plan wouldn't work.)
5. 彼女の荷物の量からして、長期旅行に行くようだ。
   (Judging just from the amount of her luggage, it seems she's going on a long trip.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** からして often carries a slightly negative or critical nuance ("even this one thing reveals the whole picture") — avoid using it for completely neutral evidence that doesn't imply a broader conclusion.
