# ～にかかわる {#にかかわる}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に関わる

**Furigana form:** ～にかかわる

**Meaning:** "Affects/concerns X (often seriously, a matter of X)" — describes something that has a significant bearing on a serious matter, often implying high stakes (life, honor, safety, credibility).

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に関わる + 名詞／動詞
- Example base: 命 → 命に関わる問題

**Typical context:** Formal-to-neutral, usable in both speech and writing; frequently used for serious or grave matters (life, death, reputation, safety), carrying a weighty, consequential tone.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **に関する／に関して** — "concerning/regarding X"; a much more neutral topic-marking construction for any subject matter, lacking に関わる's implication of serious stakes or consequence.
- **にかかる** (homophone-adjacent but different kanji 掛かる) — unrelated in meaning ("to take time/cost," "to hang"); learners should not confuse 関わる (to be involved in/affect) with 掛かる.
- **を左右する** — "to determine/sway X"; shares the "has a significant impact on" sense but focuses on influencing an outcome's direction, while に関わる more broadly signals that something is at stake or connected to a serious matter.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～とは無関係だ ("is unrelated to X") expresses the reverse — having no bearing on the matter.

**Example sentences:**
1. これは会社の信用に関わる重大な問題だ。 (This is a serious matter that affects the company's credibility.)
2. 命に関わる病気なので、すぐに治療が必要だ。 (It's a life-threatening illness, so immediate treatment is necessary.)
3. その発言は彼の評判に関わるものだった。 (That remark was something that affected his reputation.)
4. 安全に関わることなので、慎重に判断すべきだ。 (Since it concerns safety, a careful judgment should be made.)
5. この決定は社員全員の将来に関わってくる。 (This decision will have a bearing on the future of all employees.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に関わる typically signals that something carries serious consequences or is tied to an important stake (life, death, honor, safety, credibility) — using it for a trivial, low-stakes topic sounds overly dramatic; に関する would be more appropriate there. Don't confuse the kanji 関わる (to be involved in/have a bearing on) with 係る (a formal bureaucratic synonym) or 掛かる (to take time/cost, to hang) — these share readings or spellings but differ in nuance and usage.
