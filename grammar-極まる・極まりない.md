# ～極まる・～極まりない {#極まる・極まりない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～極まる・～極まりない

**Furigana form:** ～きわまる・きわまりない

**Meaning:** Describes a state or quality as being at its absolute extreme — "extremely ~," "~ in the extreme." Formal, written register.

**Conjugation:**
- ナ形 + 極まる
- ナ形（なこと）・イ形いこと + 極まりない
- 失礼極まりない — "extremely rude"

**Typical context:** Written, formal/literary register — common in newspaper commentary, formal criticism, and written expressions of strong evaluative judgment.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **～の極み（きわみ）** — similarly means "the height/extreme of ~," attaches to nouns instead of na-adjectives, and is slightly more literary.
- **～てしょうがない／～てたまらない** — express an intense personal feeling, but describe the speaker's own sensation rather than objectively evaluating a state of affairs.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite pattern; a mild/neutral evaluation would simply drop 極まる／極まりない and use the plain adjective form.

**Example sentences:**
1. このような不当な判決が出たことは、残念極まる。
   (That such an unjust ruling was handed down is extremely regrettable.)
2. 退屈極まる日常から抜け出したいと、彼は一人旅に出た。
   (Wanting to escape his utterly tedious daily life, he set off on a solo trip.)
3. ついに初優勝を決めたその選手は、インタビュー中、感極まって涙を流した。
   (Having finally secured his first championship, the athlete was overcome with emotion and shed tears during the interview.)
4. 気に入らない相手には返事もしないなんて、あの人の態度は失礼極まりない。
   (Not even replying to someone you dislike — that person's attitude is extremely rude.)
5. 失業している上、子供にもお金がかかり、生活が苦しいこと極まりない。
   (On top of being unemployed, with money needed for the children too, life is extremely hard.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern attaches mostly to na-adjectives of Chinese origin (漢語) that carry a negative evaluation (残念・失礼・退屈など) — it sounds unnatural with ordinary positive adjectives. Note the two forms: 極まる attaches directly to the na-adjective stem, while 極まりない needs な before a noun-modifying use, or こと after an i-adjective. The idiom 感極まる ("to be overcome with emotion") is a fixed expression describing a temporary surge of strong feeling, distinct from the general pattern.
