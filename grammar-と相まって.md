# ～と相まって {#と相まって}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～と相まって

**Furigana form:** ～とあいまって

**Meaning:** Combined with ~, interacting together with ~, resulting in an intensified effect or synergy — "coupled with ~," "together with ~."

**Conjugation:**
- 元の動詞：相俟つ（あいまつ、現代では使わない古語）
- 文法形式：名 + と相まって
- 努力と相まって — "coupled with (his) hard work"

**Typical context:** Written, formal register — common in essays, reviews, and analytical writing describing how two or more factors combine to produce a result.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**～に加えて**](grammar-に加えて.md) — similar "in addition to," but simply additive, without implying the two factors interact or synergize.
- [**～とともに**](grammar-とともに.md) — "together with," more neutral in tone, lacking the nuance of a combined effect being produced.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; describing a single factor acting in isolation would simply omit と相まって.

**Example sentences:**
1. あの白い建物は美しい緑と相まって、絵本に出てくるお城のように見える。
   (That white building, combined with the beautiful greenery around it, looks like a castle out of a picture book.)
2. この梅ジャムは、梅本来の酸味と相まったほどよい甘さが特徴です。
   (This plum jam is characterized by a pleasant sweetness that combines with the plum's natural tartness.)
3. 彼の実力は努力と相まって、素晴らしい結果を生んだ。
   (His talent, combined with his hard work, produced wonderful results.)
4. 円安が原材料価格の高騰と相まって、企業の利益を圧迫している。
   (The weak yen, coupled with soaring raw material prices, is squeezing corporate profits.)
5. 新しい広告戦略が口コミと相まって、売り上げが大きく伸びた。
   (The new advertising strategy, combined with word of mouth, greatly increased sales.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This is a formal, written-only expression built from the archaic verb 相俟つ, which is never used on its own in modern Japanese — only in the fixed form と相まって. The noun before と相まって is usually an already-established fact or feature, and the whole phrase typically modifies a following clause or noun describing the combined/resulting effect, not a simple list of unrelated items.
