# ～せいで {#せいで}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～せいで

**Furigana form:** ～せいで (written in kana; the kanji 所為 exists but is rarely used)

**Meaning:** "Because of X" — attributes a negative result or outcome to a cause, often with a nuance of blame or fault.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (noun) + の + せいで
- な形容詞 (na-adjective) + な + せいで
- い形容詞 (i-adjective, plain form) + せいで
- 動詞 (verb, plain form, past or non-past) + せいで

**Typical context:** Neutral register, used in both spoken and written Japanese, commonly when assigning blame or explaining an unfortunate result.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ため(に)** — neutral "because of/due to," can be positive, negative, or neutral — lacks the blame nuance of せいで.
- [**おかげで**](grammar-おかげで.md) — "thanks to," the positive counterpart, used for good outcomes.
- **のせいにする** — "to blame X for," the related verb form for actively assigning fault.

**Opposite grammar structures:** おかげで ("thanks to X," positive outcome).

**Example sentences:**
1. 雨のせいで、試合が中止になった。
   (Because of the rain, the match was cancelled.)
2. 彼が遅刻したせいで、電車に乗り遅れた。
   (Because he was late, we missed the train.)
3. 準備不足のせいで、プレゼンがうまくいかなかった。
   (Because of insufficient preparation, the presentation didn't go well.)
4. 寝不足のせいで、頭痛がする。
   (Because of lack of sleep, I have a headache.)
5. 景気が悪いせいで、就職が難しい。
   (Because the economy is bad, finding a job is difficult.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners often confuse せいで (negative, blame) with ため(に) (neutral cause) and おかげで (positive cause). おかげで can also be used sarcastically for a bad outcome, but せいで remains the default, non-ironic choice for negative causation.
