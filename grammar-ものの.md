# ～ものの {#ものの}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ものの

**Furigana form:** ～ものの (no special kanji reading)

**Meaning:** "Although..., even though..." — a formal concessive conjunction. States a fact, then notes a following fact that contrasts with what would normally be expected from it.

**Conjugation:**
- 普通形 (plain form of verbs/adjectives) + ものの
- 動詞た形 + ものの: 合格したものの (although I passed...)
- い形容詞 + ものの: 高いものの (although it's expensive...)
- な形容詞 + な + ものの: 便利なものの (although it's convenient...)
- 名詞 + である + ものの: 社会人であるものの (although I'm a working adult...)

**Typical context:** Formal, written register — common in essays, news reports, and formal speech. More literary than colloquial だけど/けど.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **のに** — also concessive, but carries a stronger nuance of surprise/dissatisfaction; more spoken.
- **けれども/が** — more neutral, general-purpose "but," usable in both speech and writing.
- [**にもかかわらず**](grammar-にかかわらず・にもかかわらず.md) — "despite X," more formal and emphatic than ものの.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though ～ので (straightforward causal "because/so") lacks the contrastive turn.

**Example sentences:**
1. 試験に合格したものの、思ったほど嬉しくなかった。
   (Although I passed the exam, I wasn't as happy as I'd expected.)
2. 新しい仕事は給料がいいものの、残業が多い。
   (Although the new job pays well, there's a lot of overtime.)
3. 謝罪したものの、彼女の怒りは収まらなかった。
   (Although I apologized, her anger didn't subside.)
4. この部屋は駅から近いものの、家賃が高い。
   (Although this room is close to the station, the rent is high.)
5. 計画は立てたものの、実行に移す勇気が出なかった。
   (Although I made the plan, I couldn't muster the courage to carry it out.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners sometimes use ものの in casual conversation where のに or けど would be more natural — ものの sounds stiff/written in casual speech. Also note ものの always follows the plain form and cannot directly follow a noun without である; don't confuse with 〜とはいうものの (a related but distinct fixed expression meaning "having said that...").
