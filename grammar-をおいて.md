# ～をおいて {#をおいて}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～をおいて

**Furigana form:** ～をおいて

**Meaning:** "Other than X, there's nothing/no one comparable" — used to emphatically single out something or someone as uniquely the best or most suitable, with no real alternative in the same league.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + をおいて
- この仕事ができるのは彼をおいて他にいない — "there's no one but him who can do this job"

**Typical context:** Written and formal spoken register — used in speeches, recommendations, and praise, when emphatically asserting that someone or something is uniquely qualified or suitable.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **～しかいない／～しかない** — plain "there's only X," conveys exclusivity but without the evaluative, praising nuance of を置いて.
- **～に勝るものはない** — "nothing surpasses X," a similarly literary superlative expression of praise.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; plainly stating that there are many alternatives (ほかにもたくさんある) would contrast with this pattern's claim of uniqueness.

**Example sentences:**
1. 今、こんな素晴らしい色使いの染色ができる人は、彼をおいてほかにいない。
   (There's no one else, besides him, who can dye fabric with such wonderful use of color today.)
2. 日本で世界的な平和会議を行うなら、広島か長崎をおいてほかに候補地は考えられない。
   (If a world peace conference were to be held in Japan, no location other than Hiroshima or Nagasaki could be considered.)
3. 毎年夏にはこのホテルに来ている。心からくつろげる場所はここをおいてほかにない。
   (I come to this hotel every summer — there's nowhere else I can relax so completely.)
4. このチームをまとめられるリーダーは彼女をおいて他にいないだろう。
   (There's probably no one but her who could lead this team together.)
5. 国内でこの技術を持つ会社は、あの企業をおいてほかに見当たらない。
   (Domestically, there's no company with this technology other than that one.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern only attaches to something the speaker genuinely holds in high regard — it's inherently a statement of praise and uniqueness, not a neutral statement of exclusion. It must be followed by a negative (いない／ない), since the whole point is that nothing else compares.
