## ~海 (~かい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** sea of ~ (suffix, e.g., 地中海 Mediterranean Sea, 日本海 Sea of Japan)

**Similar words:**
- 海（うみ）— the standalone word for "sea/ocean," read うみ when independent versus かい as a suffix in proper nouns.
- 洋（よう）— another suffix for large bodies of water, used for oceans (太平洋 — Pacific Ocean), larger in scale than ~海.

**Example sentences:**
1. 地中海沿岸はリゾート地として人気がある。— The Mediterranean coast is popular as a resort area.
2. 日本海側は冬に雪が多い。— The Sea of Japan side gets a lot of snow in winter.
3. 瀬戸内海には多くの島がある。— The Seto Inland Sea has many islands.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** As a suffix in proper nouns, 海 is read かい (地中海, 日本海), not うみ — learners who default to the independent reading うみ will mispronounce these place names.

## ~外 (~がい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** out of ~, outside of ~ (suffix, e.g., 問題外, 専門外)

**Similar words:**
- ~外れ（~はずれ）— "out of/off ~" as a native-reading suffix (期待外れ — disappointing/falling short of expectation), a softer parallel construction.
- 以外（いがい）— "other than, except for," a related but grammatically distinct construction (usually following a noun + の/the noun itself).

**Example sentences:**
1. そんな要求は問題外だ。— Such a demand is out of the question.
2. それは私の専門外の分野です。— That's a field outside my specialty.
3. 予想外の結果に驚いた。— I was surprised by the unexpected result.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** As a suffix, ~外 is read がい (問題外, 専門外, 予想外) and attaches directly to nouns to mean "outside the scope of ~" — distinct from the prefix 外~ (がい~, meaning "foreign/outside-") which attaches before a noun instead of after.

## 害 (がい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** harm, damage

**Similar words:**
- 被害（ひがい）— "damage/harm suffered," specifically from the victim's perspective (被害を受ける), whereas 害 is more general.
- [損害](vocab-24.md#損害-そんがい)（そんがい）— "damage/loss," often financial or material, more concrete than general 害.

**Opposite words:**
- [利益](vocab-33.md#利益-りえき)（りえき）— benefit, profit
- 益（えき）— benefit

**Example sentences:**
1. タバコは健康に害がある。— Tobacco is harmful to health.
2. 害虫から作物を守る必要がある。— It's necessary to protect crops from harmful insects.
3. この物質は人体に害を及ぼす可能性がある。— This substance may cause harm to the human body.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 害 is a broad, somewhat abstract word for "harm" that frequently appears in compounds (害虫 — harmful insect, 公害 — pollution, 有害 — harmful) rather than standing fully alone in casual speech — learners should recognize it mainly through these compound forms.

## 外~ (がい~)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** outside-, foreign- (prefix, e.g., 外貨 foreign currency, 外見 outward appearance)

**Similar words:**
- 外国~（がいこく~）— "foreign country ~," a more specific/longer prefix focused strictly on nationality (外国人, 外国語), whereas 外~ alone is broader.
- 内~（ない~）— the antonym prefix meaning "inside-, internal-" (内部, 内側).

**Opposite words:**
- 内~（ない~）— inside-, internal-

**Example sentences:**
1. 海外旅行のために外貨を両替した。— I exchanged foreign currency for my overseas trip.
2. 彼女は外見だけで判断されるのを嫌う。— She dislikes being judged only by her outward appearance.
3. 外交官として長年働いてきた。— I have worked as a diplomat for many years.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** As a prefix, 外~ is read がい (外貨, 外見, 外交) and generally conveys either "outward/external" or "foreign," with the specific sense determined by the following word — context is needed since the prefix covers both senses without distinction in form.

## 改悪 (かいあく)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a change for the worse

**Similar words:**
- 改正（かいせい）— "amendment," the antonym in spirit — implies a positive revision, whereas 改悪 is a revision that makes things worse.
- 悪化（あっか）— "worsening/deterioration," broader and doesn't necessarily involve an intentional "revision," just general decline.

**Opposite words:**
- [改善](vocab-11.md#改善-かいぜん)（かいぜん）— improvement
- [改良](vocab-11.md#改良-かいりょう)（かいりょう）— improvement

**Example sentences:**
1. この法改正は改悪だと批判されている。— This legal amendment is being criticized as a change for the worse.
2. サービスの変更はむしろ改悪だった。— The change to the service was actually a downgrade.
3. 規則の見直しが改悪にならないよう注意すべきだ。— We should be careful that the review of the rules doesn't turn into a change for the worse.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 改悪 is a sarcastic/critical term — it describes a change that was presented or intended as an improvement but is judged to have actually made things worse; it always carries a negative, critical tone, often used when criticizing policy or rule changes.

## 会員 (かいいん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a member (of a society, club, organization)

**Similar words:**
- [メンバー](vocab-34.md#メンバー-めんばー) — the casual loanword equivalent, often used for informal groups (team members), while 会員 is more formal/institutional (club/gym/association membership).
- 組合員（くみあいいん）— "union member," a narrower term specific to labor unions or cooperatives.

**Example sentences:**
1. このジムの会員になった。— I became a member of this gym.
2. 会員限定のセールが開催される。— A members-only sale will be held.
3. 図書館の会員証を提示してください。— Please show your library membership card.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 会員 implies formal registration with an institution (often with a card, dues, or benefits — 会員証, 会員限定), distinct from the more casual メンバー which can describe any group of people working together without formal registration.

## 海運 (かいうん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** shipping, marine transport

**Similar words:**
- 船便（ふなびん）— "sea mail/shipping (parcel delivery by sea)," a narrower, more consumer-facing term.
- 物流（ぶつりゅう）— "logistics," the broader industry term covering all transport methods, not limited to sea.

**Example sentences:**
1. 海運業界は世界貿易を支えている。— The shipping industry supports world trade.
2. この会社は海運で成功を収めた。— This company achieved success in marine transport.
3. 海運コストの上昇が物価に影響している。— The rise in shipping costs is affecting prices.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 海運 specifically refers to the shipping/maritime transport industry as a whole (海運会社, 海運業界), a formal, industry-level term — it's not used for a single act of sending a package by sea (that would be 船便で送る).

## 絵画 (かいが)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a picture, painting

**Similar words:**
- 絵（え）— the everyday, casual word for "picture/drawing," used much more broadly and informally.
- 美術（びじゅつ）— "fine art" as a general category, broader than just paintings (includes sculpture, etc.).

**Example sentences:**
1. 美術館で有名な絵画を見た。— I saw a famous painting at the art museum.
2. 彼は絵画の収集が趣味だ。— His hobby is collecting paintings.
3. この絵画は19世紀に描かれた。— This painting was created in the 19th century.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 絵画 is the formal, art-world term for "painting" (used in museums, art history, formal writing), while 絵 is the casual everyday word a child's drawing or any picture would be called — using 絵画 for a kid's doodle sounds comically formal.

## 外貨 (がいか)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** foreign currency

**Similar words:**
- 外国為替（がいこくかわせ）— "foreign exchange," the broader financial system/market term, of which 外貨 (foreign currency itself) is a component.
- [円](vocab-09.md#円-えん)（えん）— "yen," Japan's own currency, the implicit contrast point to 外貨.

**Example sentences:**
1. 銀行で外貨を両替した。— I exchanged foreign currency at the bank.
2. 外貨預金に興味がある。— I'm interested in foreign currency deposits.
3. 旅行前に外貨を準備しておいた。— I prepared foreign currency before the trip.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 外貨 specifically means currency issued by another country held/used within Japan's financial context (外貨両替, 外貨預金) — it does not mean "foreign goods" or "imports," a confusion some learners make based on the 外 prefix alone.

## 開会 (かいかい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** opening (of a meeting, ceremony)

**Similar words:**
- 開幕（かいまく）— "opening (of an event/season)," more theatrical/event-based (the curtain rising), often used for sports seasons or festivals.
- [開始](#開始-かいし)（かいし）— "beginning, commencement," more general and not limited to formal meetings/ceremonies.

**Opposite words:**
- 閉会（へいかい）— closing (of a meeting)

**Example sentences:**
1. 会議の開会を宣言します。— I declare the meeting open.
2. 開会式には多くの来賓が出席した。— Many guests attended the opening ceremony.
3. 開会のあいさつをお願いします。— Please give the opening remarks.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 開会 is specifically used for formal meetings, assemblies, and ceremonies (開会式, 開会宣言) — for a sports tournament or festival "opening," 開幕 is more natural, and for a general start of any activity, 開始 is the more neutral, all-purpose term.

## 海外 (かいがい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** overseas, abroad

**Similar words:**
- 外国（がいこく）— "foreign country," refers to a specific country, while 海外 more broadly means "abroad/overseas" as a general direction/destination.
- 国外（こくがい）— "outside the country," a more formal/legal equivalent often used in official or legal contexts (国外退去 — deportation).

**Opposite words:**
- 国内（こくない）— domestic, within the country

**Example sentences:**
1. 来年、海外旅行に行く予定だ。— I plan to travel abroad next year.
2. 彼は海外で働いている。— He works overseas.
3. 海外からの観光客が増えている。— The number of tourists from overseas is increasing.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 海外 is the natural, everyday word for "going/being abroad" (海外旅行, 海外留学), while 外国 is used more when referring to a specific foreign country or foreigners (外国人) rather than the general concept of "overseas."

## 改革 (かいかく)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a reform

**Similar words:**
- [改善](vocab-11.md#改善-かいぜん)（かいぜん）— "improvement," smaller-scale and incremental, whereas 改革 implies sweeping, structural change (often political/institutional).
- [革命](#革命-かくめい)（かくめい）— "revolution," an even more drastic, often violent overthrow of a system, bigger in scale than 改革.

**Example sentences:**
1. 政府は税制改革を進めている。— The government is pushing forward tax reform.
2. 教育改革についての議論が続いている。— Discussions about education reform continue.
3. 彼は組織の改革に取り組んだ。— He worked on reforming the organization.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 改革 implies large-scale, structural change to a system or institution (税制改革, 教育改革) — it's a stronger, more significant word than 改善, so using 改革 for a minor, everyday fix can sound exaggerated.

## 会館 (かいかん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a hall, assembly hall (a building for public/community use)

**Similar words:**
- ホール — the loanword equivalent, often used for a performance/event hall, slightly more modern-sounding.
- 公民館（こうみんかん）— "community center," a specific type of public facility for local residents' activities, narrower than general 会館.

**Example sentences:**
1. 結婚式は市民会館で行われた。— The wedding was held at the civic hall.
2. この会館では様々なイベントが開催される。— Various events are held at this hall.
3. 大使館の近くに文化会館がある。— There is a cultural hall near the embassy.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 会館 usually appears as part of a compound name for a specific public building (市民会館, 公会堂的な文化会館) rather than being used as a generic standalone word for "any hall" — it's more of a proper-noun-forming suffix than a casual descriptive term.

## 外観 (がいかん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** outward appearance, exterior

**Similar words:**
- 見た目（みため）— "appearance/looks," a much more casual, everyday word, used for people and objects alike.
- 外見（がいけん）— very close synonym, often used interchangeably with 外観, though 外見 is slightly more common for people's appearance, while 外観 is more common for buildings/products.

**Example sentences:**
1. この建物の外観はとても美しい。— The exterior of this building is very beautiful.
2. 商品は外観だけでなく機能性も重要だ。— For products, functionality matters as much as appearance.
3. 外観から判断すると、新しい車のようだ。— Judging from its appearance, it looks like a new car.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 外観 is typically used for buildings, products, and objects (建物の外観, 商品の外観), while 外見 leans toward describing a person's physical appearance (人は外見で判断すべきではない) — though there is some overlap, this is the general tendency to keep in mind.

## 階級 (かいきゅう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** class, rank (social, military)

**Similar words:**
- [階層](vocab-11.md#階層-かいそう)（かいそう）— "stratum/hierarchy," broader and more sociological, describing structural layers of society; 階級 leans more toward explicit rank/status (社会階級, 軍の階級).
- 身分（みぶん）— "social status/standing," more about one's position/identity in a social system, historically tied to class systems like feudal Japan.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は軍隊で高い階級に就いている。— He holds a high rank in the military.
2. 中産階級の人々が増えている。— The number of middle-class people is increasing.
3. 階級社会の構造について研究している。— I am researching the structure of a class-based society.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 階級 is specifically used for formal, often fixed/structural ranking systems (military rank, social class — 中産階級, 労働者階級), distinct from 階層 which describes broader social stratification without necessarily implying a rigid official rank.

## 会計 (かいけい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** accounting; the bill/check (at a restaurant)

**Similar words:**
- 経理（けいり）— "bookkeeping/accounting (department)," a more specific business-administrative term, often the name of a company department (経理部).
- お勘定（おかんじょう）— "the bill/check," a more casual synonym specifically for paying at a restaurant.

**Example sentences:**
1. すみません、お会計をお願いします。— Excuse me, could I have the bill, please?
2. 彼女は会社の会計を担当している。— She is in charge of the company's accounting.
3. 会計士の資格を取得した。— I obtained a certification as an accountant.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 会計 has two very distinct everyday uses that confuse beginners: the abstract "accounting" (会計士, 会計処理) and the concrete "bill/check" at a restaurant (お会計お願いします) — both are extremely common, so context (business vs. restaurant) is essential.

## 解決 (かいけつ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** settlement, solution (to a problem)

**Similar words:**
- 解消（かいしょう）— "resolution/elimination (of a problem/feeling)," often used for abstract issues like stress or misunderstandings (ストレス解消), while 解決 is more general-purpose for problems/disputes.
- 克服（こくふく）— "to overcome (a difficulty)," implies personal struggle/effort against an obstacle, more active/effortful than 解決.

**Example sentences:**
1. この問題はまだ解決していない。— This problem has not been solved yet.
2. 話し合いによって紛争が解決した。— The dispute was resolved through discussion.
3. 解決策を見つけるために会議を開いた。— We held a meeting to find a solution.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 解決 is the general, all-purpose word for "solving/resolving" any kind of problem, dispute, or case (事件解決、問題解決) — it's the default choice; 解消 is reserved more for dissolving an abstract negative state (不満の解消, ストレス解消) rather than solving a concrete problem.

## 会見 (かいけん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** an interview (often formal, e.g., press conference)

**Similar words:**
- [インタビュー](vocab-07.md#インタビュー) — the loanword equivalent, often used for more casual or one-on-one media interviews, whereas 会見 (especially 記者会見) implies a more formal, public event with multiple reporters.
- [面会](vocab-19.md#面会-めんかい)（めんかい）— "a meeting/visit (e.g., to see someone in person, like a patient or prisoner)," unrelated to media, more personal.

**Example sentences:**
1. 首相は緊急記者会見を開いた。— The Prime Minister held an emergency press conference.
2. 選手は試合後の会見で感想を述べた。— The player shared his thoughts at the post-match press conference.
3. 会見の様子はテレビで生中継された。— The press conference was broadcast live on TV.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 会見 almost always implies a formal, often public meeting with the press or officials (記者会見), not a casual one-on-one chat — using it for an informal interview between friends would sound odd; インタビュー is more neutral/versatile for general interviews.

## 介護 (かいご)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** nursing care (especially of the elderly or disabled)

**Similar words:**
- [看護](vocab-13.md#看護-かんご)（かんご）— "nursing (medical care)," specifically medical/clinical care typically provided by nurses in a hospital setting, whereas 介護 focuses on daily life support for the elderly/disabled.
- [世話](vocab-23.md#世話-せわ)（せわ）— "care/looking after," a much more general, everyday word for taking care of someone or something.

**Example sentences:**
1. 祖母の介護をしている。— I am taking care of (providing nursing care for) my grandmother.
2. 介護施設で働くことにした。— I decided to work at a nursing care facility.
3. 高齢化社会では介護の需要が高まっている。— In an aging society, the demand for elderly care is increasing.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 介護 specifically refers to long-term daily-life support for the elderly, disabled, or infirm (介護施設, 介護保険), distinct from 看護 which is medical/clinical nursing care in a hospital — the two fields and professions are legally and institutionally distinct in Japan.

## 会合 (かいごう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a gathering, meeting

**Similar words:**
- 会議（かいぎ）— "meeting," more business/formal and structured (with an agenda), while 会合 can be looser, more social or organizational gatherings.
- [集まり](vocab-03.md#集まり)（あつまり）— "a gathering," the most casual, everyday equivalent.

**Example sentences:**
1. 町内会の会合に参加した。— I attended the neighborhood association's gathering.
2. 定例の会合は毎月第一月曜日に開かれる。— The regular meeting is held on the first Monday of every month.
3. 業界関係者の会合が開かれた。— A gathering of industry stakeholders was held.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 会合 is a somewhat formal/written word often used for community, political, or organizational gatherings (町内会の会合, 秘密会合) — it's rarely used for casual get-togethers with friends, where 集まり or 飲み会 (for drinking gatherings) would be more natural.

## 外交 (がいこう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** diplomacy

**Similar words:**
- 国際関係（こくさいかんけい）— "international relations," the broader academic/political field, of which 外交 (diplomacy) is a practical component.
- [政治](vocab-22.md#政治-せいじ)（せいじ）— "politics," much broader, covering domestic as well as international affairs.

**Example sentences:**
1. 両国は外交関係を維持している。— The two countries maintain diplomatic relations.
2. 彼は長年外交官として働いてきた。— He has worked as a diplomat for many years.
3. 外交交渉が難航している。— The diplomatic negotiations are having difficulty progressing.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 外交 specifically refers to international, government-level diplomacy (外交官, 外交関係, 外交政策) — it's occasionally used colloquially/figuratively to mean "social skills/tact in dealing with people" (外交的な性格 — an outgoing/sociable personality), a secondary meaning worth knowing.

## 開催 (かいさい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** to hold, open (an event, meeting, exhibition)

**Similar words:**
- 行う（おこなう）— "to conduct/carry out," a more general, all-purpose verb for performing any action or event, less formal/specific than 開催.
- 催す（もよおす）— the related verb form, close synonym, often used in the same formal register as 開催 (its noun form).

**Example sentences:**
1. 来月、展覧会が開催される。— An exhibition will be held next month.
2. オリンピックは四年に一度開催される。— The Olympics are held once every four years.
3. 新商品発表会を開催いたします。— We will hold a new product announcement event.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 開催 is the standard, formal word specifically for holding organized events (展覧会、大会、セミナー), used in official announcements and news; it is not used for everyday casual activities like a small home gathering, where する or 開く would be more natural.

## 改札 (かいさつ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a ticket gate (at a train station)

**Similar words:**
- 改札口（かいさつぐち）— the fuller, equally common form specifically naming "the ticket gate entrance/exit."
- 切符（きっぷ）— "a ticket," the item checked at the 改札, related but distinct (the object vs. the checkpoint).

**Example sentences:**
1. 改札で切符を見せてください。— Please show your ticket at the ticket gate.
2. 改札を出たところで友達と待ち合わせた。— I arranged to meet my friend right after exiting the ticket gate.
3. 自動改札機が導入されて便利になった。— It became more convenient after automatic ticket gates were introduced.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 改札 specifically refers to the station checkpoint where tickets/IC cards are checked (自動改札機, 改札口) — it is a train-station-specific term and not used for other types of entry gates (e.g., a movie theater or stadium gate would use different vocabulary like 入場口).

## 解散 (かいさん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** to break up, disperse (a group, organization, parliament)

**Similar words:**
- 分裂（ぶんれつ）— "to split/fracture," implies the group breaks into opposing factions, a more contentious process than neutral 解散.
- バラバラになる — "to fall apart/scatter," a casual, descriptive way to say a group breaks up, less formal than 解散.

**Example sentences:**
1. そのバンドは解散した。— That band broke up.
2. 国会が解散され、総選挙が行われることになった。— The Diet was dissolved, and a general election was decided to be held.
3. 集会の後、参加者たちは解散した。— After the gathering, the participants dispersed.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 解散 is used broadly from casual contexts (a band or group disbanding) to formal/political contexts (国会解散 — dissolution of parliament) — it's also commonly shouted at the end of a group activity/meeting to mean "dismissed!/you're free to go."

## 開始 (かいし)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a beginning, commencement

**Similar words:**
- 始まる（はじまる）— the basic, everyday verb "to begin," intransitive counterpart used in casual speech, whereas 開始 is the more formal noun/suru-verb.
- 開幕（かいまく）— "opening (of an event/season)," more specific to ceremonial or theatrical openings.

**Opposite words:**
- [終了](vocab-20.md#終了-しゅうりょう)（しゅうりょう）— ending, completion

**Example sentences:**
1. 試験はまもなく開始されます。— The exam will begin shortly.
2. 会議の開始時刻は午前十時です。— The meeting's start time is 10 a.m.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 開始 is the formal, often official/written register word for "beginning" (used in schedules, announcements, instructions), while 始まる/始める are the everyday spoken equivalents — using 開始 in casual conversation ("じゃあ、開始しよう") sounds stiff compared to natural 始めよう.

- 割増（わりまし）— "surcharge/premium," related to the insurance-premium sense of 掛け (掛け金, premium payment).

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 保険の掛け金を毎月支払っている。— I pay the insurance premium every month.
2. このパンは食べかけで置いてある。— This bread is sitting there half-eaten.
3. 月賦払いで家具を購入した。— I purchased furniture on an installment plan.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** ～掛け is a versatile suffix with several distinct uses: attached to a verb stem it means "partway through" (読みかけの本, a book one is partway through reading), while as a noun 掛け/掛け金 it refers to installment payments or insurance premiums — context determines which sense applies.

## ヶ月/箇月 (かげつ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** months (counter for duration in months)

**Similar words:**

- 週間（しゅうかん）— the counter for a duration measured in weeks, parallel structure to ヶ月 but for weeks.
