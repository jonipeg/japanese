# ～とひきかえに {#とひきかえに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～と引き換えに

**Furigana form:** ～とひきかえに

**Meaning:** "In exchange for X" — describes a transactional or reciprocal relationship where one thing is given/received/sacrificed in return for another.

**Conjugation:**
- [Verb/い形容詞/な形容詞 の名詞修飾形] + の + とひきかえに
- [Noun] + とひきかえに
- Example pattern: 子どもとひきかえに身代金を要求する (demand ransom in exchange for the child).

**Typical context:** Formal/written register, though it also appears in news reports and dramatic narration (ransom, hostage, trade negotiations). Neutral politeness; the nuance can carry emotional weight (sacrifice, loss) depending on content.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**かわりに**](grammar-かわりに.md) — "instead of / in place of," a broader substitution structure; とひきかえに specifically implies a two-way exchange/trade, while かわりに can simply mean "substituting one thing for another" without reciprocity.
- [**とともに**](grammar-とともに.md) — "together with X," expresses simultaneity/accompaniment, not an exchange.
- [**てまえ**](grammar-てまえ.md) — means "in light of / for the sake of appearances (in front of someone)," a completely different meaning from とひきかえに; this is a confirmed real-test confusion pair, since both can follow a noun + て-ish sound, but てまえ is about social appearance/obligation, not exchange.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite exists; conceptually contrasts with ～なしに ("without any exchange/condition attached").

**Example sentences:**
1. 犯人は誘拐した子どもとひきかえに身代金一億円を親に要求した。
   (The kidnapper demanded a 100-million-yen ransom from the parents in exchange for the kidnapped child.)
2. 彼は自分の名誉とひきかえに、友人の罪をかばった。
   (He protected his friend's crime in exchange for his own honor.)
3. 会社は多額の賠償金とひきかえに、被害者と和解した。
   (The company settled with the victim in exchange for a large sum in damages.)
4. 彼女は安定した生活とひきかえに、夢をあきらめた。
   (She gave up her dream in exchange for a stable life.)
5. 政府は人質の解放とひきかえに、要求に応じざるを得なかった。
   (The government had no choice but to comply with the demands in exchange for the release of the hostages.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** On a real graded N1 test, a student picked てまえ instead of とひきかえに — a confirmed wrong-answer pair. The key distinguishing test: does the sentence describe literally trading/giving up one thing to get another (とひきかえに) versus acting a certain way "in light of/because of appearances in front of someone" (てまえ)? If there's a concrete item or condition being exchanged (money, a hostage, one's dream), とひきかえに is correct.
