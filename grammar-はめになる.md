# ～はめになる {#はめになる}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～羽目になる

**Furigana form:** ～はめになる

**Meaning:** "End up in a (rough/unwanted) situation" — describes falling into an undesirable predicament, usually as an unintended consequence of events.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形 + 羽目になる
- Example base: 歩いて帰る → 歩いて帰る羽目になる

**Typical context:** Neutral spoken and written register; conveys a resigned or self-deprecating tone about an unlucky turn of events. Common in everyday storytelling about mishaps.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**ことになる**](grammar-ことになる・なっている.md) — neutral "it turns out/ends up that X"; a general-purpose "result" construction without 羽目になる's specific "stuck in a bad situation" nuance.
- **はめになった／はめに陥る** — 陥る (おちいる, "to fall into") is a near-synonym verb sometimes paired with 羽目, emphasizing the sense of falling into a trap-like predicament.
- [**しまつだ**](grammar-しまつだ.md) — "it ended up in the (sorry) state of X"; similarly describes an unwanted outcome, often the culmination of a chain of bad decisions, with a slightly more critical/judgmental tone than 羽目になる.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; conceptually うまくいく ("things go well") expresses the reverse — a fortunate rather than unfortunate outcome.

**Example sentences:**
1. 買い物中に財布を盗まれて、深夜に歩いて帰る羽目になってしまった。 (My wallet was stolen while shopping, and I ended up having to walk home late at night.)
2. 彼の誘いを断れず、苦手なキャンプに参加する羽目になった。 (I couldn't refuse his invitation and ended up going on a camping trip I dislike.)
3. 電車を逃して、タクシーで高い料金を払う羽目になった。 (I missed the train and ended up paying an expensive taxi fare.)
4. うっかり約束を忘れて、友人に謝り続ける羽目になった。 (I carelessly forgot a promise and ended up apologizing to my friend over and over.)
5. 準備不足で、本番で大恥をかく羽目になってしまった。 (Due to lack of preparation, I ended up embarrassing myself badly during the actual event.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 羽目になる always describes an outcome the subject did not want or intend — it should not be used for neutral or positive results. Learners sometimes forget the fixed idiom 羽目 (literally "panel/frame," from a horse's bit/restraint in an old idiom) only appears in this set phrase and has no independent modern usage outside 羽目になる／羽目に陥る.
