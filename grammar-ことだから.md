# ～ことだから {#ことだから}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ことだから

**Furigana form:** ～ことだから (no special kanji reading)

**Meaning:** "Because it's (characteristic of) X / knowing X as we do" — used to make a prediction or judgment about someone's likely behavior based on well-known personality traits or past patterns.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞(人) + の + ことだから
- 彼のことだから、きっと遅刻するだろう (Knowing him, he'll probably be late)

**Typical context:** Conversational, used when speaker and listener share common knowledge about a third person's character. Carries a knowing, familiar tone.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **から** — plain causal "because," lacks the "based on known character" nuance.
- [**だけに**](grammar-だけに.md) — "precisely because X," emphasizes a fitting outcome given a trait, but not limited to personality predictions.
- [**にしては**](grammar-にしては.md) — "for X, surprisingly..." contrasts expectation with reality rather than confirming it.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentessences (example sentences):**
1. 真面目な彼のことだから、締め切りを守るだろう。
   (Knowing how serious he is, he'll surely meet the deadline.)
2. おしゃべりな彼女のことだから、もう皆に話しているはずだ。
   (Knowing how talkative she is, she's probably already told everyone.)
3. 几帳面な田中さんのことだから、書類に間違いはないはずだ。
   (Knowing how meticulous Tanaka-san is, there shouldn't be any mistakes in the documents.)
4. のんびりした性格の弟のことだから、まだ準備もしていないだろう。
   (Knowing my easy-going brother, he probably hasn't even prepared yet.)
5. 優しい彼女のことだから、きっと許してくれるよ。
   (Knowing how kind she is, she'll surely forgive you.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Requires shared background knowledge about the person's character — it doesn't work for people the speaker/listener don't know well. Often paired with だろう/はずだ to express the resulting prediction.
