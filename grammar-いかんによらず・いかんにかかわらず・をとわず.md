# ～いかんによらず／いかんにかかわらず／を問わず {#いかんによらず・いかんにかかわらず・をとわず}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**1. Kanji version:** 如何によらず／如何にかかわらず／を問わず

**2. Furigana version:** いかんによらず／いかんにかかわらず／をとわず

**3. Meaning:** Regardless of X / no matter what X is — a merged cluster of "regardless of" variants that are tested interchangeably.

**4. Conjugation (connection form):** [名詞]+の+いかん(によらず／にかかわらず) ; [名詞]+を問わず

**5. Usage context:** Formal, written/literary register. Common in official announcements, legal or business documents, job postings, and formal speeches. Not used in casual conversation.

**6. Similar grammar structures:**
- にかかわらず — near-synonym, but usable more generally (including with adjectives), while いかんによらず leans toward abstract nouns like 理由・国籍・結果.
- を問わず — essentially interchangeable, but especially common in recruitment/announcement contexts ("男女を問わず").
- いかんで／いかんによっては — a DIFFERENT and opposite-leaning meaning: "depending on X," not "regardless of X." This is a classic confusion point since both use いかん, but によらず/にかかわらず/を問わず flip the meaning to "it doesn't matter," while で/によっては keep the meaning "it depends."

**7. Opposite grammar structures:** 次第だ／によって (these show dependency — the outcome changes based on X — the conceptual opposite of "regardless of X").

**8. Example sentences:**
1. 理由のいかんによらず、殺人は許されないことだ。(Regardless of the reason, murder is unforgivable.)
2. 業務経験者なら、国籍のいかんを問わず、採用試験を受けることができます。(If you have work experience, you can take the hiring exam regardless of nationality.)
3. 結果のいかんにかかわらず、全力を尽くしたことに意味がある。(Regardless of the outcome, giving your full effort has meaning.)
4. 性別を問わず、誰でもこのスポーツを楽しむことができる。(Anyone can enjoy this sport, regardless of gender.)
5. 天候のいかんによらず、イベントは予定通り開催されます。(Regardless of the weather, the event will be held as scheduled.)

**9. Common mistakes / key nuances:** Students often confuse いかんだ／いかんで (depends on X) with いかんによらず (regardless of X) because they share the root いかん. The deciding factor is the attached word: によらず／にかかわらず／を問わず all negate the relevance of X, while で／によっては affirm that X matters. Also note the related nuance (from source notes): いかん alone is the "major/typical" usage, while いかんによっては is treated as a "minor/possibility" case closer to 次第では.
