# ～のみならず {#のみならず}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～のみならず

**Furigana form:** ～のみならず *(no kanji; written entirely in kana — occasionally seen as のみならず with 只／独 prefix: 只(ただ)～のみならず, 独(ひとり)～のみならず)*

**Meaning:** "Not only X, but also Y" — an additive, emphatic construction stating that something goes beyond the stated scope.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞・い形容詞・な形容詞・名詞 の普通形 (plain form of verbs/i-adjectives/na-adjectives/nouns) + のみならず
- Na-adjectives and nouns typically attach directly or with である: 「俳優であるのみならず」
- Can be preceded by ただ or ひとり for emphasis: ただ～のみならず, ひとり～のみならず ("not only... (merely)...")

**Typical context:** Formal, written register — common in essays, formal speeches, news commentary, and business writing. Carries no rudeness; it signals a slightly elevated, literary tone compared to casual equivalents like 〜だけでなく.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**はおろか**](grammar-はおろか.md) — means "let alone X" (used with negative predicates to say "not even X, much less Y"); のみならず is purely additive/positive in direction (X true AND Y true), while はおろか emphasizes an extreme negative scale (not even the lesser thing is true).
- [**といえども**](grammar-といえども.md) — means "even though/even if it is X" (concessive); it grants a condition and then contradicts the expected conclusion, unlike のみならず's simple additive stacking.
- [**ともなく**](grammar-ともなく・ともなしに.md) — means "without any particular intention" (e.g., 見るともなく "without meaning to look"); entirely different meaning, no additive sense.
- [**こととて**](grammar-こととて.md) — means "since it's the case of X" (reason/excuse); unrelated structurally and semantically.
- [**ともあろう**](grammar-ともあろう.md) — means "someone of such standing as X (should not...)"; a criticism construction, not additive.
- [**はさておき**](grammar-はさておき.md) — means "setting X aside"; the opposite direction of focus (excluding rather than adding).

**Opposite grammar structures:** No single direct grammatical opposite, but ～はさておき ("setting X aside") or ～はおろか ("let alone X," used to deny even the lesser case) move in a conceptually opposing direction of focus. No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は俳優であるのみならず、今や作家としても活躍している。
   (He is not only an actor, but now is also active as a writer.)
2. この薬は風邪のみならず、インフルエンザにも効果がある。
   (This medicine is effective not only for colds but also for the flu.)
3. 彼女は語学が堪能であるのみならず、交渉力にも長けている。
   (She is not only fluent in languages but also skilled at negotiation.)
4. この政策は国内のみならず、海外からも注目を集めている。
   (This policy is drawing attention not only domestically but also from abroad.)
5. 社長は経営手腕のみならず、人柄の良さでも社員から慕われている。
   (The president is beloved by employees not only for his management skill but also for his good character.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Graded tests confuse のみならず with はおろか, といえども, ともなく, こととて, ともあろう, and はさておき. The critical distinguishing feature is that のみならず is purely additive and positive-direction ("X is true, and furthermore Y"), whereas several of its distractors (はおろか especially) require a negative context or express concession/exception rather than simple addition. Students should verify the sentence is stacking two true, non-contradictory facts rather than contrasting or excepting something.
