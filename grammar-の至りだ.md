# ～の至りだ {#の至りだ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～の至りだ

**Furigana form:** ～のいたりだ

**Meaning:** "The height/utmost of X" — a very formal expression used to convey an extreme emotional state (such as honor, embarrassment, or gratitude) at its utmost degree.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞（感情・評価を表す限られた語） + の至りだ
- 光栄の至りです — "it is the height of honor"

**Typical context:** Very formal written/spoken register — used in formal speeches, greetings, acceptance remarks, and written apologies; rarely used in casual conversation.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**～極まりない**](grammar-極まる・極まりない.md) — "extremely X," also very formal, but attaches to na-adjectives (迷惑極まりない) rather than a fixed set of emotion nouns.
- **～限りだ** — "the utmost of X," attaches to i-adjectives (嬉しい限りだ) and expresses personal feeling, slightly less stiff than の至りだ.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 大統領にお目にかかれて、実に光栄の至りです。
   (Being able to meet the President is truly the height of honor.)
2. 言葉遣いの間違いを指摘され、赤面の至りだった。
   (Having my wording mistake pointed out, I was utterly embarrassed.)
3. あのころは若気の至りで、いろいろ失敗もした。
   (Back then, out of youthful folly, I made all sorts of mistakes.)
4. このような賞をいただき、感激の至りです。
   (Receiving such an award fills me with the utmost gratitude.)
5. ご指導いただき、恐縮の至りです。
   (I am extremely humbled to receive your guidance.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern only combines with a small, fixed set of formal nouns (光栄, 赤面, 感激, 恐縮, 若気), so it cannot be freely attached to any noun. Note that 若気の至り is a fixed idiomatic phrase meaning "foolish behavior that comes from youthful inexperience," distinct from the more literal "height of X" reading used with the other nouns in this set.
