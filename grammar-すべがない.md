# ～すべがない {#すべがない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～術がない

**Furigana form:** ～すべがない

**Meaning:** "There's no method/means to X" — expresses that no way exists to accomplish or resolve something, after other options have been exhausted.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形 + 術がない (する verbs often become 〜するすべ, e.g., 連絡するすべがない)
- Example base: できる → (これ以上)できる術がない

**Typical context:** Formal, written/literary register; conveys a dramatic sense of helplessness. Appears in narrative writing and formal spoken laments; not typical casual chat vocabulary.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**ようがない**](grammar-ようがない.md) — "there's no way to do X"; nearly synonymous and more commonly used in everyday speech, while 術がない carries a more literary, dramatic flavor.
- **しかたがない／しょうがない** — "it can't be helped"; expresses resigned acceptance of a situation generally, rather than specifically the absence of a method to solve a problem.
- **手段がない** — "there is no means/method"; a more neutral, business-like synonym for 術がない without its literary flavor.

**Opposite grammar structures:** 方法がある／手段がある ("there is a method/means") expresses the reverse — that a solution does exist.

**Example sentences:**
1. あらゆる方法を使ってみたが、うまくいかなかった。これ以上できる術がない。 (I tried every method, but nothing worked. There's no more I can do.)
2. 連絡を絶たれてしまい、彼の居場所を知る術がない。 (Contact was cut off, and there's no way to know his whereabouts.)
3. この古い機械を修理する術がもうない。 (There's no longer any way to repair this old machine.)
4. 突然の停電で、状況を確認する術がなかった。 (With the sudden power outage, there was no way to check the situation.)
5. 彼女を説得する術が見つからず、計画は中止になった。 (No way to persuade her could be found, and the plan was called off.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 術 (すべ) is a somewhat literary/old-fashioned noun for "method/way," so すべがない sounds more dramatic and helpless than the everyday ようがない — learners should reserve it for situations of genuine desperation or narrative emphasis rather than mundane "I don't know how to do X" statements, where ようがない or 方法がない would sound more natural.
