# ～に反して {#に反して}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に反して

**Furigana form:** ～にはんして

**Meaning:** "Contrary to X" — indicates that an actual outcome goes against or differs from an expectation, prediction, rule, or wish.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (expectation/rule-type noun) + に反して (例: 予想に反して、期待に反して、規則に反して)
- Attributive form: に反する + 名詞 (例: 規則に反する行為)

**Typical context:** Formal, mostly written — common in news reports, official statements, and formal essays describing unexpected results or rule violations.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**にもかかわらず**](grammar-にかかわらず・にもかかわらず.md) — "despite X," expresses a contrast between a condition and an outcome without implying direct opposition to a prediction/rule.
- **を裏切って** — "betraying X (expectations)," more emotionally charged/literary paraphrase of 予想に反して.
- **とは違って** — "unlike X," simpler contrastive comparison without the formal "contrary to prediction/rule" nuance.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～の通りに ("in accordance with X," matching the expectation) functions as a conceptual opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 天気予報に反して、今日は朝から晴れている。
   (Contrary to the weather forecast, it has been sunny since this morning.)
2. 周囲の期待に反して、彼は試験に落ちてしまった。
   (Contrary to everyone's expectations, he failed the exam.)
3. この行為は会社の規則に反している。
   (This act goes against the company's rules.)
4. 予想に反して、新商品の売れ行きは好調だった。
   (Contrary to predictions, sales of the new product were strong.)
5. 本人の意思に反して、転勤が決まってしまった。
   (Against his own wishes, the transfer was decided.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に反して specifically requires the preceding noun to represent an expectation, prediction, rule, or will/intention (予想・期待・規則・意思) — it cannot be used with arbitrary nouns the way にもかかわらず can with clauses. The attributive 反する modifies nouns directly (規則に反する行為 = "an act contrary to the rules").
