# ～にもほどがある {#にもほどがある}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～にも程がある

**Furigana form:** ～にもほどがある

**Meaning:** "There's a limit to ~ / ~ is really going too far" — expresses strong criticism or exasperation that something (a behavior, statement, or quality) has far exceeded acceptable or reasonable limits.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (noun) + にもほどがある
- な形容詞語幹 (na-adjective stem) + にもほどがある
- Example: 自分でやりたいと言ったのに、途中で仕事を投げ出すなんて、無責任にもほどがある。 (Even though you said you wanted to do it yourself, giving up on the job halfway through — there's a limit to how irresponsible you can be!)

**Typical context:** Spoken, casual-to-neutral register; used when scolding, criticizing, or expressing exasperation, often directed at another person's behavior. Can sound blunt or confrontational.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **すぎる** — "too much ~" — a more general, milder way to say something exceeds a reasonable degree, without the emotional charge of ほどがある.
- **も甚だしい** — "is extreme/excessive" — a more formal/written equivalent with a similar critical nuance, common in written complaints.
- [**なんて**](grammar-など・なんて・なんか.md) — often paired with にもほどがある to introduce the shocking behavior being criticized (～なんて、～にもほどがある).

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though ちょうどいい ("just right, appropriate") contrasts in spirit.

**Example sentences:**
1. 自分でやりたいと言ったのに、途中で仕事を投げ出すなんて、無責任にもほどがある。
   (Even though you said you wanted to do it yourself, giving up halfway through — there's a limit to how irresponsible you can be!)
2. 人の話を全く聞かないなんて、わがままにもほどがある。
   (Not listening to others at all — there's a limit to how selfish you can be!)
3. 約束の時間に3時間も遅れてくるなんて、非常識にもほどがある。
   (Showing up three hours late to an appointment — that's really going too far in terms of common sense!)
4. いくら忙しいからって、返事もしないなんて失礼にもほどがある。
   (No matter how busy you are, not even replying is incredibly rude — there's a limit!)
5. 冗談にもほどがある。そんなことを言ったら誰だって怒るよ。
   (There's a limit to jokes. Anyone would get angry if you said something like that.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners sometimes use this calmly like すぎる, but にもほどがある carries a strong emotional charge of anger, disbelief, or scolding — it's rarely neutral and should not be used to describe one's own mild shortcomings in polite settings.
