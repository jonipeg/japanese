# ～だに(～ない) {#だに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**1. Kanji version:** ～だに (no kanji; grammatical kana particle-like word)

**2. Furigana version:** ～だに

**3. Meaning:** Not even X — an emphatic negative expressing extreme degree (e.g. 想像だにできない = "can't even imagine").

**4. Conjugation (connection form):** 名詞 + だにしない ／ 動詞辞書形 + だに, restricted to a small set of fixed collocations such as 想像だに、予想だに、微動だに.

**5. Usage context:** Written, very formal/literary register. Appears in essays, news writing, and literature; virtually never used in spoken Japanese.

**6. Similar grammar structures:**
- すら — "even X," broader in application, attachable to more nouns, slightly less extreme/formal than だに.
- さえ — the most common/neutral version of "even," with the broadest range of usage; much more frequent in everyday Japanese than だに.
- も — a plain emphatic "even/also," with far weaker emphasis than だに.

**7. Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**8. Example sentences:**
1. 人類が月に行ける日が来るなんて、百年前には想像だにしなかったことだ。(A hundred years ago, people couldn't even imagine that a day would come when humanity could go to the moon.)
2. 突然ピストルを向けられて、微動だにできなかった。(Suddenly having a gun pointed at him, he couldn't even flinch.)
3. まさか自分が優勝するとは、予想だにしなかった。(I never even imagined that I myself would win first place.)
4. 彼が裏切るとは、夢にだに思わなかった。(I never even dreamed that he would betray us.)
5. その知らせを聞いて、彼女は言葉だに出なかった。(Hearing that news, she couldn't even utter a word.)

**9. Common mistakes / key nuances:** Students may try to attach だに freely to any noun, but it is restricted to a small set of fixed, formal collocations (想像だに、予想だに、微動だに, etc.) rather than being a fully productive grammar pattern. It is also part of the recurring N1 "extreme degree" confusable cluster alongside すら and さえ, so mixing up register/strength among these three is a common error.
