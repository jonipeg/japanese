# ～上(じょう) {#上じょう}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～上

**Furigana form:** ～じょう

**Meaning:** "From the standpoint of X / in terms of X" — attaches to a noun to indicate the perspective, field, or domain from which a statement is being made (e.g., legally, technically, historically).

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + 上（で／の／は）
- 法律上、これは問題ない (Legally speaking, this is not a problem)
- 技術上の理由で (For technical reasons)
- 健康上の問題 (A health-related problem)

**Typical context:** Formal written and spoken Japanese — business, legal, academic, and technical contexts. Rare in casual conversation.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **の点で** — "in terms of/regarding X," more general and flexible, usable in casual contexts too.
- **から見ると／から見れば** — "seen from the perspective of X," broader phrase with similar function but more explicitly about viewpoint.
- **的に (noun + 的に)** — "in an X way/-ly," broader adverbial use, less restricted to formal/technical domains than 上.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 法律上は問題ないが、倫理的には議論の余地がある。
   (Legally there's no problem, but ethically there's room for debate.)
2. この機械は構造上、修理が難しい。
   (Due to its structure, this machine is difficult to repair.)
3. 健康上の理由で、彼はお酒をやめた。
   (For health reasons, he quit drinking.)
4. 教育上、体罰は好ましくないとされている。
   (From an educational standpoint, corporal punishment is considered undesirable.)
5. 仕事上の付き合いで、週末も出かけることが多い。
   (Due to work-related relationships, I often go out even on weekends.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 上 here is read じょう, not うえ — a common misreading pitfall. It specifically frames a statement within an abstract domain/standpoint (legal, technical, educational), unlike concrete spatial うえ ("on top of"). Don't use it with ordinary everyday nouns outside formal/technical registers.
