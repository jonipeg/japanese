# ～といっても過言ではない {#といっても過言ではない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～と言っても過言ではない

**Furigana form:** ～といってもかごんではない

**Meaning:** "It wouldn't be an exaggeration to say X" — asserts a strong claim while framing it as reasonable rather than hyperbolic.

**Conjugation:**
- 普通形（動詞／い形容詞／な形容詞＋だ／名詞＋だ）+ と言っても過言ではない

**Typical context:** Formal, written register; common in essays, reviews, speeches, and persuasive writing where the speaker wants to make a bold claim while softening its audacity. Rarely used in casual conversation.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **と言っていい** — "it's fair to say X"; a milder, more casual way of hedging a claim, without the specific "not an exaggeration" framing.
- **まさに／ほとんど** + statement — adverbial intensifiers that strengthen a claim directly, rather than defending it against the charge of exaggeration.
- **といっても言い過ぎではない** — a near-synonym using 言い過ぎ ("overstatement") instead of 過言, functionally identical.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; conceptually 言い過ぎかもしれないが ("it might be an overstatement, but...") frames the same claim as potentially exaggerated rather than defending it.

**Example sentences:**
1. このワインは世界一だといっても過言ではないでしょう。 (It wouldn't be an exaggeration to say this wine is the best in the world.)
2. 彼の功績は日本の医学史を変えたといっても過言ではない。 (It's no exaggeration to say his achievements changed the history of Japanese medicine.)
3. インターネットは現代人の生活に欠かせない存在だといっても過言ではない。 (It's not an exaggeration to say the internet is indispensable to modern life.)
4. この映画は今年最高の作品だといっても過言ではないだろう。 (It wouldn't be an exaggeration to call this film the best work of the year.)
5. 彼女の努力があったからこそ、プロジェクトが成功したといっても過言ではない。 (It's no exaggeration to say the project succeeded precisely because of her effort.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners sometimes overuse this pattern for mundane claims; it is reserved for genuinely bold or superlative assertions ("the best," "changed everything," "indispensable") — using it for an ordinary statement sounds unnatural or overly dramatic. Also remember 過言 (かごん, "exaggeration/overstatement") is a somewhat literary noun rarely used outside this set phrase, so don't try to use 過言 independently in casual speech.
