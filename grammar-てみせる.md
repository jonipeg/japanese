# ～てみせる {#てみせる}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**1. Kanji version:** ～て見せる

**2. Furigana version:** ～てみせる

**3. Meaning:** Will do X to prove it / show someone that one can do X — expresses strong personal determination.

**4. Conjugation (connection form):** 動詞て形 + みせる

**5. Usage context:** Used in both spoken and written Japanese; expresses strong will or determination and can sound dramatic or assertive. Politeness level follows the base verb form, so it ranges from casual to neutral-formal depending on context.

**6. Similar grammar structures:**
- てみろ — superficially similar (て+み), but てみろ is an imperative challenge directed at someone else ("go ahead and try"), not a statement of the speaker's own resolve. Confirmed as a tested distractor.
- てくれ — a request for someone else to do something for the speaker; unrelated in function but also tested as a confusable distractor for this cluster.
- 必ず～（意志形） — plain emphatic statement of intention without the "prove it" nuance that てみせる carries.

**7. Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**8. Example sentences:**
1. 今回は負けてしまったけど、次の試合では必ず勝ってみせるさ。(I lost this time, but I will definitely win next time, you'll see.)
2. どんなに難しくても、このプロジェクトを必ず成功させてみせる。(No matter how difficult it is, I will make this project succeed, I promise.)
3. 彼女は一人で荷物を全部運んでみせた。(She carried all the luggage by herself, to prove she could.)
4. 父に心配をかけたくないから、元気になってみせるつもりだ。(I don't want to worry my father, so I intend to show him I've gotten better.)
5. 新人の彼は、先輩たちの前で完璧な料理を作ってみせた。(The new employee cooked a perfect dish in front of his senior colleagues, to prove himself.)

**9. Common mistakes / key nuances:** Confused with てみろ because of the shared て+み form — remember てみせる is about the SPEAKER's own determination/proof ("I will do X, just watch"), while てみろ commands the LISTENER to attempt something. Also distinguish from てくれ, which is a request for help, not a declaration of resolve.
