# ～くらいのものだ {#くらいのものだ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～くらいのものだ

**Furigana form:** ～くらいのものだ

**Meaning:** "It's only limited to about ~ / that's about the only case" — expresses that something is restricted to a very narrow, specific case, often with a nuance of it being surprisingly few or rare.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞/い形容詞普通形 (verb/i-adjective plain form) + くらいのものだ
- 名詞 (noun) + くらいのものだ
- Example: 着物は日本の伝統衣装ですが、実際に着るのは結婚式のときくらいのものです。 (Kimono is Japan's traditional clothing, but the only time people actually wear it is really just at weddings.)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal spoken and written Japanese; used when making a generalization or observation about how limited/rare something is, often in explanatory or essay-like contexts.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**しかない**](grammar-しかない.md) — "there is only ~" — similarly restrictive, but more direct and can sound blunter; lacks the reflective "that's really about it" nuance.
- [**にすぎない**](grammar-にすぎない.md) — "is nothing more than ~" — emphasizes that something is merely/only that, often with a slightly dismissive nuance, rather than emphasizing rarity.
- **だけだ** — "it's just/only ~" — a simpler, more neutral way to state exclusivity without the reflective tone.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though いくらでもある ("there's plenty of ~") contrasts in spirit by indicating abundance rather than scarcity.

**Example sentences:**
1. 着物は日本の伝統衣装ですが、実際に着るのは結婚式のときくらいのものです。
   (Kimono is Japan's traditional clothing, but the only time people actually wear it is really just at weddings.)
2. 最近忙しくて、友達と会うのは正月くらいのものだ。
   (I've been so busy lately that New Year's is really about the only time I see my friends.)
3. この店の常連客は近所のお年寄りくらいのものだ。
   (The regular customers at this shop are pretty much just the elderly people from the neighborhood.)
4. 今どき手紙を書く人は、年賀状を送るときくらいのものだろう。
   (These days, people who write letters probably only do so around New Year's cards.)
5. 彼と連絡を取るのは、年に一度の同窓会のときくらいのものだ。
   (The only time I really get in touch with him is at the annual reunion.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners sometimes use くらいのものだ interchangeably with だけだ, but くらいのものだ carries a reflective, almost surprised tone about how narrow the scope is, making it better suited to essays and spoken observations than to simple factual statements.
