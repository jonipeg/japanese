# ～に比べて {#に比べて}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に比べて

**Furigana form:** ～にくらべて

**Meaning:** "Compared to X" — introduces a point of comparison, contrasting the following clause's state/degree against X.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に比べて — 去年に比べて (compared to last year)
- Noun-modifying form: 名詞 + に比べると/に比べ

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, common in both speech and writing — statistics, reports, everyday comparisons.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**より**](grammar-より.md) — simple comparative "more than X," less explicit about the act of comparison itself.
- **と比較して** — more formal/written synonym, common in academic and business reports.
- [**に対して**](grammar-に対して.md) — "in contrast to X / in response to X," implies more of a contrast/opposition than a scaled comparison.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 今年の夏は去年に比べて、かなり暑い。
   (This summer is considerably hotter compared to last year.)
2. 都会に比べて、田舎は物価が安い。
   (Compared to the city, prices are cheaper in the countryside.)
3. 以前に比べて、彼の日本語はずいぶん上達した。
   (Compared to before, his Japanese has improved a great deal.)
4. 兄に比べて、弟はおとなしい性格だ。
   (Compared to his older brother, the younger brother has a quieter personality.)
5. 去年の売り上げに比べて、今年は20%増加した。
   (Compared to last year's sales, this year increased by 20%.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に比べて requires an explicit basis of comparison (a noun), unlike より which can directly follow a comparative statement more flexibly; learners sometimes misuse に比べて without a clear second term being compared, making the sentence ambiguous.
