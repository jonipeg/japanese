# ～をおいて〜ない {#をおいて〜ない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を措いて～ない

**Furigana form:** ～をおいて～ない

**Meaning:** "There's no one/nothing but X" — strongly asserts that X is the only suitable choice, with nothing else even in the running.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + をおいて + (ほかに) + ～ない (noun + をおいて + negative predicate, often with ほかに inserted for emphasis)

**Typical context:** Formal, written/literary register; used in speeches, formal praise, or serious evaluative statements about a person's unique qualification. Not used in casual speech.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**にほかならない**](grammar-にほかならない.md) — "is nothing other than X"; asserts that something IS precisely X, whereas をおいて～ない asserts that nothing else BUT X qualifies for some role — different grammatical target (identity vs. exclusivity of choice).
- **しか～ない** — plain "only X"; far more casual and general-purpose, lacking the elevated, almost ceremonial tone of をおいて～ない.
- **にかぎる** — "nothing beats X / X is best"; expresses a personal preference/recommendation rather than the absolute, almost rhetorical exclusivity of をおいて～ない.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; conceptually 誰でもいい/何でもいい ("anyone/anything would do") expresses the reverse idea that there's no special exclusivity.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼をおいて、この難事件を解決できる探偵はいない。 (There's no detective but him who can solve this difficult case.)
2. この役を演じられる俳優は彼女をおいてほかにいないだろう。 (There's surely no actress but her who could play this role.)
3. この町の歴史を語れる人は、あの老人をおいてほかにいない。 (There's no one but that old man who can speak about this town's history.)
4. この国を率いるリーダーは彼をおいて考えられない。 (There's no leader conceivable to run this country but him.)
5. 彼女をおいて、このプロジェクトを任せられる人材はいない。 (There's no one but her to whom this project could be entrusted.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners sometimes drop the final negative predicate, but をおいて virtually always requires a following ～ない (often ほかにいない/ほかにない) to complete the "nothing but X" meaning — をおいて alone is incomplete. Also, don't confuse this with をおいて meaning "leaving X behind" (置いて, a literal placement sense) — the exclusivity usage is written 措いて in formal text and is a fixed idiomatic set, not a literal "put aside" meaning.
