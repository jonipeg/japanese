# ～にとどまらず {#にとどまらず}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に留まらず

**Furigana form:** ～にとどまらず

**Meaning:** "Not limited to X (but extends further to Y)" — indicates that the scope or impact of something exceeds a stated boundary.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + にとどまらず
- Example base: 国内 → 国内のみにとどまらず

**Typical context:** Formal, written register; common in news reporting, academic writing, and formal speech describing the expanding scope of an issue, phenomenon, or impact.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **にかぎらず** — "not limited to X"; nearly synonymous and often interchangeable, though にかぎらず can also be used in more everyday contexts, while にとどまらず leans more formal/written.
- **だけでなく** — "not only X (but also Y)"; a more general, everyday way to extend scope, lacking にとどまらず's formal, almost journalistic register.
- **はもとより** — "not to mention X (let alone Y)"; similarly extends scope but with a "needless to say" framing, rather than にとどまらず's "doesn't stop at" framing.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～にとどまる ("is limited/confined to X") is the direct affirmative opposite, stating that scope stays within bounds.

**Example sentences:**
1. ウイルス感染の被害は国内のみにとどまらず、海外にも広がっていった。 (The damage from the viral infection wasn't limited to within the country — it spread overseas as well.)
2. 彼の活躍は音楽の分野にとどまらず、映画業界にも及んでいる。 (His success isn't limited to the field of music — it extends into the film industry as well.)
3. この問題は一企業の責任にとどまらず、業界全体の構造的な課題だ。 (This problem isn't limited to one company's responsibility — it's a structural issue for the entire industry.)
4. 環境破壊の影響は現在の世代にとどまらず、将来の世代にも及ぶ。 (The effects of environmental destruction aren't limited to the current generation — they extend to future generations too.)
5. 彼女の研究は理論にとどまらず、実践的な応用にも力を入れている。 (Her research isn't limited to theory — she also focuses on practical applications.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にとどまらず is almost always followed by a clause introduced with も or さえ emphasizing the broader scope reached (…にとどまらず、～も／さえ…) — omitting that follow-up leaves the sentence incomplete. Learners should also note 留まる (とどまる) literally means "to stop/stay within," so にとどまらず literally reads "not stopping at X," reinforcing the sense of scope expanding beyond an initial boundary.
