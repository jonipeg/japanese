# ～弾みで／に {#はずみで・に}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～弾みで／～弾みに

**Furigana form:** ～はずみで／～はずみに

**Meaning:** "Due to the sudden impact/momentum of ~ / in the unexpected course of ~" — describes an unwanted or unintended result caused by a sudden physical impact, momentum, or impulsive moment.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞た形 (verb past form) + 弾みで／に
- 名詞の (noun + の) + 弾みで／に
- Example: 駅で男の人とぶつかった弾みにかばんを落としてしまった。 (In the sudden impact of bumping into a man at the station, I ended up dropping my bag.)

**Typical context:** Neutral spoken and written Japanese; used in anecdotal or incident-reporting contexts to describe how an unplanned mishap occurred due to a physical jolt or sudden event.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **拍子に** — "at the moment/beat of ~" — very similar in meaning and often interchangeable (ぶつかった拍子に), emphasizing the exact instant rather than physical momentum.
- **勢いで** — "due to the force/momentum of ~" — overlaps with the physical-force nuance but can also be used more broadly for emotional momentum (勢いで言ってしまった), unlike 弾みで which is tied more to sudden physical events.
- **はずみをつけて** — "to gain momentum" — a related but distinct expression meaning to intentionally build momentum, rather than describing an accidental result.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though 慎重に ("carefully, deliberately") contrasts in spirit by implying intentional caution rather than accidental momentum.

**Example sentences:**
1. 駅で男の人とぶつかった弾みにかばんを落としてしまった。
   (In the sudden impact of bumping into a man at the station, I ended up dropping my bag.)
2. 階段で転んだ弾みに足首をひねってしまった。
   (From the sudden fall on the stairs, I ended up twisting my ankle.)
3. 急ブレーキをかけた弾みで、荷物が座席から落ちた。
   (Due to the sudden jolt of hitting the brakes, the luggage fell from the seat.)
4. 友達と笑い合った弾みで、つい秘密を話してしまった。
   (Carried away by the momentum of laughing together with my friend, I ended up accidentally revealing the secret.)
5. ボールを蹴った弾みで、靴が脱げて飛んでいった。
   (From the momentum of kicking the ball, my shoe came off and flew away.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 弾みで／に almost always describes an unintended, often mildly unfortunate consequence triggered by a sudden physical or momentary event — learners should avoid using it for deliberate or planned actions, where 勢いで or わざと would be more appropriate.
