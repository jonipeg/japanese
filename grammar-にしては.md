# ～にしては {#にしては}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～にしては

**Furigana form:** ～にしては

**Meaning:** "For X, (surprisingly)..." — expresses that the following statement is unexpected or inconsistent given what one would normally assume about X; implies a mild contrast or surprise.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + にしては — 初心者にしては (for a beginner, [surprisingly well])
- 動詞普通形 + にしては — 練習していないにしては (for someone who hasn't practiced...)

**Typical context:** Neutral, spoken and written — used when commenting on something that defies expectations set by a category or claim.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**わりに**](grammar-わりに(は).md) — very similar "considering X, surprisingly," slightly more casual than にしては.
- [**にもかかわらず**](grammar-にかかわらず・にもかかわらず.md) — "despite X," more formal, used for a clearer, often more serious contrast.
- **としては** — "as X (standpoint)," lacks the surprise/contrast nuance — purely states a role.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は初心者にしては、とても上手にピアノを弾く。
   (For a beginner, he plays the piano very well.)
2. 今日は12月にしては暖かい。
   (For December, today is warm.)
3. この店はチェーン店にしては、料理がおいしい。
   (For a chain restaurant, the food here is delicious.)
4. 子供が作ったにしては、よくできている。
   (Considering a child made it, it's very well done.)
5. 彼は忙しいと言っていたにしては、随分のんびりしているね。
   (For someone who said he was busy, he sure seems quite relaxed.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にしては always implies a gap between expectation and reality — it cannot be used for a neutral, expected outcome. Learners sometimes confuse it with としては (plain role/standpoint, no surprise nuance) — "医者としては" (as a doctor, speaking in that capacity) vs. "医者にしては" (for a doctor, surprisingly...).
