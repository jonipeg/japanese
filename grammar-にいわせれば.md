# ～にいわせれば {#にいわせれば}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に言わせれば

**Furigana form:** ～にいわせれば

**Meaning:** "In X's view / if you ask X" — introduces a personal opinion or perspective, often one that contrasts with a more common or expected view.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に言わせれば
- Example base: 私 → 私に言わせれば

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-casual spoken and written register; commonly used to voice a contrarian or more candid personal opinion, implying "others may think differently, but here's my take."

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **から見れば** — "from X's viewpoint"; a more general, neutral perspective-marker, lacking に言わせれば's slightly argumentative, "let me tell you my frank opinion" edge.
- [**にとって**](grammar-にとって.md) — "for X / as far as X is concerned"; focuses on how something affects or matters to X, rather than specifically introducing X's spoken opinion/judgment.
- **の考えでは** — "in X's thinking"; a more neutral, formal way to introduce someone's view, without に言わせれば's implied contrast against a more conventional opinion.

**Opposite grammar structures:** 一般的には ("generally speaking") expresses the reverse — the common/mainstream view, often set up as what に言わせれば's personal opinion pushes back against.

**Example sentences:**
1. この店の料理はすばらしいと言われているけど、私に言わせればこれぐらい家庭でも作れますよ。 (This restaurant's food is said to be wonderful, but if you ask me, you could make something like this at home.)
2. 専門家に言わせれば、この計画にはまだ多くの問題があるそうだ。 (According to the experts, this plan apparently still has many problems.)
3. 彼に言わせれば、今の若者はもっと努力すべきだということらしい。 (According to him, today's young people should apparently work harder.)
4. 母に言わせれば、私の部屋はいつも散らかっているそうだ。 (According to my mother, my room is apparently always messy.)
5. 先生に言わせれば、この問題は意外と簡単に解けるらしい。 (According to the teacher, this problem is apparently surprisingly easy to solve.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に言わせれば typically introduces an opinion that pushes back against, or diverges from, a more widely-held view just mentioned — it carries a slightly assertive, "let me set the record straight from my perspective" tone rather than a neutral citation. Learners should distinguish it from から見れば, which is more neutral and doesn't carry this implicit contrastive edge against a prevailing opinion.
