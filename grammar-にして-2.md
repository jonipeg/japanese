# ～にして（評価・事の起こり方） {#にして-2}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～にして

**Furigana form:** ～にして

**Meaning:** States an evaluation of how a situation turned out, or describes the manner/speed in which an event occurred — e.g. 幸いにして "fortunately" (evaluates the outcome as lucky) and 一瞬にして "in an instant" (describes how quickly something happened). This is a distinct, more adverbial sense of ～にして from the "even as X" / "only at the stage of X" concessive usage covered elsewhere (see Lesson 17-④).

**Conjugation:**
- Attaches to a limited set of fixed words/expressions as a set adverbial phrase (the textbook gives no general connection rule — treat each item as idiomatic).
- 幸いにして体調が回復した — "fortunately, his health recovered"; 一瞬にしてがけが崩れた — "in an instant the cliff collapsed"

**Typical context:** Written, somewhat formal register — news reports, narration of dramatic, sudden, or fortunate/unfortunate events.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **あっという間に** — "in the blink of an eye," overlaps with 一瞬にして for expressing how quickly something happened.
- **幸い（にも）** — "luckily," close synonym of 幸いにして for the fortunate-outcome sense.
- **～にして（17課-④の用法）** — the other, more productive sense of ～にして meaning "even being at the level of X" (e.g. 天才にして失敗する) — do not confuse the two usages, even though the connective form looks identical.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No single opposite structure, but 不幸にして ("unfortunately") is the direct negative counterpart to 幸いにして within this same set of fixed expressions.

**Example sentences:**
1. 幸いにして、体調はすぐに回復した。
   (Fortunately, his health recovered quickly.)
2. 地震が起こり、一瞬にしてがけが崩れ道路をふさいでしまった。
   (The earthquake struck, and in an instant the cliff collapsed and blocked the road.)
3. 不幸にして、その知らせは本当だった。
   (Unfortunately, the news turned out to be true.)
4. 彼は一夜にして有名人になった。
   (He became famous overnight.)
5. 幸運にして、事故に巻き込まれずに済んだ。
   (Luckily, he escaped being caught up in the accident.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This usage of ～にして is limited to a small set of fixed adverbial expressions (幸いにして, 不幸にして, 一瞬にして, 一夜にして) that evaluate an outcome or describe how quickly/suddenly something happened. Don't confuse it with the separate concessive ～にして pattern ("even being X," covered at 17課-④) — same surface form, different meaning and different set of words it attaches to.
