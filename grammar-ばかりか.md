# ～ばかりか {#ばかりか}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ばかりか

**Furigana form:** ～ばかりか (no kanji reading needed; ばかり itself has no kanji in common usage)

**Meaning:** "Not only X, but also Y" — emphasizes that something goes beyond what is expected, adding a further (often more surprising or extreme) fact on top of the first. Similar to ばかりでなく but slightly more literary/emphatic.

**Conjugation:**
- 普通形 (plain form of verb/adjective) + ばかりか
- 名詞 + ばかりか
- 彼は英語ばかりか、フランス語も話せる。(Not only English, but he can also speak French.)
- Often paired with も, さえ, まで in the second clause to reinforce "even."

**Typical context:** Written and formal spoken Japanese — news reports, essays, formal speeches. Carries a slightly more emphatic/surprised nuance than ばかりでなく.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ばかりでなく** — near-synonym, slightly more neutral/common in everyday formal speech.
- [**のみならず**](grammar-のみならず.md) — more literary/formal, used in very formal writing.
- **だけでなく** — the most casual/common equivalent, usable in daily conversation.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though しか～ない ("only") expresses the limiting/restrictive opposite concept.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は料理が上手なばかりか、掃除や洗濯まで完璧にこなす。
   (Not only is he good at cooking, he even handles cleaning and laundry perfectly.)
2. この薬は副作用がないばかりか、効果も高いと評判だ。
   (This medicine not only has no side effects, it's also reputed to be highly effective.)
3. 台風で電車が止まったばかりか、停電まで起きた。
   (Not only did the trains stop because of the typhoon, but the power even went out.)
4. 彼女は試験に合格したばかりか、首席で卒業した。
   (She not only passed the exam, she graduated at the top of her class.)
5. その映画は子供ばかりか、大人にも人気がある。
   (That movie is popular not only with children, but with adults as well.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners often use ばかりか in casual conversation where だけでなく would sound more natural; ばかりか carries a written/formal register and often implies the added fact is surprising or noteworthy. Also note ばかりか cannot be used with negative/undesirable light topics as freely as ばかりに (different structure, different meaning — don't confuse the two).
