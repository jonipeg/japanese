# ～べからず／～べからざる {#べからず・べからざる}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～べからず／～べからざる

**Furigana form:** ～べからず／～べからざる

**Meaning:** Formal prohibition: "must not do X." べからず is the terminal (sentence-ending) form commonly seen on notices/signs; べからざる is the attributive form used to modify a following noun ("that must not be done").

**Conjugation:**
- V辞書形 + べからず (する → すべからず, irregular)
- V辞書形 + べからざる + N

**Typical context:** Extremely formal, archaic written register — found on old-style signs, legal/moral texts, and classical-influenced writing. Not used in modern spoken Japanese at all.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ざるをえない** — "have no choice but to do X," a different meaning entirely (compulsion to act), not prohibition against acting.
- [**べきだ**](grammar-べきだ.md) — the modern "should/must do X" cousin of べし; べからず is essentially its archaic negative-prohibitive counterpart, forbidding rather than recommending an action.
- **てはならない** — the modern, fully usable equivalent meaning "must not do X," far more natural in contemporary speech and writing than the archaic べからず.

**Opposite grammar structures:** **べし** — the archaic affirmative "should/must do X," the direct grammatical counterpart of べからず's prohibition.

**Example sentences:**
1. 関係者以外入るべからず。
   (Persons not concerned must not enter.)
2. 彼は知るべからざる秘密を知ってしまったために命を狙われた。
   (Because he came to know a secret he should never have known, his life was targeted.)
3. 初心忘るべからず。
   (One must never forget one's beginner's spirit.)
4. ここに立ち入るべからず。
   (One must not enter this area.)
5. 許すべからざる行為に、国民は激怒した。
   (The public was enraged by this unforgivable act.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** べからず/べからざる is the most archaic member of the N1 extreme-degree/prohibition cluster, and students frequently mistake it for the modern てはならない or ざるをえない. The key distinguishing point: べからず is a classical, fixed-expression prohibition seen almost exclusively on signs and set phrases (入るべからず, 忘るべからず) — if the sentence sounds like modern spoken prohibition, てはならない is the natural choice; べからず signals old-fashioned, formal, or quoted/idiomatic register.
