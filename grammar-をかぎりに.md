# ～をかぎりに {#をかぎりに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を限りに

**Furigana form:** ～をかぎりに

**Meaning:** "As of X" — marks a specific point (often "today" or a stated event) as the cutoff or final occurrence of something, typically signaling the end of a habit, relationship, or practice.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (noun, often a time expression) + をかぎりに
- Example from source: 今日を限りに (as of today / today being the last day)

**Typical context:** Formal-to-neutral written and spoken Japanese — often used in dramatic or decisive declarations (ending a relationship, retiring from something, a final performance). Carries a sense of finality and resolve; can sound emotionally charged or solemn depending on context, but is not rude.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**をもって**](grammar-をもって.md) — "as of X / ending at X," nearly synonymous in its cutoff-point sense; をもって is more formal/bureaucratic (business announcements, official notices), while をかぎりに carries more emotional weight and is common in personal, dramatic declarations.
- **以降 (いこう)** — "from X onward"; marks the starting point of a continuing period after X, the temporal opposite emphasis of をかぎりに's "this is the last time."
- **限り(は) (かぎりは)** — "as long as X (is the case)"; a conditional meaning "while this condition holds," which is a different grammatical function from をかぎりに's "marking a final cutoff point."
- **最後に (さいごに)** — "lastly / for the last time"; a plain adverbial expression with similar finality, but without をかぎりに's more literary, declarative tone.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～から ("starting from X") or 以降 ("from X onward") function as conceptual opposites, marking a beginning rather than an ending.

**Example sentences:**
1. 今日を限りにあなたとは口をききません。
   (As of today, I will not speak to you anymore.)
2. 彼は今シーズンを限りに、現役を引退することを発表した。
   (He announced that he will retire from active competition as of this season.)
3. この店は今月を限りに閉店することになった。
   (This shop will close as of the end of this month.)
4. その夜を限りに、二人は二度と会うことはなかった。
   (As of that night, the two of them never met again.)
5. 今日の演奏を限りに、このオーケストラは解散する。
   (As of today's performance, this orchestra will disband.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** No confirmed student wrong-answer is recorded in the source for this pattern. A key nuance for learners: をかぎりに almost always marks an emotionally significant, often final and irreversible cutoff (ending a relationship, retiring, disbanding), distinguishing it from the more neutral, administrative tone of をもって used for the same "cutoff" sense (e.g., business hours ending) — students should gauge the emotional weight of the sentence to pick the more natural-sounding option.
