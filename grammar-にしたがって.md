# ～にしたがって {#にしたがって}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に従って

**Furigana form:** ～にしたがって

**Meaning:** Two main uses: (1) "In accordance with X / following X (a rule, instruction)," and (2) "As X happens, Y proportionally happens too" — a gradual, correlated change.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に従って — 規則に従って (in accordance with the rules)
- 動詞辞書形 + に従って — 年を取るに従って (as one ages)
- Noun-modifying form: 名詞 + に従う + 名詞 — 指示に従う行動 (actions that follow instructions)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, common in both speech and writing — instructions, legal/official language, and descriptions of gradual change.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**につれて**](grammar-につれて.md) — very similar to the "proportional change" sense of にしたがって, but cannot be used for the "obeying rules/instructions" sense.
- **に伴って** — "accompanying X," overlaps with the change sense, more formal/written, often for larger-scale phenomena.
- **通りに** — "exactly as X (instructed)," stricter adherence nuance than にしたがって for the rule-following sense.

**Opposite grammar structures:** ～に逆らって ("against/defying X") is a thematic opposite for the rule-following sense.

**Example sentences:**
1. 係員の指示に従って、避難してください。
   (Please evacuate in accordance with the staff's instructions.)
2. 収入が増えるに従って、税金も高くなる。
   (As income increases, taxes also rise accordingly.)
3. 時間が経つに従って、彼の態度は軟化していった。
   (As time passed, his attitude gradually softened.)
4. 会社の規則に従って、休暇を申請した。
   (I applied for leave in accordance with company rules.)
5. 標高が高くなるに従って、気温は下がっていく。
   (As altitude increases, the temperature gradually drops.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** にしたがって has two meanings that learners often conflate; context (whether a rule/instruction or a gradual natural change is involved) determines which sense applies. Unlike につれて, にしたがって can describe following rules/orders — a usage につれて cannot cover.
