# ～んがため(に) {#んがため}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～んがため(に)

**Furigana form:** ～んがため(に)

**Meaning:** "In order to X" — a literary expression of strong purpose or intent, derived from the archaic auxiliary ～んとする ("to be about to do X / intend to do X"); conveys a determined, often desperate or resolute effort to achieve a goal.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞ない形 (verb ない-form, dropping ない and attaching ん) + がため(に) — e.g., 結婚せんがために (in order to marry); irregular verb する becomes せん (not しん)
- Example from source: 結婚せんがために (in order to marry)

**Typical context:** Highly formal, literary/written Japanese — archaic in origin and essentially never used in casual spoken conversation. Appears in formal writing, speeches, or dramatic narrative to express strong, determined purpose. Not rude, but very bookish; overusing it in conversation would sound stilted or overly dramatic.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ために** — the everyday, neutral equivalent "in order to X"; んがために is simply a more literary, emphatic, and archaic-flavored version of the same purpose meaning.
- [**べく**](grammar-べく.md) — "in order to X / so as to X," also literary and formal, often used for a stated intention/duty-driven purpose (e.g., 急ぐべく); べく tends to emphasize "as one should, in order to," while んがため(に) emphasizes a more urgent, determined drive toward a goal.
- **んとする** — "to be about to do X / to attempt to do X," the direct source construction that んがため is derived from; んとする describes being on the verge of an action, while んがため describes acting for the purpose of achieving that action's goal.
- [**ものと思われる**](grammar-ものと思われる.md) — "it is thought/believed that..."; unrelated meaning (an inference/supposition marker), only superficially similar due to shared formal/written register.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼女は彼と結婚せんがために、自分の過去を偽っていた。
   (In order to marry him, she had been hiding her past.)
2. 彼は夢を叶えんがために、全てを犠牲にした。
   (In order to achieve his dream, he sacrificed everything.)
3. 生きんがために、彼らは厳しい自然と戦い続けた。
   (In order to survive, they continued to fight against the harsh natural environment.)
4. 真実を明らかにせんがため、彼女は長年調査を続けた。
   (In order to bring the truth to light, she continued her investigation for many years.)
5. 国を守らんがため、兵士たちは命を懸けて戦った。
   (In order to protect their country, the soldiers fought at the risk of their lives.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This entry notes it was listed twice in scan4's digest (reinforced), indicating it's a frequently tested pattern. A key nuance for learners: んがため(に) requires converting the verb to its ない-form stem before attaching ん, and irregular する becomes せん (not する+ん) — a common error is misconjugating する-verbs in this pattern. Students should also remember this expression conveys heightened, often desperate determination, making it inappropriate for mundane, everyday purposes (reserve ために for those).
