# ～において {#において}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～において／における

**Furigana form:** ～において／における (no special kanji readings needed)

**Meaning:** "In," "at," "regarding" — a formal marker for location, time, field, or domain, used in place of で/に in formal or written contexts. における is the attributive (pre-noun) form.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + において (adverbial): 現代社会において (in contemporary society)
- 名詞 + における + 名詞 (attributive, modifying a following noun): 日本における経済問題 (economic issues in Japan)

**Typical context:** Formal, written register — academic papers, official reports, speeches, legal/business documents. Sounds stiff or unnatural in casual conversation.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**で**](grammar-で.md) — the plain, everyday equivalent for location/context, used in casual speech.
- **に関して／に関する** — "regarding/concerning," overlaps when talking about topics/fields but without the "setting/domain" nuance.
- **の場合において** — more verbose variant emphasizing "in the case of."

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 現代社会において、インターネットは欠かせない存在だ。
   (In contemporary society, the internet is an indispensable presence.)
2. この会議において、重要な決定がなされた。
   (At this meeting, an important decision was made.)
3. 日本における少子化問題は深刻だ。
   (The declining birthrate problem in Japan is serious.)
4. 職場においては、礼儀正しさが求められる。
   (In the workplace, politeness is required.)
5. 教育における格差は、今後も議論されるべきだ。
   (Disparities in education should continue to be discussed going forward.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** において is strictly formal/written — learners should avoid it in everyday conversation where で or に is natural. Remember the attributive form is における (not においての) when directly modifying a following noun.
