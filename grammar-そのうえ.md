# そのうえ {#そのうえ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** その上

**Furigana form:** そのうえ

**Meaning:** "On top of that, in addition" — adds a notable extra point, similar to しかも but more neutral in tone.

**Conjugation:** Sentence-initial conjunction, no conjugation needed — attaches at the sentence boundary, connecting the previous sentence to the next.

**Typical context:** Written-leaning, neutral-to-formal register, usable in both essays/reports and careful spoken Japanese. It adds a further point that compounds the first, similar to しかも, but without as strong a flavor of surprise — it reads as a more composed, matter-of-fact "and in addition to that" compared to しかも's more vivid "what's more."

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**しかも**](grammar-しかも.md) — adds a surprising/notable additional fact; close synonym, but しかも carries a stronger sense of surprise or heightened impact than the more neutral そのうえ.
- [**さらに**](grammar-さらに.md) — "furthermore, moreover," adds escalating emphasis; similar degree-increasing function to そのうえ, though さらに is slightly more about intensifying degree while そのうえ is about appending another related fact.
- [**それに**](grammar-それに.md) — adds a supplementary point reinforcing an argument; more casual/conversational than そのうえ's composed written tone.
- [**また**](grammar-また.md) — neutral listing of parallel items; also just plain addition.
- [**それも**](grammar-それも.md) — emphatic/exasperated reinforcement of the immediately preceding point; そのうえ is calmer and more neutral by comparison.
- [**あと**](grammar-あと.md) — casual spoken "also," afterthought in tone; far more informal than そのうえ.

**Opposite grammar structures:** しかし/だが (contrastive "however" conjunctions) are the functional opposite, since they counter the preceding statement rather than add a compounding point to it.

**Example sentences:**
1. この部屋は広い。そのうえ、日当たりもいい。
   (This room is spacious. On top of that, it gets good sunlight too.)
2. 彼は仕事ができる。そのうえ、人望も厚い。
   (He's capable at his job. On top of that, he's also well-respected by others.)
3. 道が渋滞していた。そのうえ、雨まで降り出した。
   (The road was congested. On top of that, it even started raining.)
4. この教材は分かりやすい。そのうえ、値段も安い。
   (This learning material is easy to understand. On top of that, it's inexpensive too.)
5. 彼女は責任感が強い。そのうえ、判断力にも優れている。
   (She has a strong sense of responsibility. On top of that, she also has excellent judgment.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners often treat そのうえ as fully interchangeable with しかも, but そのうえ is more neutral and composed, while しかも leans more dramatic/surprising — in a formal report, そのうえ often reads more naturally, while しかも can feel a touch more conversational or emphatic. Both are stronger/more written than それに, and distinct from それも's exasperated-emphasis nuance.
