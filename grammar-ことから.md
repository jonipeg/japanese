# ～ことから {#ことから}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ことから

**Furigana form:** ～ことから (no special kanji reading)

**Meaning:** "Judging from the fact that X / because of the fact that X" — used to explain the reason, origin, or basis for a conclusion, name, or naming convention.

**Conjugation:**
- Verb/い-adj (plain form) + ことから
- な-adj + な / 名詞 + である + ことから
- 顔が赤いことから「赤鬼」と呼ばれている (Because his face is red, he is called "Red Demon")

**Typical context:** Written and explanatory style — news reports, essays, encyclopedic descriptions explaining origins of names or conclusions drawn from observations. Neutral-to-formal.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ので** — general causal "because," more conversational and lacks the "based on observable evidence/origin" nuance.
- **ため** — formal cause/reason, but doesn't carry the "this is how it came to be named/concluded" flavor.
- **という理由で** — "for the reason that," more explicit but less literary.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. この花は朝だけ咲くことから「朝顔」と呼ばれている。
   (Because this flower only blooms in the morning, it is called "morning glory.")
2. 彼はいつも冷静なことから、周りの信頼を集めている。
   (Because he is always calm, he has earned the trust of those around him.)
3. 証拠が何も残っていないことから、事件は迷宮入りした。
   (Because no evidence remained, the case went unsolved.)
4. 二人は同じ大学出身であることから、すぐに意気投合した。
   (Because they were both from the same university, they hit it off right away.)
5. 雨が降り続いていることから、イベントは中止になった。
   (Because the rain kept falling, the event was cancelled.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** ことから emphasizes a logical/evidential basis (often for a name, nickname, or conclusion), unlike plain ので which just states a cause. It's often used for explaining the origin of names or titles — a nuance beginners miss when they substitute plain causal forms.
