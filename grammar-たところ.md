# ～たところ {#たところ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～たところ

**Furigana form:** ～たところ (no additional kanji readings needed)

**Meaning:** "When I did X, (as a result I discovered/found) Y" — connects a completed action to an ensuing, often unexpected, discovery or result; not a plain temporal "when."

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞た形 (verb, past plain form) + ところ

**Typical context:** Written narrative and somewhat formal spoken Japanese, often used in reports or stories describing sequential discovery.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**すると**](grammar-すると.md) — "then, thereupon," connects two separate sentences rather than attaching directly to a verb.
- **たら** — general conditional "when/if," broader usage without the discovery nuance.
- **結果** — "as a result," more explicit and formal.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 先生に聞いたところ、宿題は来週までということだった。
   (When I asked the teacher, it turned out the homework was due next week.)
2. 新商品を試してみたところ、とても使いやすかった。
   (When I tried the new product, I found it very easy to use.)
3. 警察が調べたところ、犯人は近所の住人だった。
   (When the police investigated, it turned out the culprit was a neighbor.)
4. 病院で検査を受けたところ、特に異常はなかった。
   (When I had a checkup at the hospital, there turned out to be nothing wrong.)
5. 彼に電話をかけたところ、留守番電話だった。
   (When I called him, it went to voicemail.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** たところ is not a plain "when" like とき — it specifically implies the following clause describes an unexpected finding or result of the preceding action, and cannot be used for habitual or general statements.
