# ～にそくして／にそくしたN {#にそくして}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に即して／に即したN

**Furigana form:** ～にそくして／にそくしたN

**Meaning:** "Based on/in line with X" — describes acting, judging, or explaining in close conformity with a particular standard, reality, or set of facts, rather than abstractly or ideally.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + に即して
- 名詞 + に即した + 名詞 (attributive form before a noun)
- Example base: 現実 → 現実に即して

**Typical context:** Formal, written register; common in academic, administrative, and policy-related writing to emphasize that a judgment or plan is grounded in concrete reality or established criteria rather than being merely theoretical.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**にてらして**](grammar-にてらして.md) — "checked/compared against X"; similarly references a standard for judgment, but に照らして emphasizes the act of comparing/verifying against a rule, whereas に即して emphasizes closely conforming to or being grounded in actual conditions.
- [**に基づいて**](grammar-に基づいて.md) — "based on X"; overlaps substantially, though に基づいて more often references a formal foundation (data, law), while に即して emphasizes practical alignment with real, concrete circumstances.
- [**に沿って**](grammar-に沿って.md) — "in accordance with X / along the lines of X"; close in meaning, often used for following a plan or policy, while に即して leans more toward grounding a judgment in actual facts/reality rather than following a procedural guideline.

**Opposite grammar structures:** 理想論として／抽象的に ("as an ideal argument / abstractly") expresses the reverse — detached from concrete, grounded reality.

**Example sentences:**
1. 現実に即した対策を立てる必要がある。 (We need to formulate countermeasures grounded in reality.)
2. 生徒の理解度に即して授業を進めている。 (The lessons are being conducted in line with the students' level of understanding.)
3. 法律に即して適切な判断を下した。 (An appropriate judgment was made in accordance with the law.)
4. 時代の変化に即して、制度も見直すべきだ。 (In line with the changing times, the system should also be reviewed.)
5. 現場の状況に即して柔軟に対応することが求められる。 (A flexible response in line with the actual on-site situation is required.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** に即して specifically emphasizes grounding something in concrete, practical reality (現実, 実情, 実態) rather than abstract principle — using it with a purely abstract or theoretical noun sounds slightly off; に基づいて or に沿って would be more natural there. Learners frequently confuse the three similar formal patterns に即して／に照らして／に基づいて; remembering 即する's core meaning "to closely fit/conform to" helps anchor its specific "grounded in actual conditions" nuance.
