# ～を頼りに {#を頼りに}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～を頼りに

**Furigana form:** ～をたよりに

**Meaning:** "Relying on ~ / depending on the help of ~" — describes using something or someone (memory, a map, a person's help) as one's main support or guide in accomplishing something, often in an uncertain or difficult situation.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (noun) + を頼りに
- Example: 昔の記憶を頼りに、学生時代によく通ったラーメン屋を探した。 (Relying on my old memories, I searched for the ramen shop I used to frequent as a student.)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal spoken and written Japanese; common in narrative and descriptive contexts about navigating difficulty with limited resources or support.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**をもとに**](grammar-をもとに(して).md) — "based on ~" — similar in using something as a foundation, but emphasizes a source of information/material rather than reliance for support or guidance.
- **に頼る** — the base verb form (頼る, "to rely on") used independently rather than as a connective phrase modifying another clause.
- **を手がかりに** — "using ~ as a clue/lead" — similar in guiding an action, but specifically about using a clue to figure something out, rather than general reliance/support.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though 自力で ("by one's own effort, without relying on anything") contrasts in spirit.

**Example sentences:**
1. 昔の記憶を頼りに、学生時代によく通ったラーメン屋を探した。
   (Relying on my old memories, I searched for the ramen shop I used to frequent as a student.)
2. 地図を頼りに、山奥にある温泉宿までたどり着いた。
   (Relying on a map, I made it all the way to the hot spring inn deep in the mountains.)
3. 友人のアドバイスを頼りに、初めての転職活動を進めた。
   (Relying on my friend's advice, I proceeded with my first job-hunting experience.)
4. 彼は杖を頼りに、ゆっくりと階段を上っていった。
   (Relying on his cane, he slowly climbed the stairs.)
5. わずかな手がかりを頼りに、警察は犯人の行方を追った。
   (Relying on scant clues, the police pursued the whereabouts of the culprit.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** を頼りに implies dependence on something limited or uncertain (old memory, a cane, scant information) to accomplish a task despite difficulty — learners should distinguish it from をもとに, which is more neutral and used for clearly defined source material (data, a novel's original story, etc.) rather than a precarious means of support.
