# また {#また}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** 又 (rarely written in kanji; conventionally kana)

**Furigana form:** また

**Meaning:** "Also, additionally" — a neutral conjunction listing parallel items or facts.

**Conjugation:** Sentence-initial conjunction, no conjugation needed — attaches at the sentence boundary, connecting the previous sentence to the next.

**Typical context:** Neutral, formal-friendly register, common in written Japanese such as reports, manuals, announcements, and formal speeches, where it introduces an additional, parallel point of equal weight to the first (often used to list separate requirements, facts, or features). Usable in spoken Japanese as well, but reads as slightly more composed/structured than casual あと.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**そして**](grammar-そして.md) — neutral sequential "and then," more about event order/narrative flow than listing parallel facts like また.
- [**それに**](grammar-それに.md) — adds a supplementary point reinforcing an argument; また is more neutral, simply listing an additional parallel fact without persuasive flavor.
- [**および**](grammar-および.md) — very formal/written "and; as well as," used in official documents and lists; even more formal and rarely spoken compared to また.
- [**あと**](grammar-あと.md) — casual spoken "also," often added as an afterthought; much more informal than また.
- [**さらに**](grammar-さらに.md) — "furthermore, moreover," adds escalating emphasis; stronger in degree than また's plain, equal-weight listing.
- **しかも/そのうえ** — add a surprising or notable extra fact; また is plainer and doesn't carry that added-emphasis nuance.

**Opposite grammar structures:** しかし/だが (contrastive "however" conjunctions) are the functional opposite, since they interrupt with contrast rather than adding a parallel point.

**Example sentences:**
1. この公園は広い。また、緑も豊かだ。
   (This park is spacious. It's also rich in greenery.)
2. 申込書にはお名前をご記入ください。また、電話番号も必要です。
   (Please fill in your name on the application form. Also, a phone number is required.)
3. 彼は英語が話せる。また、フランス語も少し分かる。
   (He can speak English. He also understands a bit of French.)
4. この薬は眠気を引き起こすことがある。また、空腹時の服用は避けてください。
   (This medicine may cause drowsiness. Also, please avoid taking it on an empty stomach.)
5. 新しいオフィスは駅から近い。また、設備もとても整っている。
   (The new office is close to the station. It also has very well-equipped facilities.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners sometimes use また where それに (argument-reinforcing) or さらに (escalating emphasis) would be more precise — また is the most neutral of the three, simply presenting an additional fact of comparable weight without implying it strengthens an argument or ramps up intensity. Also note また has a separate, very common adverbial usage meaning "again" (また会おうね "let's meet again") — context usually disambiguates it from the sentence-initial "also" conjunction use covered here.
