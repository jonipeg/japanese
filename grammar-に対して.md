# ～に対して {#に対して}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～に対して

**Furigana form:** ～にたいして

**Meaning:** "Towards X / in regard to X / in contrast to X" — indicates the target or direction of an action, attitude, or feeling, or sets up a contrast between two things.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 (noun) + に対して
- Also appears as に対する + noun (attributive form), e.g. 彼に対する態度 (attitude towards him)

**Typical context:** Neutral-to-formal, used in both speech and writing — common in essays, news reports, and polite conversation when discussing attitudes, treatment, or comparisons.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**について**](grammar-について・に関して.md) — "about/concerning X," simply marks a topic, without the directional/confrontational nuance of に対して.
- [**に関して**](grammar-について・に関して.md) — "regarding X," more formal, topic-marking like について but drier/more written.
- [**に比べて**](grammar-に比べて.md) — "compared to X," explicitly comparative rather than directional.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though in its contrastive sense it already implies a "whereas" relationship.

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は後輩に対して、いつも優しく接している。
   (He always treats his juniors kindly.)
2. この問題に対して、どう思いますか。
   (What do you think about this issue?)
3. 兄は几帳面なのに対して、弟は大雑把だ。
   (Whereas my older brother is meticulous, my younger brother is sloppy.)
4. 政府の対応に対して、国民から批判の声が上がった。
   (Criticism arose from the public toward the government's response.)
5. 彼女は自分の子供に対して、厳しすぎると言われている。
   (She is said to be too strict toward her own children.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Learners often confuse に対して with について/に関して — に対して implies a directed attitude, action, or contrast (often with an emotional or evaluative charge), while について/に関して are neutral topic markers. Don't use に対して for purely informational topics like "a book about history" (historyについての本, not に対して).
