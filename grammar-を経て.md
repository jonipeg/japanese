# ～を経て {#を経て}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～を経て

**Furigana form:** ～をへて

**Meaning:** "Having gone through / passed through X" — describes reaching a current state or result only after passing through a certain process, stage, or period of time.

**Conjugation:**
- 名詞 + を経て／を経た＋名詞
- 長い審査を経て — "after a long screening process"

**Typical context:** Formal written register — common in narratives of personal history, company histories, biographies, and formal reports describing a sequence of stages.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**を通して**](grammar-を通して・を通じて.md) — "through X," focuses more on means or a channel than on time passing through stages.
- **のすえに** — "after X (a difficult or prolonged process)," often implies hardship before a final result.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a phrase like 直接 ("directly, without an intervening process") contrasts with the idea of passing through stages.

**Example sentences:**
1. 3年の交際を経て、二人は結婚した。
   (After three years of dating, the two got married.)
2. わが社は創立以来、さまざまな試練を経て今に至ります。
   (Since its founding, our company has gone through various trials to reach where it is today.)
3. 長い審査を経て、ようやく許可が下りた。
   (After a long screening process, permission was finally granted.)
4. 彼は海外勤務を経て、本社の部長に就任した。
   (After working overseas, he was appointed department manager at headquarters.)
5. 幾多の困難を経て、プロジェクトは完成した。
   (After overcoming numerous difficulties, the project was completed.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** を経て emphasizes a time period, process, or series of stages (交際, 試練, 審査, 勤務) that must be passed through before a result is reached; it's most natural with nouns describing a duration or process rather than a single instantaneous event. It's often used in formal CVs, histories, and narrative summaries.
