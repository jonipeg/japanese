# ～っぱなしだ {#っぱなしだ}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar
#to-review

**Kanji form:** ～っぱなしだ

**Furigana form:** ～っぱなしだ

**Meaning:** Describes an unusual or improper state that is continuing — typically an action left undone that should have been reversed, or a one-sided position endured for too long.

**Conjugation:**
- 動ます形 + っぱなしだ
- 電気をつけっぱなしで寝てしまった — "fell asleep with the light left on"

**Typical context:** Casual spoken Japanese — everyday conversation about forgetting to undo something, or about enduring an uncomfortable or unfair ongoing situation; frequently carries a complaint.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **～たまま** — "leaving a state as it is" — broader and more neutral, since it can describe perfectly fine ongoing states too, not just negative ones.
- **～続ける** — "to keep on doing X" — describes a continuing action in general, without っぱなし's implication of carelessness or being left unattended.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; a phrase like ちゃんと～する ("to properly do X") describes the corrective, attentive counterpart instead.

**Example sentences:**
1. 昨日は電気をつけっぱなしで寝てしまった。
   (Yesterday I fell asleep with the light left on.)
2. 友達に半年も本を借りっぱなしになっている。
   (I've had a book borrowed from a friend for half a year now without returning it.)
3. 一日立ちっぱなしの仕事なので、足が疲れる。
   (It's a job where I'm on my feet all day, so my legs get tired.)
4. 相手も悪いのに、わたしだけ言われっぱなしで何も言い返せなかった。
   (The other person was in the wrong too, but I just took the criticism without being able to say anything back.)
5. 掃除をさぼっていたら、部屋が散らかりっぱなしになってしまった。
   (I'd been skipping cleaning, and now the room has just stayed a mess.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Mostly describes negative or improper states — something left undone that should have been attended to, or a one-sided situation endured without pushing back. Frequently expresses complaint, dissatisfaction, or self-criticism about carelessness, rather than neutrally describing a continuing state.
