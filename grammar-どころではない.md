# ～どころではない {#どころではない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～どころではない

**Furigana form:** ～どころではない (no special kanji readings needed)

**Meaning:** "It's not the time/situation for X," "far from being able to do X" — expresses that circumstances make a certain action impossible or inappropriate, usually due to a more pressing concern.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞辞書形／名詞 + どころではない
- 忙しくて旅行どころではない。(I'm too busy — it's not the time for traveling.)
- 風邪がひどくて、勉強どころではなかった。(My cold was so bad, studying was out of the question.)

**Typical context:** Neutral, spoken and written; used to explain why a seemingly normal activity is currently impossible due to urgent or overwhelming circumstances.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **ている場合ではない** — "this isn't the time to be doing X," very similar nuance, slightly more direct/urgent.
- [**どころか**](grammar-どころか.md) — emphatic contrast ("far from X, actually Y"), different function (does not mean "not the time for").
- [**わけにはいかない**](grammar-わけにはいかない.md) — "can't possibly do X" (due to social/moral obligation), different reasoning (obligation vs. circumstance).

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite.

**Example sentences:**
1. 締め切りが近くて、休むどころではない。
   (The deadline is close — this isn't the time to be resting.)
2. 地震の後は、仕事どころではなかった。
   (After the earthquake, work was the last thing on my mind.)
3. 赤ちゃんが夜泣きして、ゆっくり眠るどころではない。
   (The baby cries at night, so getting a good sleep is out of the question.)
4. 台風が来ていて、旅行どころではない。
   (With the typhoon coming, this isn't the time for a trip.)
5. 試験前で忙しくて、デートどころではなかった。
   (I was so busy before the exam that dating was out of the question.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** This pattern emphasizes that the situation is too serious/urgent for the mentioned activity — it's stronger than simply "I'm busy, so I can't." Learners sometimes confuse it with どころか; どころではない always concerns feasibility/appropriateness given circumstances, while どころか sets up a contrast between an expectation and a surprising reality.
