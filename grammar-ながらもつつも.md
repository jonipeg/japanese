# ～ながらも／つつも {#ながらもつつも}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ながらも／つつも

**Furigana form:** ～ながらも／つつも

**Meaning:** "Although X, nonetheless Y" — a concessive connector acknowledging one state or action while the main clause presents a contrasting or unexpected outcome.

**Conjugation:**
- 動詞ます形／い形容詞／な形容詞語幹 + ながらも
- 動詞ます形 + つつも
- Example base: わかる → わかりながらも／わかりつつも

**Typical context:** Formal-to-neutral, usable in both speech and writing; expresses a thoughtful acknowledgment of tension between two facts, common in reflective or explanatory statements. More composed than plain けど/のに.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- **のに** — plain "even though X, Y (unexpectedly)"; a more casual, everyday concessive, often carrying surprise or complaint, while ながらも／つつも are more measured and literary, typically describing a willing or self-aware concession rather than a surprised complaint.
- [**けれども**](grammar-けれども.md) — general-purpose "but/however"; a broader, more neutral contrast connector, lacking the single-clause, same-subject "even while doing X" structure of ながらも／つつも.
- [**とはいえ**](grammar-とはいえ.md) — "although it's true that X, nonetheless Y"; operates across a full preceding statement rather than attaching directly to a single verb/adjective stem, and is slightly more formal in register.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite; だから／それゆえに ("therefore, because of that") expresses a non-concessive, consequential link instead of a concessive one.

**Example sentences:**
1. 体に悪いとわかっていながらも、たばこをやめられない。 (Even though I know it's bad for my health, I can't quit smoking.)
2. 彼は忙しいながらも、必ず家族との時間を作っている。 (Even though he's busy, he always makes time for his family.)
3. 不安を感じつつも、彼女は新しい挑戦に踏み出した。 (Even while feeling anxious, she took the step into a new challenge.)
4. 小さいながらも、この店は地元で評判がいい。 (Even though it's small, this shop has a good reputation locally.)
5. 反省しつつも、彼はまた同じ失敗を繰り返してしまった。 (Even while reflecting on his mistake, he ended up repeating the same failure.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** ながらも and つつも are largely interchangeable in the concessive sense, though ながらも can also attach to adjectives and nouns (小さいながらも) while つつも is restricted to verb stems — learners should check which word class precedes the connector before choosing one. Both require the same subject throughout the sentence (unlike のに, which can shift subjects), and both carry a slightly more composed, self-aware tone than casual のに or けど.
