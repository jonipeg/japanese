## 湾 (わん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** bay, inlet, gulf

**Similar words:**
- 入り江（いりえ）— a smaller inlet/cove, narrower and more intimate in scale compared to a larger 湾 (bay/gulf).
- 海岸（かいがん）— coast/seashore in general, a broader term for the shoreline rather than a specific curved body of water like 湾.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 東京湾には多くの船が行き交う。— Many ships come and go in Tokyo Bay.
2. この湾は漁業が盛んだ。— Fishing is thriving in this bay.
3. 湾に面したホテルに泊まった。— I stayed at a hotel facing the bay.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 湾 is used for named geographic bays/gulfs (東京湾, ペルシャ湾) — it's a formal geographic term, distinct from the more casual 入り江 for small coves.

## ワンピース
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** one-piece dress

**Similar words:**
- ドレス — "dress," often implying a more formal or elegant garment (e.g. for a party), whereas ワンピース covers casual-to-formal one-piece dresses broadly.
- スーツ — a suit (separate top and bottom pieces), the structural opposite of a ワンピース, which is a single connected garment.

**Opposite words:**
- セットアップ — a matching separate two-piece outfit (top + bottom), contrasted with the single-piece construction of ワンピース

**Example sentences:**
1. 夏らしい花柄のワンピースを買った。— I bought a summery floral one-piece dress.
2. 彼女は結婚式に黒いワンピースを着ていった。— She wore a black one-piece dress to the wedding.
3. このワンピースはウエストが絞られたデザインだ。— This dress has a design that's cinched at the waist.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** In Japanese, ワンピース refers specifically to a one-piece dress (clothing) — it's a wasei-eigo term and should not be confused with the English "jumpsuit" or the manga/anime title "One Piece," which share the same loanword spelling but different referents.

## ~を通して (~をとおして)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** through, throughout, via (a means, period, or intermediary)

**Similar words:**
- ~を通じて（をつうじて）— nearly synonymous, often interchangeable; 〜を通じて can feel slightly more formal/written, while 〜を通して is common in both speech and writing.
- ~によって — "by means of," used for the agent/method causing a result, more about causation than the "via/throughout" sense of 〜を通して.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼女は友人を通してその情報を知った。— She learned that information through a friend.
2. 一年を通して温暖な気候が続く。— A mild climate continues throughout the year.
3. 交渉は通訳を通して行われた。— The negotiation was carried out through an interpreter.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 〜を通して and 〜を通じて are largely interchangeable, but 〜を通して more often emphasizes the entire duration/medium being traversed (一年を通して "throughout the year"), while 〜を通じて can lean slightly more toward means/channel of communication.

## ~を始め (~をはじめ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** starting with..., such as..., including (introducing a representative example from a larger group)

**Similar words:**
- ~など — "and so on, etc.," a simpler way to list examples without highlighting one as the most prominent/representative, unlike 〜を始め which spotlights the lead example.
- ~をはじめとして — a slightly more formal/extended variant of 〜を始め with the same meaning, often used in written/formal contexts.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 社長を始め、多くの社員がパーティーに参加した。— Starting with the president, many employees attended the party.
2. 日本を始め、アジア各国でこの商品が人気だ。— This product is popular in Asian countries, starting with Japan.
3. 彼は英語を始め、三か国語を話せる。— He can speak three languages, including English.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 〜を始め singles out the most prominent/representative example before listing a broader category — it's not interchangeable with plain 〜など, which lists examples without implying any one is more notable.

## ~をめぐる
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** concerning, over, surrounding (a contested topic or issue)

**Similar words:**
- ~に関する（にかんする）— "concerning/related to," a neutral way to indicate topic relevance; 〜をめぐる specifically implies debate, conflict, or controversy surrounding the topic.
- ~について — "about," the most basic and neutral way to introduce a topic, without the connotation of dispute that 〜をめぐる carries.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. その土地をめぐる争いが続いている。— The dispute over that land continues.
2. 政策をめぐる議論が国会で行われた。— A debate over the policy was held in the Diet.
3. 遺産をめぐる家族間のトラブルが起きた。— Trouble broke out among family members over the inheritance.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 〜をめぐる specifically implies the topic is contested or disputed (often followed by words like 争い, 議論, トラブル) — using it for a neutral, non-controversial topic sounds odd; for plain "about X," use 〜について or 〜に関する instead.
