## 県 (けん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** prefecture

**Similar words:**
- 都道府県（とどうふけん）— the collective term for all of Japan's administrative divisions (to, do, fu, ken); 県 is just one of the four types (used for most of Japan).
- [州](vocab-20.md#州-しゅう)（しゅう）— state/province (used for other countries like the US or China), a different administrative concept.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 私は神奈川県に住んでいる。— I live in Kanagawa Prefecture.
2. この県は観光地として有名だ。— This prefecture is famous as a tourist destination.
3. 県庁所在地はどこですか。— Where is the prefectural capital?

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Japan's 47 administrative units include 都 (Tokyo), 道 (Hokkaido), 府 (Osaka, Kyoto), and 県 (the remaining 43) — learners should know 県 is the most common but not universal designation, unlike "state" in some other countries' systems.

## ~券 (~けん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** ticket, coupon, voucher

**Similar words:**
- チケット — loanword "ticket," often used for event/show tickets, more casual and modern than the suffix 券.
- 切符（きっぷ）— a ticket, specifically for trains/transportation, a standalone noun rather than a suffix like 券.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 映画の前売り券を買った。— I bought an advance movie ticket.
2. 割引券を使って安く買い物をした。— I used a discount coupon to shop cheaply.
3. 商品券をプレゼントされた。— I was given a gift voucher as a present.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 券 is a productive suffix attached to many nouns (入場券, 商品券, 乗車券) rather than a standalone word in most cases — learners should learn it as a building block for compound vocabulary rather than using it alone.

## 現~ (げん~)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** current, present (prefix)

**Similar words:**
- 元~（もと~）— "former" (prefix), the contrasting prefix for past/ex- roles (元社長 "former president" vs 現社長 "current president") — easily confused due to similar pronunciation/kanji shape.
- 今~（いま~／こん~）— "this/current" in some compounds, overlapping somewhat but less systematic as a prefix than 現.

**Opposite words:**
- 元~（もと~）— former

**Example sentences:**
1. 現市長は再選を目指している。— The current mayor is aiming for reelection.
2. 現段階では詳細は未定だ。— At the present stage, the details are undecided.
3. 現住所を記入してください。— Please fill in your current address.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 現~ (current) and 元~ (former) are easily confused since both attach to titles/roles (現社長 vs 元社長) — misreading one for the other completely reverses the meaning, so pay close attention in formal documents.

## 権威 (けんい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** authority (status/influence), a leading expert

**Similar words:**
- 権力（けんりょく）— power (often political/coercive), distinct from 権威, which emphasizes respected authority/prestige rather than force.
- 専門家（せんもんか）— an expert/specialist, overlaps when 権威 is used to mean "an authority (person) in a field."

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼はその分野の権威として知られている。— He is known as an authority in that field.
2. 政府の権威が揺らいでいる。— The government's authority is wavering.
3. 権威ある賞を受賞した。— He received a prestigious award.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 権威 can refer both to abstract "authority/prestige" and to a specific respected expert person (この分野の権威 "an authority in this field") — don't confuse it with 権力, which implies actual power to control or coerce, often political.

## 見解 (けんかい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** opinion, view, outlook (often official/formal)

**Similar words:**
- 意見（いけん）— opinion in general, much more common and casual; 見解 is more formal, often used for official or expert positions.
- 見方（みかた）— a way of viewing/perspective, broader and less formal than 見解.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 専門家の見解を聞いてみたい。— I'd like to hear the expert's view.
2. 両国の見解には大きな隔たりがある。— There is a large gap between the two countries' positions.
3. 政府は公式見解を発表した。— The government announced its official view.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 見解 is typically used for formal, often official or professional opinions/positions (government, experts, organizations) — for casual personal opinions in everyday conversation, 意見 or 考え is more natural.

## 限界 (げんかい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a limit, a boundary, a margin

**Similar words:**
- 限度（げんど）— limit/degree, very close in meaning, often interchangeable, though 限度 leans slightly more toward a measurable degree/extent.
- [境界](vocab-15.md#境界-きょうかい)（きょうかい）— boundary/border, more spatial/physical than 限界's often abstract sense of capacity or endurance.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼の体力はもう限界に達している。— His physical strength has already reached its limit.
2. この機械の性能には限界がある。— This machine's performance has its limits.
3. 我慢の限界を超えてしまった。— I've gone beyond the limit of my patience.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 限界 often describes personal or systemic limits (physical, mental, performance) reached under strain — the common phrase 限界だ ("I'm at my limit") is a natural way to express being overwhelmed, useful in everyday emotional expression too.

## 見学(する) (けんがく)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** study by observation; to tour/observe (e.g., a facility) for learning purposes

**Similar words:**
- [観光](vocab-13.md#観光-かんこう)（かんこう）— sightseeing/tourism, for leisure rather than 見学's educational/observational purpose.
- 視察（しさつ）— an inspection tour, more formal/official, often used for officials or business inspecting operations.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 工場を見学する機会があった。— I had the opportunity to tour the factory (for observational learning).
2. 学校で博物館を見学した。— Our school visited the museum for observational study.
3. 授業を見学させてもらった。— I was allowed to observe the class.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 見学 specifically implies observing/touring for the purpose of learning (school trips, facility tours), distinct from 観光 (pure leisure sightseeing) — using 見学 for a vacation trip to see tourist spots would sound mismatched.

## 元気 (げんき)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** healthy, energetic, vigor, lively

**Similar words:**
- [健康](#健康-けんこう)（けんこう）— health (more clinical/medical sense); 元気 is more about vitality, energy, and liveliness in everyday speech.
- 活発（かっぱつ）— lively/active, overlapping with 元気 but more focused on activity level (e.g., a lively child) than overall well-being.

**Opposite words:**
- 病気（びょうき）— sick, ill

**Example sentences:**
1. お元気ですか。— How are you (are you doing well)?
2. 子供たちは元気よく遊んでいる。— The children are playing energetically.
3. 彼女はいつも元気いっぱいだ。— She is always full of energy.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 元気 is one of the most basic/common words beginners learn, but note it's a na-adjective, not a noun used with the copula の — 元気な人 ("an energetic person"), not 元気の人.

## 研究(する) (けんきゅう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** study, research, investigation; to research

**Similar words:**
- [調査](vocab-27.md#調査-ちょうさ)（ちょうさ）— investigation/survey, often more fact-finding/data-gathering oriented; 研究 implies deeper academic or scientific inquiry.
- 勉強（べんきょう）— studying in the sense of learning existing material, distinct from 研究, which implies generating new knowledge/findings.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は大学でAIの研究をしている。— He is researching AI at university.
2. この分野の研究は急速に進んでいる。— Research in this field is advancing rapidly.
3. 新しい薬の研究に多額の資金が投じられた。— A large amount of funding was invested in research for a new drug.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 研究 implies original investigation to produce new knowledge (academic/scientific research), unlike 勉強, which is about learning existing material — mixing these up is a common beginner error (e.g., saying 研究する for simply "studying for a test").

## 兼業 (けんぎょう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** additional/side business, dual occupation

**Similar words:**
- 副業（ふくぎょう）— side job/side business, a more common modern term, largely overlapping with 兼業.
- 専業（せんぎょう）— exclusive/sole occupation, the contrasting term for someone who does only one job.

**Opposite words:**
- 専業（せんぎょう）— exclusive occupation

**Example sentences:**
1. 彼は農業と兼業でカフェを経営している。— He runs a cafe as a side business alongside farming.
2. 兼業主婦として働いている女性が増えている。— The number of women working while also being homemakers is increasing.
3. 会社員と作家を兼業している。— He holds dual occupations as a company employee and a writer.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 兼業 implies holding two occupations simultaneously (not necessarily a casual side hustle) — in modern business contexts, 副業 (side job) is more commonly used for a secondary income source alongside a main job.

## 現金 (げんきん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** cash; (figuratively) a calculating/mercenary person

**Similar words:**
- お金（おかね）— money in general, much broader; 現金 specifically means physical cash as opposed to credit/cards/checks.
- キャッシュ — loanword "cash," often used interchangeably with 現金 in casual/modern speech.

**Opposite words:**
- クレジット — credit

**Example sentences:**
1. この店は現金払いのみです。— This store accepts cash payment only.
2. 財布に現金があまり入っていない。— I don't have much cash in my wallet.
3. 彼は現金な人で、得にならないことはしない。— He's a calculating person who won't do anything that doesn't benefit him.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** Beyond "cash," 現金 has a figurative na-adjective usage describing someone whose attitude changes depending on self-interest (現金な人) — learners who only know the "physical money" sense often miss this common idiomatic criticism of opportunistic behavior.

## 原型/原形 (げんけい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** model, prototype, archetype, original form

**Similar words:**
- モデル — loanword "model," overlapping with 原型 in the sense of a prototype or template.
- 雛形（ひながた）— a template/pattern, often used for documents or forms rather than physical/conceptual prototypes.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. この彫刻は粘土で原型を作ってから作られた。— This sculpture was made after creating a clay prototype.
2. 事故で車は原形をとどめないほど壊れた。— The car was damaged beyond recognition (lost its original form) in the accident.
3. このキャラクターの原型は彼の経験に基づいている。— The archetype for this character is based on his experience.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 原型 (prototype, model) and 原形 (original shape/form) are both read げんけい and often interchangeable, but 原形をとどめない ("not retaining its original form," i.e., destroyed beyond recognition) specifically uses 原形 — learners should be aware both kanji forms exist for this reading.

## 権限 (けんげん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** authority, commission, competence, power (to act within a scope)

**Similar words:**
- 権利（けんり）— a right (something one is entitled to), distinct from 権限, which specifically means the official authority/power to make decisions or act within a role.
- 権力（けんりょく）— power (broader, often political), whereas 権限 is more about the scope of authority granted within an organization or role.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 部長にはその件を決定する権限がある。— The department head has the authority to decide on that matter.
2. 彼は自分の権限を超えた行動を取った。— He took action beyond his authority.
3. この契約には特別な権限が必要だ。— This contract requires special authorization.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 権限 specifically refers to officially delegated authority/scope of power within a role or organization (e.g., 決裁権限 "decision-making authority") — don't confuse it with 権利 (a right one possesses as an individual, like human rights).

## 言語 (げんご)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** language, speech

**Similar words:**
- 言葉（ことば）— word/language in a more everyday, concrete sense (a specific word, or "language" casually); 言語 is more academic/formal, used in linguistics and formal discussion.
- 語学（ごがく）— language study/learning, the educational pursuit of acquiring a language.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 言語の違いはコミュニケーションの壁になる。— Differences in language become a barrier to communication.
2. 彼は複数の言語を話すことができる。— He can speak multiple languages.
3. この大学では言語学を専攻できる。— At this university, you can major in linguistics.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 言語 is the formal/academic term for "language" (linguistics, computer languages, formal writing), while 言葉 is the everyday word used in casual speech — using 言語 to ask "what language do you speak?" casually would sound overly formal; 何語を話しますか is more natural.

## 健康 (けんこう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** health

**Similar words:**
- [元気](#元気-げんき)（げんき）— energy/vitality, more casual and about liveliness; 健康 is the broader, more clinical term for overall physical well-being.
- 体調（たいちょう）— one's physical condition/state at a given time, narrower and more situational than 健康.

**Opposite words:**
- 病気（びょうき）— illness

**Example sentences:**
1. 健康のために毎日運動している。— I exercise every day for my health.
2. 彼は健康診断を定期的に受けている。— He regularly undergoes health checkups.
3. 健康な食生活を心がけている。— I try to maintain a healthy diet.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 健康 functions as both a noun ("health") and a na-adjective ("healthy") — 健康な体 ("a healthy body") requires な before the noun, a common grammar point learners need to practice.

#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** a manuscript, a draft, notes (for a speech/writing)

**Similar words:**
- 草稿（そうこう）— a rough draft, emphasizing the unfinished/preliminary nature more than 原稿, which can refer to a finished submission copy too.
- 下書き（したがき）— a rough draft/outline, more casual and commonly used for everyday writing.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 原稿 refers broadly to any written piece prepared for publication, printing, or delivery (an article, a novel manuscript, a speech script) — 原稿用紙 (manuscript/composition paper with grid squares) is a specific related item commonly used in Japanese schools.

## 検査(する) (けんさ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** inspection; to examine, test, audit

**Similar words:**
- [調査](vocab-27.md#調査-ちょうさ)（ちょうさ）— investigation/survey, broader and less technical; 検査 specifically implies a formal, often standardized check (medical, quality control, safety).
- テスト — loanword "test," often used more casually for assessments or trial checks.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 定期的に健康検査を受けている。— I regularly undergo health examinations.
2. 製品の品質を検査する工程がある。— There is a process to inspect product quality.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 検査 typically implies a formal, often standardized or official inspection/test (medical exams, quality control, security checks) — for a casual "let me check this," plain 見る or 確認する is more natural than 検査する.

## 健在 (けんざい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** alive and well, still going strong

**Similar words:**
- [元気](#元気-げんき)（げんき）— healthy/energetic, broader everyday term; 健在 specifically emphasizes that someone (often older or expected to have declined) is still alive and doing fine.
- 現役（げんえき）— still active (in a role/profession), overlapping when describing someone still working despite age.

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. 祖父は90歳を超えても健在だ。— My grandfather is still alive and well even past 90.
2. 彼の実力は依然として健在だ。— His skill is still as strong as ever.
3. そのブランドは今も健在で人気がある。— That brand is still thriving and popular today.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 健在 is often used not just for people's health but figuratively for things "still holding up" or "still relevant" (a brand, a skill, a tradition) — it implies a pleasant surprise that something expected to fade is still strong.

## 現在 (げんざい)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** the present, now

**Similar words:**
- 今（いま）— "now," casual and immediate; 現在 is more formal/written, often used in reports, statistics, and formal writing to mean "at present."
- [目下](vocab-19.md#目下-めした)（もっか）— "at present/currently," a formal written term, narrower in usage than 現在.

**Opposite words:**
- [過去](vocab-12.md#過去-かこ)（かこ）— the past

**Example sentences:**
1. 現在、この問題について検討中です。— At present, this issue is under consideration.
2. 彼は現在、海外で働いている。— He is currently working abroad.
3. 現在の人口はおよそ1億人だ。— The current population is approximately 100 million.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 現在 is the formal/written register for "now," commonly used in reports and statistics, whereas casual speech favors 今 — also note 現在 can mean "as of now" with an implicit contrast to past or future states, unlike the more immediate, momentary 今.

## 原産 (げんさん)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** native (to a place), place of origin

**Similar words:**
- 産地（さんち）— place of production, overlapping but more commonly used for agricultural/manufactured goods' production location.
- 原産地（げんさんち）— place of origin, the fuller compound form often used on labels (e.g., food origin).

**Opposite words:**
- None notable

**Example sentences:**
1. このコーヒー豆はブラジル原産です。— These coffee beans are native to Brazil.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 原産 is typically used for plants, animals, and agricultural products' natural origin — for manufactured goods' country of manufacture, 製造国 is more precise, though 原産国 is also common on labeling.

## 検事 (けんじ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** public prosecutor

**Similar words:**
- 弁護士（べんごし）— lawyer/attorney (defense side), the contrasting legal role often confused with 検事 by learners unfamiliar with the Japanese justice system.
- 裁判官（さいばんかん）— judge, another related but distinct legal role.

**Opposite words:**
- 弁護士（べんごし）— defense lawyer

**Example sentences:**
1. 検事は被告人を厳しく追及した。— The prosecutor pursued the defendant harshly.
2. 彼女は検事として多くの事件を担当してきた。— She has handled many cases as a prosecutor.
3. 検事は証拠をもとに起訴を決定した。— The prosecutor decided to indict based on the evidence.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 検事 (prosecutor) is easily confused with 刑事 (detective, けいじ) due to similar sound and shared kanji 刑/検 — they are completely different roles in the justice system, so context and careful reading matter.

## 原始 (げんし)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** primitive, primeval; primitive man/age

**Similar words:**
- 原初（げんしょ）— the very beginning/origin, more literary and abstract than 原始, which often specifically describes primitive human society or nature.
- 未開（みかい）— undeveloped/uncivilized, overlapping when describing primitive societies, though it carries more judgmental connotation.

**Opposite words:**
- 文明（ぶんめい）— civilization

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 原始的 (primitive, as an adjective) can carry a mildly negative/judgmental nuance when describing methods or societies as "backward" — be mindful of context since it can sound dismissive if used carelessly about modern cultures.

## 現実 (げんじつ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

**Meaning:** reality, actuality

**Similar words:**
- 事実（じじつ）— fact, a specific true piece of information; 現実 is broader, referring to the state of reality as a whole, often contrasted with ideals or dreams.
- リアル — loanword "real," casual modern usage often describing something as realistic/genuine, close to 現実的 in spirit.

**Opposite words:**
- [理想](vocab-33.md#理想-りそう)（りそう）— ideal
- [空想](vocab-15.md#空想-くうそう)（くうそう）— fantasy, imagination

1. 夢と現実のギャップに悩む人は多い。— Many people struggle with the gap between dreams and reality.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 現実的 (realistic, practical) is a common derived adjective — don't confuse 現実 (reality itself) with 現実的 (an approach/plan being realistic/pragmatic), which function differently grammatically.

## 元首 (げんしゅ)
#vocabulary
#to-review

- [大統領](vocab-25.md#大統領-だいとうりょう)（だいとうりょう）— president, often serves as both head of state and government in many countries, sometimes overlapping with 元首.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 元首 specifically denotes the symbolic/constitutional head of state, which may be distinct from the head of government (首相/prime minister) — learners should not assume 元首 always means "president" since the specific title varies by country's system.

## 厳重 (げんじゅう)
#vocabulary
#to-review

- 艶やか（つややか）— glossy, lustrous (adjective form), descriptive rather than the noun 光沢.

**Opposite words:**
- 無光沢（むこうたく）— matte, non-glossy

**Example sentences:**
1. この紙は光沢があって高級感がある。— This paper has a glossy finish and feels luxurious.
2. 髪に光沢を与えるトリートメントを使った。— I used a treatment that gives hair shine.
3. 光沢のある塗装で車を仕上げた。— The car was finished with a glossy paint coating.

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** 光沢 is a slightly more technical/written term for "gloss/shine" often used in product descriptions (紙, 塗装, 髪) — in casual speech, つや is the more natural, commonly spoken equivalent.
