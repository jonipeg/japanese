# ～ないでは／ずにはいられない {#ずにはいられない}
#japanese-grammar
#grammar

**Kanji form:** ～ないでは／ずにはいられない

**Furigana form:** ～ないでは／ずにはいられない (no special kanji reading)

**Meaning:** "Cannot help but do X / cannot resist doing X" — expresses that the speaker's emotions or natural impulses make it impossible to refrain from a certain action, often involuntary emotional/physical reactions.

**Conjugation:**
- Verb (ない form) + ではいられない
- Verb (ない form, dropping ない) + ずにはいられない
- する → せずにはいられない (irregular)
- その映画を見ると、泣かずにはいられない (Whenever I watch that movie, I can't help but cry)

**Typical context:** Both spoken and written Japanese; expresses strong, often involuntary emotional reactions — laughter, tears, sympathy, curiosity, criticism.

**Similar grammar structures:**
- [**ざるを得ない**](grammar-ざるを得ない.md) — "have no choice but to," focuses on external circumstances forcing an action rather than an internal emotional impulse.
- **てしまう** — expresses an unintentional/regrettable completed action, can overlap in describing involuntary reactions but lacks the explicit "cannot resist" framing.
- **たくてたまらない** — "want to do X so badly I can't stand it," expresses strong desire rather than an irresistible compulsion to act.

**Opposite grammar structures:** No direct opposite, though 我慢できる ("can hold back/resist") contrasts conceptually with the "cannot resist" meaning.

**Example sentences:**
1. その話を聞いて、笑わずにはいられなかった。
   (Hearing that story, I couldn't help but laugh.)
2. 彼の優しさに、感謝せずにはいられない。
   (I cannot help but feel grateful for his kindness.)
3. 悲しいニュースを見ると、涙を流さないではいられない。
   (When I see sad news, I can't help but shed tears.)
4. 不正を目の当たりにして、声を上げずにはいられなかった。
   (Witnessing the injustice firsthand, I couldn't help but speak up.)
5. おいしそうなケーキを見ると、食べずにはいられない。
   (When I see a delicious-looking cake, I can't resist eating it.)

**Common mistakes / key nuances:** する conjugates irregularly to せずにはいられない (not "しずにはいられない"). ないでは is slightly more literary/old-fashioned than the more common ずには variant in modern speech. This pattern stresses an uncontrollable internal reaction, distinct from ざるを得ない's externally-forced-by-circumstance nuance.
